# Adult attachment draft item development record

## Status

`adult-attachment` is an `educational/draft` reflection tool. It is not a validated psychometric scale and is not an ECR, ECR-R, or ECR-RS instrument.

## Rejected first draft

The first internal draft was rejected before release because its avoidance prompts and reverse-key structure were too close in meaning and shape to the ECR family. It must not be restored.

## Current draft method

The replacement prompts describe observable moments rather than rephrasing published scale claims:

- anxiety: replaying changed plans, attention during post-conflict silence, task disruption while awaiting clarity, mood response to tone, holding multiple explanations, tolerating unresolved uncertainty;
- avoidance: shifting emotional talk toward logistics, delaying hurt conversations, returning quickly to practical matters after care, keeping routines separate, staying present for impact feedback, allowing two-way emotional conversation.

The instrument keeps six prompts per concept and two reverse-keyed prompts only as an internal scoring balance. That structure is not evidence of validity.

For draft lineage tooling, English is the working canonical source language proposed by Codex on 2026-07-14. This is pending approval by the human product/research owner and does not itself establish translation equivalence, validity, or promotion readiness.

## Promotion gates

Before changing `educational/draft` or enabling broad indexing:

1. independent item-by-item similarity review against ECR-family wording;
2. qualified relationship-psychology content review;
3. cognitive interviews for comprehension and emotional safety;
4. human review of every locale;
5. pilot reliability and construct-validity analysis;
6. explicit decision on whether a published instrument license is needed.

No percentile, norm, diagnostic label, natural cutoff, compatibility claim, or four-type result may be added without a separate evidence review.

The operational review script, cognitive interview protocol, translation ledger, pilot data contract, and promotion record are defined in `docs/adult-attachment-review-packet-2026-07-14.md`.
