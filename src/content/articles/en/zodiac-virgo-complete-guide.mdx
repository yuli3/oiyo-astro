In the tropical zodiac, Virgo's usual stretch runs from about 23 August to 22 September. The boundary shifts a little from year to year. A birthday inside that window does not make someone Virgo in every year. What follows is the portrait sun-sign pages usually give. It is not a measurement of personality from a birth date.

## Virgo in brief

| Item | What the pages say |
|------|------|
| Usual range | About 23 August to 22 September. It shifts a little by year |
| Element | Earth |
| Modality | Mutable |
| Ruler | Traditional ruler: Mercury, also used for Gemini |
| Symbol | The maiden ♍ |
| Colors | Navy, grey, and pale green are a modern popular list |
| Numbers | 5, 14, 15, 23, and 32 are a modern popular list |
| Body | Digestion, intestines, and nerves. Traditional medical-astrology correspondences, not a health guide |
| House | Modern astrology links Virgo with the 6th house: daily work, routine, and care |

Mutable earth, in this vocabulary, means noticing what is in front of you and then revising it. That is why Virgo pages talk about analysis, editing, and use.

## Mercury, and a modern contrast

The traditional ruler is Mercury. The same planet is used for Gemini. The line that Gemini spreads wide while Virgo sorts one thing in depth is a modern gloss. It is not an ancient official split.

## Traits the portrait repeats

Sun-sign writing paints Virgo as analytical and practical. The lines below are that portrait. They are not a claim that everyone born near these dates lives this way.

### Often listed on the bright side

**Analysis.** Pages say this sign sees the whole and the detail. They describe noticing what does not fit. That is not a claim of being first every time, or of being right every time.

**Practical sense.** In the earth telling, a good idea still has to fit in the hand. The portrait is about moving a theory into work.

**Care.** Many pages say time and effort go to people close by. That is not a measurement of every Virgo person's nature.

**Keeping a promise.** Deadlines and duties are said to be held.

**A wish to learn.** The portrait includes picking up a skill and looking again at what is already known.

### Often listed on the shadow side

**Perfectionism.** Pages often say the standard stays high for oneself and for others, and that enough is hard to say.

**Criticism.** A sentence meant as help can land as a correction.

**Worry.** Things that have not happened are said to be turned over in small pieces. That is not health advice about the nerves.

**Self-criticism.** Some pages say the stricter voice is turned inward. That is not true of every person.

## Love

Virgo love, in this portrait, shows itself in acts more than in large speeches. Filling a cup, remembering a snack once mentioned, leaving a concrete help when someone is tired. The usual story is watchfulness first, and more openness after trust. That is not a rule for every relationship.

What the pages say this sign wants: frankness, reliability, and someone who notices the care in small things. What they call difficult: dropped promises, sharp emotional swings, and a setting with no order.

## Fields sun-sign pages list

These are fields popular sun-sign pages put next to Virgo. They are not a measured fit for a career.

- Medicine, nursing, and nutrition
- Editing and proofreading
- Accounting and tax
- Research and analysis
- Programming
- Libraries and the ordering of records

## What compatibility pages often say

There is no ranking and no score. Popular astrology often mentions Taurus, Capricorn, and Scorpio beside Virgo, and it calls Gemini and Pisces harder.

Pisces is the opposite sign in the traditional scheme. That is a description of polarity, not a verdict on two people. The list does not decide a relationship.
