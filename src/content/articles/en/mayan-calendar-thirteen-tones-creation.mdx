# A count of 13, and names added much later

The Mayans did not view time merely as passing numbers. To them, time was a living wave with specific 'qualities' and 'rhythms.' The sacred calendar called the tzolk'in runs 260 days, built as 13 × 20. Ancient Maya records do not call those thirteen numbers galactic tones.

The orchestra-like names, and the reading of each number as a stage in a life cycle, are modern symbolism from José and Lloydine Argüelles's 1990 Dreamspell. Knowing the list does not tell you which stage of life you are in.

---

## I. Thirteen names from Dreamspell

The names below are not an ancient Maya diagnosis. They are the 1990 Dreamspell list, set out here for reflection.

1.  **Magnetic Tone (Tone 1)**: Determining purpose and gathering into one. (Beginning)
2.  **Lunar Tone (Tone 2)**: Perceiving challenges and crossing polarity. (Separation)
3.  **Electric Tone (Tone 3)**: Activating action and making energy flow. (Combination)
4.  **Self-Existing Tone (Tone 4)**: Defining form and establishing order. (Stability)
5.  **Overtone Tone (Tone 5)**: Gathering power and issuing commands. (Center)
6.  **Rhythmic Tone (Tone 6)**: Balancing and adjusting the flow. (Flexibility)
7.  **Resonant Tone (Tone 7)**: Fine-tuning and receiving inspiration. (Mystery)
8.  **Galactic Tone (Tone 8)**: Achieving harmony and performing with integrity. (Perfection)
9.  **Solar Tone (Tone 9)**: Realizing intention and emitting light. (Peak)
10. **Planetary Tone (Tone 10)**: Completing and materializing the result. (Fruit)
11. **Spectral Tone (Tone 11)**: Letting go and becoming free. (Purification)
12. **Crystal Tone (Tone 12)**: Cooperating and dedicating. (Sharing)
13. **Cosmic Tone (Tone 13)**: Elevating existence and leaping into a new dimension. (Transcendence)

---

## II. Reading the list as a metaphor

These thirteen names cannot settle which stage you are in now.

- Spectral (11) is a name that brings letting go to mind. It does not mean you are in that stage.
- Self-Existing (4) is a name that brings giving shape to mind. It is not an instruction to solidify your foundations.

---

## III. Meaning of Following the Rhythm

The core of ancient Maya teaching was not a motto that says to align with time. That slogan comes from the modern Dreamspell telling. Feeling tired and strained is not something you can pin on a cosmos that ordered rest while the ego insisted on pushing ahead.

The thirteen names are not a method for seeing where a cosmic baton points.

## Wrapping Up: You are a Sacred Instrument of the Universe

Your life is not a sequence of random events. A sequence from 1 to 13 is the order Dreamspell tells, not a measure of where you are.

If things are not working out, that is not evidence that you are in tone 7 or tone 11. Some people use this list when they want a pause. You are already perfect music by your existence itself. That sentence is a metaphor, not a factual claim.
