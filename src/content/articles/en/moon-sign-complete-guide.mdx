## What the Moon sign is

The **Moon sign** is the sign the Moon was crossing at the moment of birth. Modern psychological astrology treats the Sun as a conscious style, the Moon as emotional habit and the need for safety, and the ascendant as a first impression. That is the tradition. It is not a measured fact.

| Piece | What the tradition reads | When the texts talk about it |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | Conscious style | When a goal is being stated |
| Moon | Emotional habit, need for safety | Close relationships, under strain |
| Ascendant | First impression | A first meeting |

The Moon moves about 13° a day. It usually changes sign in a little over two days. Birth time matters because the sign can change during a day. astro.com is one free chart site. It is not our product.

Some people say a Sun-sign portrait does not feel like them. In this tradition the Moon and the ascendant are other places in the chart.

## The twelve Moon signs

What follows is the portrait this tradition gives. It is not a measured trait.

### Moon in Aries

The tradition says feeling rises quickly and passes soon. Joy and anger are described as immediate, and as not held for long. Safety is placed in moving the body or saying the thing at once. Love, in the story, starts by speaking first and starting hot. Under strain, anger rises and cools fairly soon. Honesty and a quick recovery are named as strengths. Impulse and patience are named as the work.

### Moon in Taurus

Change is described as slow and deep. Anger, once up, stays. Love, once given, stays. Safety is placed in food, nature, and security you can hold. Love, in the story, decides slowly and then keeps the decision. Under strain, the texts mention eating more, buying, or stopping still. Stability and a long attachment are the strengths. Delaying change, and holding on, are the work.

### Moon in Gemini

Feeling is processed by thought and by talk. The portrait asks why the feeling is there, then turns it into a story. Safety is placed in conversation, reading, information, and movement. Love, in the story, wants someone whose talk connects. Under strain, analysis grows, words multiply, or attention scatters. Putting feeling into words is the strength. The tradition's complaint is that the feeling is not stayed with.

### Moon in Cancer

The tradition calls Cancer the Moon's domicile and the most lunar portrait. Feeling is described as rich, sensitive, and long in memory. Safety is placed in home, family, food, and familiar objects. Love, in the story, is care and a partner with whom a home is made. Under strain, the texts mention hiding inward or turning toward food. Empathy and care are the strengths. Mood moving up and down, staying in the past, and drawing a boundary are the work.

### Moon in Leo

Feeling is shown large, and both love and hurt are taken large. Safety is placed in expression, recognition, and the sense of being loved. Love, in the story, is dramatic romance and a wish to be someone particular. Under strain, the texts mention showing oneself more, or a wounded pride. Warm expression is the strength. Leaning on recognition, and being easily hurt, are the story's work.

### Moon in Virgo

The tradition talks about worry and fussing, and about care shown by doing rather than by a declaration. Safety is placed in routine, tidying, analysis, and helping. Love, in the story, is practical attention and a partner who can be relied on. Under strain, analysis and fault-finding grow, and worry grows. Fine care is the strength. Criticism, and worry aimed at perfection, are the work.

### Moon in Libra

The tradition wants harmony and avoids conflict. Safety is placed in a beautiful setting, a fair relationship, and a partner to be with. Love, in the story, is easiest when two people are together. Under strain, decisions wait, conflict is avoided, or someone else's mood is matched too closely. Evening out a relationship is the strength. Saying one's own view, and deciding, are the work.

### Moon in Scorpio

The tradition says feeling is deep and not easily seen on the surface. Safety is placed in deep trust and in inquiry. Love, in the story, is all given or nothing given. Control and destructive patterns under strain are this tradition's warning. They are not a diagnosis. Depth and renewal are named as strengths. Jealousy, holding on, and difficulty forgiving are named as the work.

### Moon in Sagittarius

Feeling is read as part of a large picture and a meaning. Sorrow, in the portrait, is turned into a story of growth. Safety is placed in movement, freedom, inquiry, and laughter. Love, in the story, is a free companionship. Under strain, the texts mention leaving, or skipping the feeling with optimism. Optimism and bounce are the strengths. Not facing the feeling is the work.

### Moon in Capricorn

Feeling is managed, handled in a practical way, and shown sparingly. Safety is placed in achievement and a sense of control. Love, in the story, wants a partner who can be trusted. Under strain, the texts mention more work, or feeling pressed down. Steadiness is the strength. Expression that hardens is the work.

### Moon in Aquarius

Feeling is handled by thought and by distance, and the general is preferred to the personal. Safety is placed in independence, a new idea, and a community. Love, in the story, treats the partner as the closest friend. Under strain, feeling is cut off or moved only into the head. Objectivity at a distance is the strength. Seeming cold is the story's complaint.

### Moon in Pisces

The boundary is described as thin, and nearby feeling is felt along with one's own. Safety is placed in art, a spiritual practice, a quiet place, and water. Love, in the story, is romance and an ideal. Under strain, the texts mention stepping back from what is real, or feeling that overflows. Empathy, art, and a spiritual tone are the strengths. Boundary, reality, and a pattern of self-sacrifice are the tradition's warning. They are not a diagnosis.

If something hurts, see a clinician. A sign portrait is not a medical opinion.

## A symbol of nurture

The Moon is a traditional symbol for a nurturing figure. It is not a description of anyone's actual parent.

## Safety, by element, as the tradition tells it

| Element | Moons | What the tradition calls safety |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Action, expression, movement |
| Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Routine, nature, the body |
| Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Talk, thought, relationship |
| Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Time alone, art, a spiritual practice |

This table is also a suggestion from the tradition. It is not a prescription.
