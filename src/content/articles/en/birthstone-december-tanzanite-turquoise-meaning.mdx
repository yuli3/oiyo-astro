December's birthstones are **tanzanite** and **turquoise**. Both read as blue, but the places they come from and the length of their histories are not the same. Wisdom, truth, and a new beginning are old inherited symbols. They are not a claim that the stone produces those effects.

## Tanzanite, a blue-violet zoisite

Tanzanite is the blue-violet variety of **zoisite**. The only commercial source is the **Merelani (Mererani) hills** in northern Tanzania, near Kilimanjaro. The mining belt is commonly described as about 7 km long and 2 km wide.

GIA gives a Mohs hardness of **6 to 7**. The stone has cleavage, so a ring is riskier than a pendant or earrings.

Trichroism is strong. Along different directions the colors are **blue, violet, and a brownish red**. Most commercial blue-violet stones are heated to quiet the brown. Published temperatures differ, roughly 370–600°C.

Discovery accounts differ. A Tanzanian government account names **Jumanne Ngoma** in January 1967. Trade history also tells a story of a **Maasai** and **Manuel d'Souza**. **Tiffany & Co.** named the stone tanzanite. The line that the English name blue zoisite sounded like blue suicide is an anecdote told about Henry Platt, not a verified quote. Tiffany's 1968 marketing, which presented it as the most beautiful blue stone in 2,000 years, is advertising, not a measurement.

Crystal-healing custom has tied this color to intuition, change, and compassion, including talk of the third eye and the crown. That talk is inherited symbolism. It does not mean the stone turns a chakra on or heals the body.

## Turquoise, a blue made by copper

Turquoise is a phosphate mineral. The formula is **CuAl₆(PO₄)₄(OH)₈·4H₂O**. Copper makes the blue; iron shifts it greener. Mohs hardness is **5 to 6**. Oils, chemicals, and long sun hurt it.

The name comes from Old French for Turkish, because the stones reached Europe through Turkish lands. Historic fine material is **Persian (Iran)**, not from Turkish mines.

Mining in Sinai is ancient. The **4th millennium BCE** is the usual archaeological claim. Calling Hathor Lady of Turquoise is a known epithet; read it as tradition.

The Blue Mosque of Tabriz is **Masjed-e Kabud**. It is famous for blue ceramic tile. It is not a dome paved with the gem.

In Mesoamerica, *chalchihuitl* is a green-stone word covering jade and turquoise, not turquoise alone.

In the Southwest, turquoise is important among **Navajo, Zuni, Hopi, and others**. The four sacred stones are a Navajo teaching as commonly retold, not a fact about every nation. The Sleeping Beauty mine in Arizona is reported closed in **2012**.

Protection, wisdom, healing, and friendship are inherited symbols. The stone is not a device that prevents accidents or heals the body.

## Side by side

| | Tanzanite | Turquoise |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Source | Only commercial source: the Merelani hills | Historic fine material: Persia (Iran) |
| Hardness | 6 to 7, with cleavage | 5 to 6; oils, chemicals, and long sun |
| Color | Blue, violet, and brownish-red trichroism | Copper makes the blue; iron shifts it greener |
| History | Differing accounts around 1967; Tiffany's name | Sinai mining: usually the 4th millennium BCE |
| Symbols | Intuition and change, as inherited talk | Protection, speech, and friendship, as inherited talk |

A person born in December, or anyone closing a year, is looking at two different records of time. Tanzanite is a gem name with a single commercial source. Turquoise has a very long record of use. Wisdom and protection hung on the blue are symbols carried with that record.
