## What is the day stem?

A Four Pillars chart (四柱八字) is four pillars: **year, month, day, and hour**. The heavenly stem on the day pillar — the day stem (日干), also called the Day Master — **stands for you**.

In a reading, the day stem is the center. Which of the ten stems it is colors a basic temperament, the feel of the energy, and the way a person meets life. Read what follows as traditional symbolism, not as a measured personality test. The lines of work named below are traditional symbolic aptitudes. They are not job statistics, and they do not say how common a stem is in any field.

| Stem | Phase | Yin–yang | Seasonal energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gab (甲) | Wood (木) | Yang (陽) | Spring, growth, forward drive |
| Eul (乙) | Wood (木) | Yin (陰) | Spring, flexibility, adapting |
| Byeong (丙) | Fire (火) | Yang (陽) | Summer, passion, expression |
| Jeong (丁) | Fire (火) | Yin (陰) | Summer, focus, a fine finish |
| Mu (戊) | Earth (土) | Yang (陽) | The turn of the seasons, steadiness, a wide hold |
| Gi (己) | Earth (土) | Yin (陰) | The turn of the seasons, care with detail, consideration |
| Gyeong (庚) | Metal (金) | Yang (陽) | Autumn, decision, principle |
| Sin (辛) | Metal (金) | Yin (陰) | Autumn, sensitivity, a wish to get it right |
| Im (壬) | Water (水) | Yang (陽) | Winter, strategy, freedom |
| Gye (癸) | Water (水) | Yin (陰) | Winter, intuition, depth |

In classical order, the odd stems are yang and the even stems are yin: Gab, Byeong, Mu, Gyeong, and Im are yang; Eul, Jeong, Gi, Sin, and Gye are yin.

Two cycles sit under the notes below. Generating: wood generates fire, fire generates earth, earth generates metal, metal generates water, and water generates wood (木生火, 火生土, 土生金, 金生水, 水生木). Controlling: metal controls wood, wood controls earth, earth controls water, water controls fire, and fire controls metal (金克木, 木克土, 土克水, 水克火, 火克金).

## Gab Wood (甲木) — a tree that grows straight

Gab Wood is a **great tree, a main beam**. The energy goes straight up, the way a large tree grows toward the sky and toward a goal.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: drive, leadership, a sense of fairness, a pioneer’s bent
- **Weak spots**: stubbornness, little flexibility, rigidity
- **Outward energy**: a style that steps in first, leads, and opens a path

### Temperament
A Gab Wood day stem **has a strong sense of self and dislikes compromise**. The temperament is built for leading, so standing under someone else is hard. Unfairness stings, and there is a pull to help the person with less power. Once a decision is made, the will to push it through is a strength. The same lack of bend can rub in relationships.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Founder, politician, athlete, soldier, sports coach, project manager, physician

### Relationships
For Gab, **Gyeong Metal is the strongest stimulus** (metal controls wood, 金克木). They collide, and they sharpen each other. **Im Water and Gye Water grow Gab** (water generates wood, 水生木). They are steady supporters.

<Callout type="tip">
**For Gab Wood**: Set down the thought that only your way is right, and you can lead on a larger scale. Flexibility is real strength.
</Callout>

## Eul Wood (乙木) — a vine that bends in the wind

Eul Wood is wood that grows the way **a flowering plant, a vine, or grass** does. The life in it bends in a hard wind and does not snap.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: adapting, sociability, fineness, the ability to feel with someone
- **Weak spots**: indecision, dependence, a changeable mind
- **Inward energy**: a style that reads the room and tunes itself to the situation

### Temperament
An Eul Wood day stem **reads people quickly and cares about the tie**. Rather than going straight, as Gab does, Eul looks at the situation and waits for a fitting moment. A fine sensitivity often shows up as an artistic gift, and partnership matters. There is a lean toward what is strong, so Eul shines more when there is someone to lean on.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Counselor, designer, writer, actor, diplomat, sales work, nurse, psychotherapist

### Relationships
**Gyeong Metal and Sin Metal shape Eul** (metal controls wood, 金克木). The pressure can feel heavy, and it can also be the occasion for growth. **Byeong Fire and Jeong Fire give Eul a place to shine** (wood generates fire, 木生火). It is a tie in which your energy can move outward.

## Byeong Fire (丙火) — the sun in full blaze

Byeong Fire is **the sun, a great fire**. The energy is bright and warm, and it lights what it reaches.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: passion, sociability, a sunny outlook, expression, charisma
- **Weak spots**: scattered attention, exaggeration, little patience
- **Outward energy**: a style that shows itself brightly and draws the eye

### Temperament
A Byeong Fire day stem **lights the people nearby, the way the sun does**. In almost any room this person sets the tone, and warmth with a stranger comes quickly. The positive energy runs high. Breadth is preferred to depth, so staying with one thing can be hard. Being seen feels good, and recognition is when this fire works best.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Performer, host, teacher, publicist or marketer, sales, politician, entrepreneur, event planner

### Relationships
**Im Water is what checks Byeong** (water controls fire, 水克火). The tie is tense, and the two balance each other. **Gab Wood and Eul Wood support Byeong** (wood generates fire, 木生火). They are companions who do not hold back their encouragement.

<Callout type="tip">
**For Byeong Fire**: Staying lit matters more than a bright flare. Grow your focus, and let the fire last.
</Callout>

---

## Jeong Fire (丁火) — a candle that does not flicker out

Jeong Fire is a **candle, a torch, the fire in a hearth**: small, and burning at a steady pace.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: focus, fineness, accuracy, an artistic sense, insight
- **Weak spots**: sensitivity, suspicion, timidity
- **Inward energy**: a style that concentrates deeply and finishes with care

### Temperament
A Jeong Fire day stem **is not showy like Byeong, and it is deep and precise**. A sensitive feel often becomes real skill in art or in a craft. A perfectionist bent takes the quality of the result seriously. Caution and a tendency to doubt mean a new relationship takes time. Once trust is there, the tie stays deep.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Researcher, physician, composer, precision technician, painter, cook, jewelry designer, surgeon

### Relationships
**Gab Wood is a steady support for Jeong** (wood generates fire, 木生火). It draws out what Jeong can do. **With Im Water the tie is tense** (water controls fire, 水克火), and Im can ease Jeong’s habit of gripping too hard.

## Mu Earth (戊土) — a wide land

Mu Earth is **a mountain, broad ground, a desert**: earth that is wide and steady.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: a wide hold, steadiness, trustworthiness, patience, the ability to bring things together
- **Weak spots**: stubbornness, refusing change, too much responsibility
- **A centering energy**: a style that receives, joins, and holds the middle

### Temperament
A Mu Earth day stem **has the wide embrace of mother earth, taking in what comes**. A solid presence becomes the center other people lean on. Steadiness is preferred to change, and what has once been trusted is not swapped lightly. The sense of duty is strong. Work that is taken on gets finished.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Real estate, farming, construction, administration, teacher, counselor, social worker, manager

### Relationships
**Wood stirs Mu** (wood controls earth, 木克土). It is a spur toward change. **Byeong Fire and Jeong Fire strengthen Mu** (fire generates earth, 火生土). They are steady allies who supply energy.

<Callout type="tip">
**For Mu Earth**: Do not try to hold everything yourself. Sharing the load, and working with others, raises a bigger mountain.
</Callout>

---

## Gi Earth (己土) — a fertile field

Gi Earth is **a field, a paddy, the soil of a garden**: earth that is fine and fertile.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: care with detail, consideration, analysis, ease with people, thoroughness
- **Weak spots**: indecision, too much worry, putting yourself down
- **Inward energy**: a style that tends and looks after things closely

### Temperament
A Gi Earth day stem **is finer and more sensitive than Mu**. The ability to look after people and a place, in detail, is excellent. Worry comes often, and the mind is easily unsettled, which is also why the preparation is thorough. Inside a team, this person often takes the role of settling the mood.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Nurse, childcare teacher, dietitian, landscape gardener, human-resources staff, customer service, counselor, cook

---

## Gyeong Metal (庚金) — steel that has been forged

Gyeong Metal is **a blade, an axe, iron ore**: metal that is hard and sharp.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: decisiveness, principle, drive, follow-through, plain speech
- **Weak spots**: stubbornness, coldness, refusing to bend
- **Outward energy**: a style that sets a principle and carries it out firmly

### Temperament
A Gyeong Metal day stem **is decisive, like an axe that cuts away what is not needed**. Principle and fairness matter, and pointing out what is wrong does not wait. Logic comes before feeling, and a decision moves quickly into action. In relationships, a clear boundary is preferred.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Lawyer or judge, soldier, police officer, financial analyst, surgeon, engineer, auditor, athlete

### Relationships
**Fire forges Gyeong** (fire controls metal, 火克金). Conflict appears, and it makes the metal stronger. **Earth supports Gyeong** (earth generates metal, 土生金). The tie offers steadiness and backing.

<Callout type="tip">
**For Gyeong Metal**: Fellow-feeling matters as much as fairness. One sharp sentence can end a relationship.
</Callout>

---

## Sin Metal (辛金) — a jewel that shines

Sin Metal is **a diamond, a metal ornament, a cut gem**: metal that is precise and bright.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: an eye for beauty, a perfectionist standard, sharp judgment, dignity
- **Weak spots**: sensitivity, pride, being easily wounded
- **Inward energy**: a style that polishes and refines until it is right

### Temperament
A Sin Metal day stem **is finer than Gyeong, and richer in feeling**. What is beautiful and what is refined are sought, and appearance and surroundings get a lot of care. A strong perfectionist bent judges the self by a high standard, and the same standard is easy to put on other people. When hurt, this person rarely shows it, and works through it alone.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Jewelry designer, fashion designer, cosmetic surgery, dentist, curator, photographer, the beauty field

---

## Im Water (壬水) — a great river and the sea

Im Water is **a river, the sea, a lake**: water that is large and free.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: strategy, free thinking, adapting, insight, the wide picture
- **Weak spots**: scattered attention, a loose hold on duty, little sense of direction
- **Outward energy**: a style that flows wide and catches an opening

### Temperament
An Im Water day stem **wants to flow without a dam, the way a river does**. Constraint and a fixed frame are disliked, and a free life is the wish. Strategy is strong, and a crisis often brings a creative way through. The wide view draws the large picture. Detail in the doing is where things get dropped.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Strategist, investor, adventurer, writer, shipping or aviation, travel work, diplomat, researcher

### Relationships
**Earth checks Im** (earth controls water, 土克水). It is a needed presence that reins in too much freedom. **Metal strengthens Im** (metal generates water, 金生水). It gives Im direction and wisdom.

<Callout type="tip">
**For Im Water**: Enjoy the flow, and still keep a destination if you want to reach the sea. Choose a direction, and add depth.
</Callout>

---

## Gye Water (癸水) — dew and rain

Gye Water is **dew, rain, mist, a spring**: water that is fine and deep.

### At the core
- **Strengths**: intuition, empathy, an artistic sense, a quiet magnetism
- **Weak spots**: unease, a heavy mood, secrecy
- **Inward energy**: a style that feels deeply and looks inward for an answer

### Temperament
A Gye Water day stem is **quieter and finer than Im Water**. Intuition is highly developed, so a situation is read by feel more than by words. Deep empathy makes other people’s feelings easy to sense, and art and philosophy draw a lot of interest. The inner life is rich, and it is not shown easily. Feelings sink quietly, so rest and a trusted conversation help.

### Symbolic aptitudes
Psychotherapist, philosopher, poet, musician, practitioner of a spiritual discipline, clinician, photographer, researcher of classical fate studies

| Day stem | Phase | Energy | Core words | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gab (甲) | Yang wood | Strong, straight | Leadership, opening a path | Stubbornness |
| Eul (乙) | Yin wood | Flexible, adapting | Sociability, empathy | Dependence |
| Byeong (丙) | Yang fire | Bright, showy | Passion, a sunny outlook | Scattered attention |
| Jeong (丁) | Yin fire | Focused, precise | Insight, a fine finish | Sensitivity |
| Mu (戊) | Yang earth | Steady, embracing | Trust, duty | Refusing change |
| Gi (己) | Yin earth | Detailed, considerate | Analysis, settling a mood | Too much worry |
| Gyeong (庚) | Yang metal | Firm, decisive | Principle, follow-through | Coldness |
| Sin (辛) | Yin metal | Refined, exacting | An eye for beauty, dignity | Pride |
| Im (壬) | Yang water | Free, strategic | The wide picture, insight | Little sense of direction |
| Gye (癸) | Yin water | Deep, intuitive | Empathy, an artistic sense | Unease |

<Callout type="note">
**Please note**: Do not judge a whole chart from the day stem alone. The month branch, the day branch, the structural pattern (격국), and the useful phase (용신) need to be read together before the picture is fair.
</Callout>
