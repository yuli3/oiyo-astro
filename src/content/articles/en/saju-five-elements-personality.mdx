## What Are the Five Elements (五行)?

The **Five Elements (五行, Wuxing)** are a framework from traditional East Asian thought that explains how all things change through five kinds of energy. Saju has used the Five Elements not only for natural phenomena but also as a symbolic language for describing **people's character, desires and patterns of behavior**.

| Element | Symbol | Direction | Season | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wood (木) | Tree, growth | East | Spring | Blue-green (靑) |
| Fire (火) | Fire, passion | South | Summer | Red (赤) |
| Earth (土) | Soil, stability | Center | Seasonal transitions | Yellow (黃) |
| Metal (金) | Metal, decisiveness | West | Autumn | White (白) |
| Water (水) | Water, wisdom | North | Winter | Black (黑) |

## Traditional Personality Symbols of Each Element

What follows are the personality images that Saju has traditionally associated with each element. They are not the results of measuring real people's personalities.

### 🌱 Wood (木) — The Person of Growth and Creativity

Wood energy is **the force that reaches upward, like a spring shoot**.

**Traits:**
- Future-oriented and drawn to growth
- Full of energy for starting new things
- Idealistic, with strong principles
- Direct, candid expression

**When Wood energy is strong:**
```
Strengths: drive, leadership, creativity, sense of justice
Weaknesses: stubbornness, rigidity, occasional impulsiveness
```

**Too much Wood:**
- Excessive stubbornness and rigidity
- Quick to anger when things go against one's principles
- Finds it hard to accept help from others

**Too little Wood:**
- Lack of drive and confidence
- Not great at starting things, but flexibility is a strength
- A tendency to follow other people's direction

---

### 🔥 Fire (火) — The Person of Passion and Expression

Fire energy is **the bright, hot power of expression, like a flame**.

**Traits:**
- Emotionally rich and highly expressive
- Sociable, with a magnetic charm
- Values the present moment and acts spontaneously
- A strong desire for recognition

**When Fire energy is strong:**
```
Strengths: passion, charisma, creativity, optimism
Weaknesses: impulsiveness, distractibility, lack of follow-through
```

**Too much Fire:**
- Easily excited, with big emotional swings
- Can wear others out with too much expression
- May tire out easily

**Too little Fire:**
- Finds it hard to show passion
- May come across as cold
- Inner warmth that does not get expressed

---

### 🌍 Earth (土) — The Person of Stability and Acceptance

Earth energy is **the power that receives everything and offers stability, like the ground itself**.

**Traits:**
- Values stability and trust
- Strong tolerance and patience
- Realistic, practical judgment
- Plays the role of mediator and connector

**When Earth energy is strong:**
```
Strengths: reliability, tolerance, realism, patience
Weaknesses: resistance to change, an overly heavy sense of responsibility
```

**Too much Earth:**
- Growth stalls from seeking too much stability
- Strong resistance to change
- An excessive sense of responsibility that wears oneself out

**Too little Earth:**
- A weak sense of practicality
- Flexible with change but can seem unsettled
- Difficulty building routines

---

### ⚔️ Metal (金) — The Person of Decisiveness and Principle

Metal energy is **the hard, sharp power of decision, like iron**.

**Traits:**
- Values principles and standards
- A clear, direct communication style
- Decisive about cutting away what is unnecessary
- Perfectionistic, with high standards

**When Metal energy is strong:**
```
Strengths: decisiveness, logic, precision, courage
Weaknesses: coldness, refusal to compromise, difficulty expressing feelings
```

**Too much Metal:**
- Excessive criticism and coldness
- Stubborn refusal to compromise
- Holding emotions down too much

**Too little Metal:**
- Lack of decisiveness and drive
- Gentle and flexible but unable to assert oneself firmly
- Indecisive at important moments

---

### 💧 Water (水) — The Person of Wisdom and Adaptation

Water energy is **the power of wisdom that flows freely and seeps into every space, like water**.

**Traits:**
- Resourceful and quick to read a situation
- Prefers freedom and change
- Deep intuition and insight
- Highly adaptable

**When Water energy is strong:**
```
Strengths: wisdom, adaptability, intuition, creative thinking
Weaknesses: lack of direction, impulsiveness, irresponsibility
```

**Too much Water:**
- Avoiding responsibility through too much pursuit of freedom
- Scattered, unable to settle on a direction
- So much change that people nearby feel uneasy

**Too little Water:**
- Relies on principle rather than resourcefulness
- Lack of flexibility
- Slow to adapt to new situations

## The Generating (相生) and Controlling (相剋) Cycles

### Generating cycle (相生): elements that support each other

```
Wood → Fire: Wood feeds Fire (wood makes fire)
Fire → Earth: Fire creates Earth (ash becomes soil)
Earth → Metal: Earth bears Metal (the ground holds metal ore)
Metal → Water: Metal produces Water (water condenses on metal)
Water → Wood: Water nourishes Wood (water makes trees grow)
```

**Generating in relationships**: In the traditional reading, a person rich in the element you lack is said to complement you.

### Controlling cycle (相剋): elements that restrain each other

```
Wood → Earth: Wood controls Earth (roots break into the soil)
Earth → Water: Earth controls Water (soil dams water)
Water → Fire: Water controls Fire (water puts out fire)
Fire → Metal: Fire controls Metal (fire melts metal)
Metal → Wood: Metal controls Wood (the axe cuts the tree)
```

## Finding Your Five-Element Balance

Count how many of each element appear among the eight characters of your Saju chart (four heavenly stems + four earthly branches).

- Heavenly stems: Gap (甲) and Eul (乙) are Wood, Byeong (丙) and Jeong (丁) are Fire, Mu (戊) and Gi (己) are Earth, Gyeong (庚) and Sin (辛) are Metal, Im (壬) and Gye (癸) are Water
- Earthly branches: In (寅) and Myo (卯) are Wood, Sa (巳) and O (午) are Fire, Jin (辰), Sul (戌), Chuk (丑) and Mi (未) are Earth, Sin (申) and Yu (酉) are Metal, Hae (亥) and Ja (子) are Water

For a simple count, each branch is counted by its main element, and the stems hidden inside the branches (jijanggan) are left aside.

**Balance as traditionally seen**: each of the five elements appears once or twice
**Excess**: one element appears three or more times
**Lack**: one element does not appear at all

### Traditional Readings of Element Imbalance

| Situation | Life pattern | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|
| Too much Wood | Rigid principles, frequent conflict | Build flexibility, Metal-type surroundings |
| Too much Fire | Emotional swings, tiring easily | Steady routines, Water-type surroundings |
| Too much Earth | Resistance to change, stubbornness | New experiences, Wood-type stimulation |
| Too much Metal | Coldness, relationship conflict | Practice empathy, Fire-type surroundings |
| Too much Water | No direction, instability | Set goals, Earth-type surroundings |

<Callout type="tip">
**Five-element balance tip**: The Saju tradition says a missing element can be supplemented with the colors, directions and surroundings associated with it. The goal is not perfect balance; the point is to use these symbols as a mirror for looking back on your own habits.
</Callout>
