February's birthstone is **amethyst**. The GIA birthstone list says so. It is purple quartz. An old story of calm and restraint stays with the stone. That story does not mean the stone removes anxiety or repairs sleep.

## What mineral it is

Amethyst is **quartz**. The chemical formula is SiO₂. Gemology explains that iron enters the lattice, and that purple centers form under natural radiation. The color runs from pale lavender to deep purple.

The Mohs hardness is **7**. The places that supply a lot of it in trade are Brazil, Uruguay, and Zambia. Madagascar and Sri Lanka are also named as sources. Brazilian amethyst geodes are often shown because of their size. Saying that Uruguayan stones tend to have a deeper color is a description from the gem market. It does not mean the energy is stronger.

## The name, and a story added later

The Greek **amethystos** is close to the meaning of not being drunk. In antiquity, too, there were words that linked this stone to wine and to restraint. The story that the pure maiden Amethyst was turned into crystal by Artemis, and that Dionysus poured wine and made it purple, is a legend that spread widely later. It is not treated here as one canonical text of classical myth.

That legend brought with it the belief that drinking from an amethyst cup keeps a person from getting drunk. That is why it was read as a symbol of a clear mind and of restraint. It is not a way to make someone stop drinking, and it is not a treatment for compulsion or addiction. If those problems are present, it is better to see a clinician.

## What is easier to check in the history

In ancient Egypt, amethyst was used as a gem. This article does not assert one particular bead from one particular temple.

A Catholic bishop's ring was often set with amethyst, by custom. It is not a mandatory rule for every era. Purple was read as the color of penance and of office. That is why it was called a cleric's stone.

Queen Victoria and amethyst jewelry are often mentioned together. A claim that it sits in one particular crown is not confirmed here.

## Words tied to the chakras

Crystal tradition links amethyst to the third eye between the brows and to the crown at the top of the head. Those words are a **metaphor for a state of mind**, such as intuition, imagination, and a wider view. They are not organs of the body. The stone does not open a chakra, and it does not cure illness.

## Words that remain as symbols

**Stillness.** Some people hold it on a busy day and use it as a tool for steadying the breath. It does not treat anxiety. If anxiety gets in the way of daily life, it is better to get help from a professional.

**Sleep.** There is a custom of placing it beside the bed. It is not a treatment for insomnia. If sleep stays poor for a long time, it is better to see a clinician.

**Clarity.** The old saying that it keeps a person from getting drunk is a belief about a wine cup. It is not extended here into an effect that restores judgment.

**Meditation.** It often appears among the stones people hold while meditating. It can be used as a physical symbol that helps focus. It is not a device that lets someone hear an inner voice.

**Protection.** The claim that it blocks a negative force or a psychic attack is a story from the modern crystal market. No barrier forms.

## Kinds named in the market

| Lavender | A pale purple |

| Spirit quartz | A trade name for a shape in which small crystals cover a cluster |
| Chevron | A pattern in which white bands and purple bands cross |
| Brazilian geode | A large crystal cluster. Often seen as decoration |

## Ways people place it

What follows is custom. It is not a method of use whose effect has been shown.

- People place it beside the bed or near the pillow. An affirmation written out as a sentence does not produce sleep.
- Some people hold it while meditating, or rest it between the brows. Picturing purple light wrapping the body is imagination.
- Some people place a geode or a cluster in the living room. The claim that it filters energy coming and going is a symbol.
- Some people put it in a pocket before a meeting or a talk. It is not a treatment that lowers tension.

Customs called cleansing include full-moon light, sage or palo santo smoke, a singing bowl, and burying it in soil for a day. Amethyst can fade if it stays in direct sun for a long time. Avoid charging it in sunlight.

Lists that place it with rose quartz, black tourmaline, clear quartz, and selenite are pairings that sellers make. They are not experimental results in which the stones add energy or cleanse one another.

## February and the zodiac

In the usual span of the tropical zodiac, February falls across Aquarius and Pisces. The boundary shifts a little each year. Writing the birthstone together with those two signs is an overlap of calendars. This article does not follow the claim that Sagittarius or Capricorn is a good match as well.
