Riso and Hudson call Enneagram type 8 the Challenger. Protector or Leader is only a popular extra label, not a second official name. Naranjo's passion word is lust. It names the push of appetite past a limit. It is not another official title.

The basic desire is self-determination and protecting one's own life. The basic fear is being controlled or harmed. The center is the gut, the instinctive center. Those three lines are the Riso-Hudson teaching.

## Riso-Hudson levels 1–9

Levels 1 through 9 are the Riso-Hudson frame. They are not a diagnosis. Strength in this model is not a promise that someone becomes a hero.

### Levels 1–3

This band is drawn as strength that is not hidden, and as a softer side shown to people who are trusted. The portrait includes respect for another person's autonomy, and a wish to stand by people close by. There is no guarantee of becoming a hero.

### Levels 4–6

Speech is described as direct, and sometimes as hard to hear. When the person is sure they are right, the portrait says they push. It also says weakness is covered by looking stronger, and that the reach of their influence is held tightly.

### Levels 7–9

The frame draws a threatening stance, and the use of other people for one's own gain. It says the outside can look strongest when the inside is most closed. That is not the situation of every type 8.

## Wings

### 8w7

With a 7 wing, the portrait opens outward and looks for new experience. It pairs force with movement. It is not a measured law of a subtype.

### 8w9

A 9 wing is drawn as quieter, with force kept low. A mediating stance shows up here too.

## Stress and growth

| State | Direction | What the frame says |
|------|------|------|
| Under stress | Moves toward 5 | Withdraws into thought, and action thins |
| Security or growth | Moves toward 2 | Turns warmer, and toward caring for others |

The arrows are the Riso-Hudson teaching. Under stress the arrow goes to 5. In security or growth it goes to 2.

## What relationship pages often say

Listed as strengths: a wish to protect a partner, speech that does not go around the point, and a strong heat. Listed as difficulties: expecting the other person to follow, closing the soft side so closeness is hard, and control that grows tight. This page does not assign one correct partner. It only records how often the portrait names someone who can speak while standing next to type 8's force.

## Notes placed on the growth side

1. This model writes showing a soft side as one form of courage. It is not a law that softness is strength.
2. The growth side says not everything has to be held. Letting a task go is a suggestion of the frame, not an order.
3. There is also a sentence about allowing a gentler manner. It does not guarantee that people will draw closer.
4. The tradition says an old hurt can sit behind a strong stance. That is not every type 8's story.

Strength in this model is not a promise that someone becomes a hero.
