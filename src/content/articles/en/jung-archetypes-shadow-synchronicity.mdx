## Why are Jung's ideas so widely used?

The Swiss psychiatrist Carl Gustav Jung (1875–1961) worked with Freud, then split from him to found **analytical psychology**. His concepts — archetypes, the shadow, the persona, synchronicity — spread into MBTI, tarot, storytelling and spiritual content. That also means they are often used in ways that drift from their original meaning. Here we separate **what Jung actually said** from **how it has been assessed since**.

## Archetypes and the collective unconscious

Jung held that beneath the **personal unconscious**, built from each person's experiences, lies a **collective unconscious** shared by humanity. It contains **archetypes** — the mother, the hero, the wise old man, the trickster — which is why similar figures and plots appear in myths and dreams around the world (Jung, 1959, Collected Works vol. 9, part 1).

For Jung, an archetype was **not a fixed image but a pattern that produces images**. At the centre is the **Self**, the whole that embraces conscious and unconscious, and he called the lifelong process of moving towards that wholeness **individuation**.

Assessments are mixed. The concept of archetypes has deeply influenced mythology, literary criticism and storytelling. But the claim that archetypes are inherited is **hard to test**, and critics argue that similar stories can be explained by cultural transmission and shared human experience (Neher, 1996). So it is more accurate to use archetypes as **an interpretive framework for reading stories and ourselves** than as a scientific fact.

## The shadow and projection

The **shadow** is the side of ourselves we don't want to accept and push out of awareness (Jung, 1951, *Aion*). Jung saw the shadow as often **projected onto other people**: if a particular trait in someone makes you disproportionately angry, it may be a part of yourself you haven't acknowledged.

"Shadow work" is a reflective practice of noticing such reactions and acknowledging the needs and feelings inside them. The myth of **Persephone descending to Hades' underworld and returning** is often used as a metaphor for this "journey down into the dark and back up again" (Hillman, 1979).

However, no set therapy called "shadow work" has been validated in clinical trials. If you are after a similar effect, pair it with **evidence-based approaches**, such as practising accepting uncomfortable feelings without judgement. If you have trauma or severe depression, work with a professional rather than alone. You can also start by looking at your own reaction patterns with OIYO's [shadow self test](/en/shadow-self-test/).

## Synchronicity: meaningful coincidence

In a book co-written with the physicist Pauli, Jung called coincidences that are **connected by meaning rather than by cause** **synchronicity** (Jung, 1952) — like hearing from someone at the very moment you were thinking of them.

Synchronicity is useful as a concept for describing **the experience of someone who finds meaning in coincidence**. But claims that it is "a sign from the universe" or that "quantum entanglement proves synchronicity" have no basis. Quantum entanglement is a statistical correlation between particles, and physics concludes that it **cannot be used to send information or meaning**. We also tend to remember the coincidences that hit and forget the misses, so coincidences feel more frequent than they are. How scientific terms are used in spiritual writing is covered further in [science words in spirituality](/en/science-words-in-spirituality/).

## Summary at a glance

| Concept | Jung's original meaning | Assessment and how to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Archetypes | Universal patterns that generate images | Inheritance claim hard to test; use as an interpretive frame |
| Self and individuation | The whole of conscious and unconscious; a lifelong process | Use as a direction for reflection |
| Shadow | The unaccepted side of the self | Useful for noticing projection; not a therapy |
| Synchronicity | Coincidences linked by meaning | Useful for interpreting experience; "signs from the universe" and "quantum proof" have no basis |
