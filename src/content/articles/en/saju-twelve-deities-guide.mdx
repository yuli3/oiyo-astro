## What are the twelve stars?

The twelve stars (十二神殺) are **twelve names attached by the three-harmony groups of the earthly branches (地支)**. The table is the one commonly used in Korean classical fate study. Each name is a **traditional symbol** for a texture of life.

This is not a measured personality, and it is not a job distribution. The work named below is work often written beside that energy. It does not mean excellence in that work. A star called bad does not mean a life is bad.

Read the day stem, the structural pattern, the useful phase, and the major luck cycle first. Attach the stars afterward, as a reference.

### How to find the seat

The base is the **day branch (日支)** or the **year branch (年支)**. Find the three-harmony row that branch belongs to. The 寅午戌 row starts at the Robbery Star on 亥 and ends at the Canopy Star on 戌. The other three rows are exactly as in the table below.

| Base branch | Robbery | Calamity | Heaven | Ground | Year | Month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 寅午戌 | 亥 | 子 | 丑 | 寅 | 卯 | 辰 |
| 申子辰 | 巳 | 午 | 未 | 申 | 酉 | 戌 |
| 巳酉丑 | 寅 | 卯 | 辰 | 巳 | 午 | 未 |
| 亥卯未 | 申 | 酉 | 戌 | 亥 | 子 | 丑 |

| Base branch | Reputation | General | Saddle | Traveling Horse | Six Harms | Canopy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 寅午戌 | 巳 | 午 | 未 | 申 | 酉 | 戌 |
| 申子辰 | 亥 | 子 | 丑 | 寅 | 卯 | 辰 |
| 巳酉丑 | 申 | 酉 | 戌 | 亥 | 子 | 丑 |
| 亥卯未 | 寅 | 卯 | 辰 | 巳 | 午 | 未 |

The **Year Star** seat in this table is the same as the Peach Blossom (桃花). In this table, the Year Star is also called the Peach Blossom. That does not add the Peach Blossom as a thirteenth star.

The characters for the Reputation Star are **亡神煞**, not 亡身殺. The characters for the Saddle Star are **攀鞍煞**.

<Callout type="note">
The Ground Star and the Traveling Horse sit in different places on the table. A reading widely spread in Korea writes both as movement. That overlap is an overlap in popular explanation. It does not mean two different scientific forces.
</Callout>

## Robbery Star (겁살, 劫殺): a symbol of fast drive

The **Robbery Star** is the picture in the characters for seizing. Traditional texts write a fast, impulsive drive beside this name.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of a fast decision
- A temperament often written beside competition
- A symbol of a hasty decision
- A picture of a force that pushes on

### Work often written beside it
Business, sport, and a seat where competition is frequent. That is not a statement of excellence in those jobs.

### Where to read with care
Traditional texts write a symbol of resources shaken by a hasty decision. That is not a forecast that loss will repeat.

---

## Calamity Star (재살, 災殺): a symbol of sudden change

The **Calamity Star** is a picture of a sudden turn. Traditional texts also write, beside it, a force that holds on inside that change.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of change outside what was expected
- A symbol of setting a judgment in an urgent place
- A picture of a force that holds on
- Cases where a move or a parting is written as a symbol

This is not a forecast of an accident or an illness. Even if an old text has such a sentence, read it here only as the symbol the traditional text is naming.

### Work often written beside it
Work that deals with crisis response: fire service, the military, and medicine that deals with emergencies. That does not mean ability in those fields has been measured.

---

## Heaven Star (천살, 天殺): a picture of trial and insight

The **Heaven Star** is a picture of a test sent from heaven. Traditional texts write an unexpected place, and the insight gained there, together.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of a large test repeating
- A reading that takes that test as a symbol of wisdom
- Often written beside religion, philosophy, and an inner search
- A picture of intuition and insight

### Work often written beside it
Philosophy, religion, psychological counseling, and spiritual guidance. That is not a measurement that the energy acts strongly in those roles.

---

## Ground Star (지살, 地殺): a name often written as movement

The **Ground Star** is a picture of moving across the land. A reading widely spread in Korea writes it as a texture of not staying in one place.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A reading that writes a move of home, travel, or a change of workplace as a symbol
- A picture of fitting oneself to several settings
- Cases where a tie beyond the country is written as a symbol
- A picture of strength rising in a new setting

This picture of movement is written over the Traveling Horse in the same way. The seats are not divided into two scientific forces.

### Work often written beside it
Work with a lot of movement, such as diplomacy, sales, travel, a posting away, or trade.

<Callout type="tip">
The advice not to fear movement is a popular reading. There is no guarantee that a new setting will open a talent.
</Callout>

---

## Year Star (년살, 年殺): a picture of popularity, and the seat is the Peach Blossom

The **Year Star** is often written as a picture of charm that draws people, and of popularity. The Year Star seat in this table is the same as the Peach Blossom (桃花), so the Year Star is also called the Peach Blossom.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A symbol of charm and popularity
- A picture of a place where people meet
- A reading that writes the feeling of romance as a symbol
- A picture of attraction coming often in a relationship

### Work often written beside it
The stage, acting, singing, work where a public is present, sales, and service.

### Where to read with care
Traditional texts sometimes write the bends of feeling, and of relations with the other sex, as a symbol. That is not a forecast that conflict will repeat.

---

## Month Star (월살, 月殺): a picture of solitude and depth

The **Month Star** is a name that draws time alone, and depth inside.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of solitude
- A texture of thinking for a long time
- A symbol that recognition takes time
- A picture that value remains if something is ripened for a long time

### Work often written beside it
Work that goes deep alone, such as writing, research, art, or a spiritual discipline.

---

## Reputation Star (망신살, 亡神煞): a symbol of showing

The characters for the **Reputation Star** are 亡神煞. They are not 亡身殺. Traditional texts write, together, a picture of one’s name being hurt by showing oneself, and a force of speaking without holding back.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of showing oneself frankly
- A symbol of private life, or of one’s name, becoming a topic
- A picture of bold expression
- An image of not avoiding controversy

This is not a forecast that one’s name will in fact be hurt.

### Work often written beside it
Work of showing, such as broadcasting, speaking, writing, or art.

---

## General Star (장성살, 將星殺): a picture of command

The **General Star** is the characters for the general’s star. Traditional texts write it as a picture of leading a group.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of stepping to the front
- A symbol of standing in the middle of a group
- An image of authority and command
- A picture of a strong stubbornness that stays away from compromise

### Work often written beside it
The military, the police, politics, a seat that leads an organization, and a supervisor. That is not a measurement that the energy is fully expressed in that seat.

---

## Saddle Star (반안살, 攀鞍煞): a picture of rising slowly

The characters for the **Saddle Star** are 攀鞍煞. It is a picture of climbing into the saddle. Traditional texts write it as a symbol of building skill and rising to a seat.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of going slowly, but without a break
- A reading that writes stability, and a building up of resources, as a symbol
- An image of patience and steadiness
- A picture of watching for social recognition

This does not mean stability is guaranteed.

### Work often written beside it
A professional practice built over a long time, a public seat, and research.

---

## Traveling Horse (역마살, 驛馬殺): another name often written as movement

The **Traveling Horse** is the characters for the horse of a post station. Both traditional texts and popular readings write it as a picture of moving without rest.

The Ground Star is also written as movement in the same popular reading. Two names do not make two scientifically different forces.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A reading that writes a move of home, a change of workplace, or activity outside the country as a symbol
- A picture of change and of a new setting
- An image of feeling tight if one stays long in one place
- A picture of noticing a new seat

### Work often written beside it
Work outside the country, talk of study abroad or of moving country, sales, trade, travel, and work that goes from place to place. It does not mean a condition for a global talent.

### Where to read with care
Traditional texts write a picture of roots that are hard to put down, and of going on to the next thing before the started work is finished. That is not a forecast that a life will go that way.

<Callout type="note">
Do not read the Traveling Horse as a modern qualification. It is only often written as a symbol of movement. Even if the sentences overlap with the Ground Star, the two are not divided into different forces.
</Callout>

---

## Six Harms (육해살, 六害殺): a symbol of hidden friction

The **Six Harms** are a picture of interference that is hard to see. Traditional texts write them as a symbol of effort and result failing to meet.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of recognition coming late even with effort
- A symbol of hidden friction and of betrayal
- A picture of inner skill growing in that process
- A symbol of something coming to be after a long ripening

This is not a forecast that a betrayal will happen. It is not a forecast of an accident or an illness either.

### Work often written beside it
Work of a long search. It is a symbol that draws a long completion rather than a short result. Achievement is not guaranteed.

---

## Canopy Star (화개살, 華蓋殺): a picture of art and solitude

The **Canopy Star** is a name that draws solitude inside a covered splendor. Traditional texts write art, a spiritual search, and time alone beside this name.

### The texture traditional texts draw
- A picture of art and creativity
- Often written beside religion, philosophy, and mystery
- A picture of solitude
- A symbol that the time of being recognized is late

### Work often written beside it
Art, music, writing, philosophy, and a spiritual discipline.

A picture of putting inner completion ahead of recognition from outside is a symbol in the traditional texts. It is not a diagnosis of a weakness.

<Callout type="tip">
The Canopy Star is a picture that the time of being recognized can be late. It is not a promise that recognition will certainly come.
</Callout>
