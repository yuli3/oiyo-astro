## How to Get the Most Out of Psychology Tests

```
✅ Effective psychology test practices:

1. Don't treat results as definitive truth
   → Psychological traits exist on a spectrum and shift with context

2. The process matters more than the score
   → Pay attention to your reactions while answering, not just the final result

3. Always follow up with deeper reading
   → The number or label means less than understanding the theory behind it

4. Don't reduce others to their type
   → "You're an INTJ so you must be cold" — that's misusing the tool

5. Believe in your capacity to change
   → Psychological traits are a starting point for understanding, not a fixed destiny
```
