## Introduction: The River of Time

In Saju, your birth chart (the Four Pillars) is like a **car**, but your **Daewoon** (10-Year Luck Cycle) is the **road** upon which that car drives. You could have a Ferrari-level natal chart, but if the road is a muddy, ice-covered mountain path, you cannot drive fast. Conversely, even a modest car can reach its destination comfortably if the road is a smooth, sunlit highway.

The term "Daewoon" is often misunderstood as "Great Luck" or "Winning the Lottery." In reality, the "Dae" (大) simply means "Big" or "Major" in terms of time scale. It represents the shifting **Seasons of Life**. Just as nature moves through Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter, your energetic life path moves through 10-year chapters that dictate the "climatological atmosphere" of your existence.

## The Seasonal Theory of Daewoon

Saju holds that a human life moves through something like seasons. Traditionally, the season of a Daewoon is read from its earthly branch (寅卯辰 is Spring, 巳午未 Summer, 申酉戌 Autumn, 亥子丑 Winter). The same season can be welcome for one chart and heavy for another, so please read the descriptions below as metaphors for the broad flow. Getting a sense of which season you are in can help you pace yourself instead of pushing too hard.

### 1. Spring Daewoon: The Time of Sowing

- **Characteristics**: New ideas, fresh starts, education, and planting seeds.
- **The Mood**: There is a lot of activity but little immediate "fruit" (wealth). It is a time for investment—not necessarily financial, but investment in yourself.
- **Strategy**: Learn as much as possible. Network. Don't be discouraged by lack of immediate profit; you are building the foundation.

### 2. Summer Daewoon: The Time of Growth

- **Characteristics**: Expansion, visibility, heat, and intense activity.
- **The Mood**: Your reputation begins to spread. You are busy, often overwhelmed, and "out in the world."
- **Strategy**: This is the time to work hard and push for growth. Manage your energy carefully, as the heat of summer can lead to burnout.

### 3. Autumn Daewoon: The Time of Harvest

- **Characteristics**: Results, wealth, consolidation, and reaping rewards.
- **The Mood**: The seeds you planted in Spring and nurtured in Summer finally become solid gold. This is the period of peak material success.
- **Strategy**: Focus on efficiency and collection. It's time to take the profits and secure your legacy.

### 4. Winter Daewoon: The Time of Storage

- **Characteristics**: Introspection, rest, preparation, and spiritual depth.
- **The Mood**: The external world may feel cold or stagnant. Projects that worked before may suddenly fail.
- **Strategy**: **Do not dig the ground in winter.** You will only break the shovel. This is a time for study, meditation, and inner work. The pain of Winter Daewoon only comes when you try to act like it's Summer. Accept the silence, and you will find profound wisdom.

## Gyoun-gi: The Fever of Transition

In Saju, the 1-2 years around the point where one Daewoon ends and the next begins are seen as a period of major change. This is called **Gyoun-gi** (交運期, the changing of luck period).

Think of it as the chaotic weather during the transition from Winter to Spring. You may experience:

- **Mental Weariness**: The tradition links this period with unexplained weariness or low motivation. Physical symptoms such as fatigue, frequent minor illnesses, or changes in sleep are not a matter of luck, though; if they persist, please see a doctor.
- **Environment Changes**: Moving house, changing jobs, or a sudden shift in your social circle.
- **Psychological Shift**: A "dark night of the soul" where you suddenly lose interest in things you loved for the last 10 years.

From the Saju point of view, these changes are not signs of failure. They are likened to a **"seasonal fever of the mind"** as you let go of old ways and adapt to the new season.

## How to Navigate Your Current Cycle

1.  **Check Your Element**: Is your current Daewoon bringing an element that balances your chart (Yongsin) or one that overwhelms it?
2.  **Harmonize with the Flow**: If you are in a "Career Luck" cycle, push professionally. If you are in a "Resource Luck" cycle, focus on studying and health.
3.  **The 10-Year Rule**: Remember that any hardship in a Daewoon is temporary. It is a 10-year lesson. Look for the "gift" the current cycle is trying to teach your soul.

## Wrapping Up: Dancing with Time

Fate is not a fixed prison; it is a rhythmic dance. By understanding your 10-year seasons, you stop fighting the "weather" of your life and start using it to your advantage. Whether you are currently in the deep silence of Winter or the radiant heat of Summer, remember that every season is essential for the full flowering of your destiny.

Wait for your time, act when the road is clear, and never forget that **Spring always returns.**
