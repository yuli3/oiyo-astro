## The fourth animal

The rabbit (兔) is the **fourth animal** of the twelve, and its earthly branch is Mao (卯). Across East Asia people tell of a jade rabbit on the moon, pounding something in a mortar. In that story the rabbit often stands for long life and quiet. That is symbol and folklore. It is not a measured personality of people born in a rabbit year.

## What the tradition assigns

The rows below are traditional assignments. They are not a personality score.

| Item | Traditional assignment |
|---|---|
| Earthly branch | Mao (卯) |
| Element and yin-yang | Yin wood |
| Direction | East |
| Time | The Mao double-hour, 05:00–07:00 |

Pink, red, and purple, the numbers 3, 4, and 6, and the daffodil and the lily show up often on modern popular lists. They are not values fixed by the old texts.

## When the zodiac year changes

A zodiac year does not start on January 1. Popular custom starts it at Lunar New Year, while saju (Four Pillars) starts it at Lichun, the Start of Spring, around February 4. The Gui-Mao rabbit year, for example, ran from January 22, 2023 to February 9, 2024 by Lunar New Year, and from Lichun on February 4, 2023 to just before Lichun on February 4, 2024 by the saju rule. If you were born in January or early February, you may belong to the previous year's animal (for a rabbit year, the tiger), depending on the rule used.

## The portrait the tradition tells

The tradition often draws a rabbit year as someone gentle and careful, who likes beautiful things and a calm place, and who looks for a middle way before a fight. On the other side come the complaints: putting off what needs saying to avoid conflict, hesitating long over decisions, and being hurt easily by criticism. Some also call the rabbit the sign born with luck. All of that is a traditional portrait. It is not a law for people born in those years.

## Love and relationships

The tradition speaks of rabbit-year love as tender and attentive. The story is someone who reads a partner's mood and wants a quiet, steady relationship. There is also a warning that, in a conflict, holding words back can let feelings pile up. That is neither a diagnosis nor a destiny. As a wished-for partner, the tradition names someone who respects sensitivity and is willing to bring up hard topics together. That, too, is the tradition's story.

Triad and six-harmony are not a higher score, and clash, harm, and punishment are not a lower one. They are the names this scheme has used.

## Work

The tradition often names work that uses an eye for beauty or a talent for smoothing things over: design, writing, or work that connects people. It does not claim an innate medical talent or a unique result. It is also not proof that those jobs fit best.

## Names kept only as birth years

Only the birth year is written. It is not used as evidence of personality. Albert Einstein was born in 1879, Michael Jordan in 1963, and Angelina Jolie in 1975.

## 2026

2026 is Bing-Wu (丙午). Mao (卯) and Wu (午) are not a triad. Some texts list them as a break (破) pair. This page does not forecast the year.

## Suggestions

1. Try saying one thing you have been putting off, in a single sentence. If it is not said, the other person has a hard time knowing it.
2. Try making one small decision within a set time. There is no guarantee the result will be good.
3. A suggestion, not an order: do not avoid every conflict. A calm conversation does not have to hurt a relationship.
