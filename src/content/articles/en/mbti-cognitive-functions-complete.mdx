This guide explains cognitive-function terms and type arrangements in the tradition of Jungian typology. Functions are not measured brain parts or ability scores. The model does not establish the causes of individual differences or stress behavior.

The descriptions below summarize the model. Dominant and auxiliary assignments are type-dynamics conventions; the questions are illustrations, not assessments of reasoning, empathy, memory, athletic skill, or prediction.

## The 8 cognitive functions

### Perceiving functions

**Extraverted Sensing (Se)**
- Focused on sensory experience in the immediate present
- Dominant in ESTP, ESFP / Auxiliary in ISTP, ISFP
- "What's happening right here, right now?"

**Introverted Sensing (Si)**
- Seeks stability by comparing the present against past experience
- Dominant in ISTJ, ISFJ / Auxiliary in ESTJ, ESFJ
- "How did we handle a situation like this before?"

**Extraverted Intuition (Ne)**
- Explores multiple possibilities and connections at once
- Dominant in ENTP, ENFP / Auxiliary in INTP, INFP
- "What could this possibly mean?"

**Introverted Intuition (Ni)**
- Converges toward a single, unified vision or insight
- Dominant in INTJ, INFJ / Auxiliary in ENTJ, ENFJ
- "Where is this ultimately heading?"

### Judging functions

**Extraverted Thinking (Te)**
- Organizes and optimizes the external world through logic
- Dominant in ENTJ, ESTJ / Auxiliary in INTJ, ISTJ
- "What's the most efficient way to do this?"

**Introverted Thinking (Ti)**
- Pursues truth through an internal, self-consistent logical framework
- Dominant in INTP, ISTP / Auxiliary in ENTP, ESTP
- "Is this internally consistent and logical?"

**Extraverted Feeling (Fe)**
- Attunes to group harmony and shared external values
- Dominant in ENFJ, ESFJ / Auxiliary in INFJ, ISFJ
- "Is this good for everyone involved?"

**Introverted Feeling (Fi)**
- Stays true to inner values and personal authenticity
- Dominant in INFP, ISFP / Auxiliary in ENFP, ESFP
- "Does this align with what I actually value?"

## A function stack is not an ability ranking

Dominant, auxiliary, tertiary, and inferior name positions within a model. They are not an observed time sequence of thinking or a ranking of skill. “Inferior” does not imply lower worth or intelligence, and it is not a diagnostic cause of overeating, impulsive spending, or compulsions.

### Comparing INTJ, INFP, and ENTP

| Position | INTJ | INFP | ENTP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dominant | Ni | Fi | Ne |
| Auxiliary | Te | Ne | Ti |
| Tertiary | F: Fi/Fe | S: Si/Se | F: Fe/Fi |
| Inferior | Se | Te | Si |

The tertiary orientation is disputed. The provider acknowledges this debate, so a fixed four-function sequence found online should not be treated as the only settled account. [Provider explanation of order and tertiary orientation](https://www.myersbriggs.org/unique-features-of-myers-briggs/type-dynamics-processes/)

An INTJ label does not explain perfectionism, and an INFP label does not establish that suppressed feelings erupt through Te. Nor should Si in an ENTP table be used to infer compulsions. Examine actual circumstances, experience, and habits separately.

## Shadow functions are an interpretive model

Shadow-function language appears in discussions of functions outside the main four, including discussions of John Beebe's eight-function, archetypal model. The existence of a model does not mean that hidden-function activation has been measured or that stress reactions have been validated.

<Callout type="warning">
Feeling that “I am INFP but acted like ESTJ” does not establish shadow-function activation. A behavior change is neither proof of a function nor a mental-health diagnosis. Do not delay needed support because of a type explanation.
</Callout>

## Shared functions are not a compatibility test

INTJ and INFJ sharing Ni is a classification within the model, not an observation of deep mutual understanding. Ni versus Ne should not be described as necessarily opposing visions, nor Ti versus Fe as an inevitable clash of logic and harmony.

| What can be compared | What needs separate observation |
| --- | --- |
| Whether the model assigns the same function | Whether communication actually works |
| How perceiving and judging terms differ | Whether needs and boundaries are respected |
| How a situation is described | How conflict and responsibility are handled |

Shared functions do not guarantee a good relationship; different functions do not predetermine conflict. Do not judge a friend, partner, or colleague's suitability by type alone.

## Separate development stories from practice

Type-development models describe development of dominant, auxiliary, tertiary, and inferior functions. They are not a validated age-by-age timetable for an individual. Individuation is also a Jungian theoretical concept, not a guarantee that function practice makes someone complete.
