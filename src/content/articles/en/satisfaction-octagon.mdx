## 1. A framework for reflection

This octagon is OIYO's editorial grouping of eight areas of life. It is not a validated psychological scale, diagnostic tool or formula for happiness. The same eight areas need not matter equally to everyone, and satisfaction does not require a high rating in all of them. You can change or add areas.

An evaluation of life and a feeling at this moment can be considered separately. That does not make happiness mere excitement or satisfaction the only genuine experience. Difficult health or financial circumstances do not exclude meaning and enjoyment; do not treat hardship as failed self-management.

## 2. Related theories are not this framework

| Reference concept | Focus | Relationship to the octagon |
|---|---|---|
| Self-determination theory | Autonomy, competence and relatedness | Does not certify these eight areas as its official need list |
| Ryff's psychological well-being | Self-acceptance, relationships, autonomy, environmental mastery, purpose and growth | Shared topics do not make the model or items identical |
| Satisfaction With Life Scale (SWLS) | Cognitive evaluation of life as a whole | Domain ratings or polygon area cannot be converted into an SWLS score |

See the [official SDT overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/theory/), [Diener's SWLS information](https://eddiener.com/satisfaction-with-life-scale-swls/) and [Ryff's 1989 paper](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.57.6.1069). This article does not reproduce their questionnaire items or scoring.

## 3. Explore eight areas with context

These are OIYO reflection prompts, not items with correct answers or a normal range. You can skip a prompt or replace an area.

| Area | Reflection question | Context to keep visible |
|---|---|---|
| Physical health | What support does discomfort or recovery require? | Pain, disability and access to care; health does not qualify someone for passion |
| Relationships | Where can you feel respected and ask for help? | Safety, quality and preferences, not a required number of friends |
| Autonomy | Which activities feel endorsed, and which feel pressured? | Choosing with support is not the opposite of autonomy |
| Environment | Does your space support safety and access? | Untidiness does not diagnose character or mental health |
| Growth and learning | What would you like to learn or maintain? | Rest and maintenance are legitimate; daily growth is not an obligation |
| Meaning and purpose | Which direction matters now? | Meaning does not oblige you to tolerate unsafe conditions |
| Leisure and rest | Is there time or an activity without added demands? | Rest need not be productive; consider cost and caring responsibilities |
| Financial stability | What resources or support are needed for essentials and changes? | Finances do not measure human worth or willpower |

## 4. Context matters more than the shape

If you draw an octagon, use it as a personal note with the same questions and period when comparing records. Numbers are optional. You could write “enough for now,” “support needed” or “undecided,” with reasons and importance recorded separately. Do not automatically turn a skipped area into zero.

Arbitrary ratings do not guarantee equal intervals or equal weights. Changing axis order can change polygon area, so do not rank happiness or people by area or a total. A low rating in one area does not make the whole life a failure or require compensation by another area.

## 5. Reflection and professional support have different roles

This is an editorial suggestion for describing your circumstances. It does not replace clinical assessment, treatment or financial advice, and has not been validated as a program that increases health or satisfaction. Consider appropriate professional support for persistent distress or difficulties in daily life.
