## How Four Pillars reads work aptitude

In Four Pillars (saju myeongri), the tradition looks at four main elements when it talks about work:

1. **Day stem (日干)**: your core energy (the most important)
2. **Ten Gods makeup**: how strong the Wealth, Officer, and Output stars are
3. **Structure (格局, gyeokguk)**: the broad direction of a life
4. **Useful god (用神, yongsin)**: the energy the chart needs

Of these, **the day stem is the foundation**. This article introduces the jobs and workplaces that the tradition has linked to each day stem's five-phase image. These are **traditional associations** inside a symbolic system, not predictions that you will succeed or fail in any line of work.

<Callout type="warning">
No scientific study has shown a link between saju and career aptitude. Please do not base decisions such as choosing a career or changing jobs on saju. Base them on your real experience, interests, and skills, on labor-market information, and, if useful, on help from a career counselor. Read this article only as material for self-reflection.
</Callout>

## Gab Wood (甲木) day stem

### Core energy
The energy of a great tree growing straight toward the sky. Leadership, a pioneering spirit, firm principles.

### Traditionally associated work
```
🏆 Traditional association: entrepreneur, politician, doctor, lawyer, educator
✅ Also mentioned: sports, military, project manager, director
⚠️ Seen as a hard fit: detailed repetitive work, roles that only follow orders
```

### Workplaces seen as a good fit
- **Room for independence**: deciding and moving forward on your own
- **Projects with clear goals**: the tradition says Gab Wood does best when there is a mountain to climb
- **Fields with social impact**: a sense of changing the world

### Advice from the traditional reading
The traditional reading holds that Gab Wood's stubbornness can easily cause friction in teams, and advises letting go of the idea that "my way is the best way."

## Eul Wood (乙木) day stem

### Core energy
The energy of a vine: adapting flexibly and sensing things with care.

### Traditionally associated work
```
🏆 Traditional association: counselor, diplomat, designer, marketer, writer
✅ Also mentioned: actor, nurse, psychotherapist, sales, HR
⚠️ Seen as a hard fit: fiercely competitive, lone-wolf environments
```

### Workplaces seen as a good fit
- Environments built on **cooperation and partnership**
- Fields where you can **use creativity**
- Work with **plenty of contact with people**

### Advice from the traditional reading
The traditional reading sees Eul Wood shining more in a cooperative setup with a strong partner or team than in a place where one person decides everything.

## The ten day stems and their work associations

| Day stem | Work association keywords | Workplace seen as a fit | Workplace seen as a hard fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gab Wood | Leadership, pioneering | Autonomy, clear goals | Only taking orders |
| Eul Wood | Cooperation, creativity | Partnership | Lone-wolf settings |
| Byeong Fire | Expression, charisma | In front of the public | Hidden repetition |
| Jeong Fire | Focus, perfection | Expertise recognized | Rushed results |
| Mu Earth | Stability, tolerance | Stable structure | Change with no direction |
| Gi Earth | Care, consideration | Thoroughness valued | Sloppiness |
| Gyeong Metal | Decision, principle | Judged on results | Too much compromise |
| Sin Metal | Aesthetics, perfection | Pursuit of quality | Forced low quality |
| Im Water | Strategy, freedom | Movement, change | Repetitive structure |
| Gye Water | Intuition, empathy | Feelings recognized | Suppressed emotion |

<Callout type="note">
**Important**: This table sums up the associations the tradition has passed down when looking at the day stem alone. Even within the tradition, structure, useful god, Ten Gods makeup, and the ten-year luck cycles are supposed to be read together, and in any case saju cannot be a basis for choosing a career. People with the same day stem differ widely in interests and abilities.
</Callout>
