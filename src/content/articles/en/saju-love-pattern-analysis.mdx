## The Saju Formula for Love

In Saju (the Korean Four Pillars of Destiny), romance and marriage are analyzed through **the placement of the Ten Gods (十神)**.

| Gender | Partner | Related Ten Gods |
|---|---|---|
| Man | Female partner | Direct Wealth (正財, Jeongjae) · Indirect Wealth (偏財, Pyeonjae) |
| Woman | Male partner | Direct Officer (正官, Jeonggwan) · Seven Killings / Indirect Officer (偏官, Pyeongwan) |

Practitioners read love patterns from how strong these Ten Gods are, where they sit, and which clashes (沖) or combinations (合) they form. This formula is a traditional framework built on the assumption of heterosexual relationships, and it does not predict or decide any real relationship. Please read what follows as a symbolic language for reflecting on yourself.

## Love in a Man's Chart

### Men with Strong Direct Wealth (正財)

**Traits**: Prefers steady, sincere relationships

```
How he shows love: Consistent care and practical support
Ideal partner: Realistic and dependable
Relationship style: Builds trust slowly; once decided, he does not waver
Weak spots: Slow to notice change, not very expressive
```

**Excess Direct Wealth**: Traditional readings interpret this as a tendency to cling to both money and relationships. Balance matters.

### Men with Strong Indirect Wealth (偏財)

**Traits**: Prefers freedom and a variety of relationship experiences

```
How he shows love: Bold, flamboyant gestures
Ideal partner: Attractive and lively
Relationship style: Starts hot but finds it hard to sustain
Weak spots: Finds it hard to stay in one place for long
```

**Excess Indirect Wealth**: Traditional readings see this as energy that spreads relationships widely. Actual behavior is a person's own choice, not something the chart decides.

## Love in a Woman's Chart

### Women with Strong Direct Officer (正官)

**Traits**: Prefers a stable, socially established partner

```
How she shows love: Practical and devoted
Ideal partner: Someone with a stable job who can be trusted
Relationship style: Wants an official, clearly defined relationship
Weak spots: High expectations and standards in relationships
```

### Women with Strong Seven Killings (偏官)

**Traits**: Drawn to strong, charismatic partners

```
How she shows love: Intense and direct
Ideal partner: Independent and resilient
Relationship style: Prefers an equal footing, or a partner who is strong, over a strict hierarchy
Weak spots: Relationships may carry a lot of conflict and tension
```

**Excess Seven Killings**: Traditional readings interpret this as a structure where tension in relationships comes up often. In any relationship, healthy boundaries matter.

## Women Without Officer Stars (官星)

When a woman's chart has no Officer star, or a very weak one:

**Traditional reading**: Marriage is seen as delayed or difficult
**Modern reading**: Read as a pattern of independent living that values one's own career and life

Modern interpreters sometimes read the absence of Officer stars as a metaphor for tendencies like these:
- An independent, self-directed life
- A life that gives great weight to career
- Putting personal growth before marriage

Neither reading decides whether or when someone marries.

## The Spouse Palace: Reading the Day Branch (日支)

In Saju, the day branch is called the 'spouse palace'. Traditionally, its symbolism is used to describe the partner's energy. The table below summarizes each branch's symbolism as a metaphor; it does not predict your actual partner's personality.

| Day branch | Partner energy | Relationship tone |
|---|---|---|
| Ja (子, Rat) · Water | Resourceful and adaptable | Intellectual |
| Chuk (丑, Ox) · Earth | Diligent and steady | Stable |
| In (寅, Tiger) · Wood | Leader-like and independent | Energetic |
| Myo (卯, Rabbit) · Wood | Artistic and sociable | Graceful |
| Jin (辰, Dragon) · Earth | Charismatic and distinctive | Unusual |
| Sa (巳, Snake) · Fire | Wise and strategic | Thoughtful |
| O (午, Horse) · Fire | Passionate and free | Heated |
| Mi (未, Goat) · Earth | Sensitive and artistic | Emotional |
| Sin (申, Monkey) · Metal | Clever and versatile | Stimulating |
| Yu (酉, Rooster) · Metal | Refined and perfectionist | Elegant |
| Sul (戌, Dog) · Earth | Loyal and independent | Trusting |
| Hae (亥, Pig) · Water | Generous and free | Relaxed |

## Improving Love Luck: Strategies by Chart

What follows are traditional associations based on the colors, directions, and places linked to each of the Five Elements. They are not proven methods, so treat them simply as a prompt to try new surroundings.

### When your useful god (用神) is Fire (火)
- Spend time in south-facing or sunny spaces
- Red and orange clothing, the colors of Fire
- Gatherings around activities that symbolize Fire (cooking, dance, sports)

### When your useful god is Water (水)
- Places near water, which symbolizes Water (rivers, the sea, pools)
- Black and navy outfits
- Intellectual settings (libraries, seminars, cafés)

### When your useful god is Wood (木)
- Parks, forests, nature
- Green and blue clothing
- Exercise, outdoor, or education-related groups

### When your useful god is Metal (金)
- Urban, polished settings (galleries, fine restaurants)
- White, silver, or gold outfits
- Professional meetups and industry networking

### When your useful god is Earth (土)
- Calm, comfortable settings (cafés, parks, neighborhood gatherings)
- Brown and beige tones
- Local communities, alumni gatherings, introductions through friends

## Timing of Marriage: Wealth and Officer Luck Cycles

Traditional Saju names the following periods as times when a marriage connection arrives. This is a traditional interpretation, not a validated prediction.

**Men**: Wealth luck cycles and years (Direct and Indirect Wealth activated)
**Women**: Officer luck cycles and years (Direct Officer and Seven Killings activated)

**Factors traditionally checked alongside:**
- Years whose earthly branch combines (合) with your day branch
- Years in which the spouse element (Wealth or Officer) is strong
- Periods in which the spouse element in the natal chart is not clashed or controlled

<Callout type="note">
**A note on Saju love readings**: Love and marriage are matters of personal choice and effort. Saju describes patterns of energy, but having no Officer star does not make marriage impossible. What matters is understanding your own patterns and choosing consciously. There is no scientific evidence that Saju can predict love or marriage.
</Callout>
