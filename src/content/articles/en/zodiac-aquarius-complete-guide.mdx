## Sign dates are not constellation boundaries

Aquarius (♒) is one of the twelve signs used in Western astrology. January 20 to February 18 is a common approximate birthday guide, not a precise boundary for every year and time zone. If you were born near a transition, the year, time zone and birth time matter. This article has not calculated or verified the transition time for a particular year.

The tropical zodiac divides the ecliptic into twelve sections of 30°. Astronomical constellations occupy regions of unequal size. [The ASP educational document hosted by NASA/JPL](https://nightsky.jpl.nasa.gov/documents/850/SolSysStarMaps2.pdf) explains that distinction and precession. A zodiac birthday table and the Sun’s astronomical constellation position are not the same classification.

## Fixed air is a traditional classification

[Astrodienst’s Aquarius entry](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/aquarius) classifies Aquarius as air and fixed, distinguishing traditional Saturn from modern Uranus as rulers. This is an astrology provider’s explanation of a convention, not independent research validating the personalities of people born under a sign.

The water-bearer’s name does not make this a water sign. Elements and modalities belong to the interpretive tradition; they are not material ingredients or psychological test scores.

## Distinguish rulers from houses

An account using Saturn as the traditional ruler and one using Uranus as the modern ruler do not use the same interpretive convention. State the convention before comparing readings. [NASA’s Uranus facts](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/facts/) describe Herschel’s discovery in 1781. The discovery date does not demonstrate that Aquarius people are innovative.

[Astrodienst’s explanation of houses](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Houses) treats houses as separate divisions of a birth chart. If a reading connects Aquarius with the eleventh house, do not treat them as identical coordinates. Actual house placements need to be read in relation to birth conditions and the calculation method.

## Check personality descriptions against experience

Independence, new ideas and concern for community are recurring themes in Aquarius interpretations. Reading those themes does not establish that a birthday caused those tendencies. Emotional distance or rebelliousness should not be treated as an inborn defect.

Rather than simply agreeing with “I am original,” look for examples. What did you propose, what feedback did you receive, and did you behave differently with close friends and strangers? Keep counterexamples too, so the description does not become something you fit only to favourable memories.

## Agreements matter more than compatibility tables

Readings that connect signs of the same element or opposite signs are symbolic interpretations. They do not establish a best partner or an inevitable conflict. A reading that places Leo opposite Aquarius is not a score predicting relationship success or failure.

Discuss time alone, contact frequency and ways of resolving disagreement with the other person. “Aquarius is detached” does not excuse disregard or breaking agreements. [The zodiac compatibility tool](/en/zodiac/compatibility/) and [the compatibility reading guide](/en/zodiac-compatibility-complete-guide/) can provide conversation topics, but they cannot replace consent or observed behaviour.

## Do not infer a career or MBTI type from a birthday

You may enjoy work involving new ideas, including research or software development. Career decisions still need evidence from tasks, learning experience, working conditions and feedback. An astrological symbol of innovation does not establish aptitude or suitability for recruitment.

Some articles place Aquarius alongside types such as INTP or ENTP. The sources used here do not establish a statistical correspondence between those classifications. Keep a personality self-report and an astrological sign as separate information. Adding an MBTI label does not validate job performance or relationship behaviour.

## Read research within its scope

[Carlson’s 1985 bibliographic record](https://www.nature.com/articles/318419a0) and searchable returns from the original describe a double-blind task matching birth charts with CPI personality profiles. The returned conclusion found no performance better than chance in that task. It did not directly test every romantic, occupational or future event prediction. Full-text extraction of [the original PDF](https://muller.lbl.gov/papers/Astrology-Carlson.pdf) failed during this check.

The first-page abstract of [Hartmann and colleagues’ author-hosted PDF](https://helmuthnyborg.dk/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Publ_2006_Date-of-birth.pdf) reports no association of birth date or sun signs with personality and general intelligence. Do not expand that into a test of MBTI mappings, careers or compatibility. The abstract was read; the full methods and results were not reviewed.
