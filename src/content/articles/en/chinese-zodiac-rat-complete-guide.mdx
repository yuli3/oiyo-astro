## First in a folktale

In a widely told race folktale, the rat is the **first animal**. In the race held by the Jade Emperor, the rat rides on the ox's back and jumps off just before the finish line to come in first. That is a folktale. The theme the story shows is wit and timing. It is not a measured personality of people born in a rat year.

## What the tradition assigns

The rows below are traditional assignments. They are not a personality score.

| Item | Traditional assignment |
|---|---|
| Earthly branch | Zi (子) |
| Element and yin-yang | Yang water by branch order. Some saju schools treat its use as yin |
| Direction | Due north |
| Time | The Zi double-hour, 23:00–01:00 |

Gold, blue, and green, the numbers 2 and 3, and the lily and the African violet are names that show up often on modern popular lists. They are not values fixed by the old texts.

## Year pillars

The heavenly stem is the year's element in the sexagenary cycle. The branch stays Zi. Only the stem changes from year to year. This page does not read a personality from that name. The same stem and branch are the notation saju uses for the year pillar. That notation is not evidence of character.

| Year | Year pillar | Element name of the stem |
|---|---|---|
| 1936 | 丙子 | fire |
| 1948 | 戊子 | earth |
| 1960 | 庚子 | metal |
| 1972 | 壬子 | water |
| 1984 | 甲子 | wood |
| 1996 | 丙子 | fire |
| 2008 | 戊子 | earth |
| 2020 | 庚子 | metal |
| 2032 | 壬子 | water |

<Callout type="info">
**When the year turns**: the everyday zodiac animal changes at **Lunar New Year**, which falls between January 21 and February 20. The saju year pillar changes at **Lichun** (Ipchun), the start of spring, around February 4. So if you were born between January and mid-February, you may belong to the previous year's animal, and if you were born between Lunar New Year and Lichun, the two rules can give different answers. For example, someone born in January 1984 belongs to the previous year, Gui-Hai (癸亥), a pig year, under both rules.
</Callout>

## The portrait the tradition tells

The tradition often draws a rat year as someone quick to read a room, who spots an opening early and settles fast into a new place. Stories of a ready tongue, sociability, and a good sense for money come along too. A complaint follows: so much calculating makes the inside hard to read. A warning follows: looking too far ahead brings a lot of worry, and in a tight corner the self comes first. All of that is a traditional portrait. It is not a law for people born in those years.

This page does not turn the folktale's wit into an innate intelligence or memory. Wit stays the story's theme.

## Love and relationships

The tradition speaks of rat-year love as warm but practical. The story is someone who watches a partner closely and shows care through small attentions rather than big words. There is also a story of hesitating to show everything inside. That is neither a diagnosis nor a destiny. As a wished-for partner, the tradition names someone who gives trust, respects independence, and can hold a thoughtful conversation. That, too, is the tradition's story.

## Names of traditional relations

This page does not use star ratings or the language of destiny. It keeps the names.

| Name | Pair | Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Triad (三合) | Monkey, rat, dragon | 申子辰 |
| Six-harmony (六合) | Rat and ox | 子丑 |
| Clash (冲) | Rat and horse | 子午 |
| Harm (害) | Rat and goat | 子未 |
| Punishment (刑) | Rat and rabbit | 子卯 |

Triad and six-harmony are not a higher score, and clash, harm, and punishment are not a lower one. They are the names this scheme has used.

## Work

The tradition often names work that gathers information and handles words, or buying and selling: writing, planning, trade, research. It does not claim that a rat year is bound to stand out there. It is also not proof that those jobs fit best.

## Names kept only as birth years

Only the birth year is written. It is not used as evidence of personality. Shaquille O'Neal was born in 1972, and Mark Zuckerberg and Scarlett Johansson in 1984.

## 2026

2026 is Bing-Wu (丙午). In this scheme Zi (子) and Wu (午) are a clash pair. This page does not forecast the year. A clash is the name of a relation, not a warning that something bad will happen that year.

## Suggestions

1. Try setting the calculating aside and simply doing one thing. A better outcome is not guaranteed.
2. Try writing one worry on paper and separating it from what you can do today.
3. A suggestion, not an order: say what is inside in one sentence. If it is not said, the other person has a hard time knowing it.
