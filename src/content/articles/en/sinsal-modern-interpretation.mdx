## What kind of explanation is sinsal?

Sinsal refers to symbolic names encountered in saju and divinatory interpretation. The [Encyclopedia of Korean Culture’s entry on saju](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0025957) discusses its cultural context and twelve sinsal. Describing a tradition is different from demonstrating accurate predictions about an individual’s future.

An ominous name does not establish an accident, illness or personality trait. This article introduces cultural language and suggests optional ways to turn it into questions about experience. It is not a sinsal calculator, psychological test, diagnosis or treatment.

## Distinguish historical claims and calculation rules

The [encyclopedia entry on sal](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0026407) discusses a concept in shamanism, geomancy and folk belief, describing its origin as unknown. That broader concept is not identical to all saju sinsal, and the entry does not establish individual sinsal origins. We have not secured evidence to attribute their invention to one person or era. Continued popular use is not proof of predictive accuracy either.

When you encounter a label, check which book or calculation rule was used and which birth information was entered. We do not introduce new stem-and-branch conditions for individual sinsal or merge results from different rules as though they were equivalent. The absence of a label does not guarantee freedom from accidents.

## Turn seven terms into optional questions

These are editorial reflection examples using terms from the previous article, not standard definitions of traditional meanings or claims about actual traits. You can leave out questions that do not fit.

| Term | Optional question | Conclusion to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dohwa — Peach Blossom | Am I comfortable with attention? What boundaries do I want in relationships? | Predetermined popularity, infidelity or romantic success |
| Yeokma — Travel Horse | When has movement or change helped me? | A requirement to move abroad to be healthy |
| Baekho — White Tiger | What facts should I check after hearing a frightening claim? | Predictions of accidents, surgery or a dangerous personality |
| Hwagae — Canopy | How do I balance time alone and with others? | A verdict of solitary destiny or artistic genius |
| Gwimun — Ghost Gate | How can I record situations that feel uncomfortable or overwhelming? | Diagnoses of mental illness, possession or special powers |
| Hyeonchim — Needle | What environment and skills help me with detailed work? | Belief that a tool or occupation removes misfortune |
| Hyeong — Punishment | What facts and sources of help matter in a conflict? | Predetermined litigation or punishment |

For example, enjoying travel can be observed in your life, but the Travel Horse label does not establish its cause. Relating to a question is also different from demonstrating a theory’s predictive ability.

## Psychological language does not make a psychological test

You might compare Peach Blossom with a lover, Travel Horse with an explorer, White Tiger with a warrior or Canopy with a sage. Such connections here are narrative metaphors, not evidence that Jungian archetypes and sinsal are identical. We do not claim to measure energy imprinted on the unconscious.

Do not use sinsal names to assess emotions, symptoms or intelligence, or to stigmatise someone. Health concerns can be considered separately from divination. [NIMH’s help-seeking guide](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/my-mental-health-do-i-need-help) considers symptom duration and effects on daily life. You can consult an appropriate professional when needed.

## A profession does not guarantee that misfortune disappears

The previous article used the expression *up-sang dae-che* to suggest replacing an ominous symbol with a profession. We have not verified evidence that using medical tools or entering a legal profession cancels accidents, illness or lawsuits. We do not offer that claim as career guidance.

Compare actual tasks, required education and qualifications, interests and practical circumstances when exploring work. You may enjoy helping people or precise tasks, but you are not obliged to take an occupation to neutralise a symbol. Nor should harmful experiences be blamed on a person’s failure to manage one.

## An example grounded in experience

This is an optional editorial note-taking example, not a procedure for prediction or preventing misfortune.

| What to record | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Claim heard | I was told I have a strong Travel Horse. |
| Actual observation | I sometimes enjoy business travel, but consecutive trips tire me. |
| Another explanation | Sleep, workload and familiarity with a place may also matter. |
| A small choice | I could set aside rest time before my next schedule. |

Keep both enjoyable and difficult experiences. Rather than collecting only confirming cases and concluding that a symbol is always right, leave alternative explanations open.

## Questions and the scope of the sources

**Does an ominous name mean someone is dangerous?** No. This article does not judge morality, violence or health from sinsal labels.

**Does having no sinsal mean having no talent?** We provide no evidence for that conclusion. Consider abilities and achievements through experience and action.

**Does a positive reinterpretation make it an accurate psychological test?** No. Changing a frightening phrase into a talent does not establish reliability or validity.

The encyclopedia sources support cultural context; NIMH supports the boundary around mental health help. They are not cited as proof of individual sinsal predictions, occupational effects or diagnostic ability. Unverified sinsal bibliographies and a direct identification with Jung’s theory from the previous article are not retained as evidence.
