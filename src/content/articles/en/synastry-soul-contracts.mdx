## Comparing two charts is not knowing two people’s destiny

Synastry is a relationship-astrology practice that compares planetary positions, angles and house overlays in two birth charts. [Astrodienst’s definition](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Synastry) describes that procedure. It is an astrology provider’s account of a convention, not independent validation of relationship prediction.

Here, a soul contract is a belief expressed in some spiritual interpretations. A chart does not establish that a meeting was predetermined or that someone owes another person a karmic debt.

## Lunar nodes are astronomical intersection points

[NASA’s explanation of the Moon’s orbit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5326) identifies nodes where the lunar orbit crosses the ecliptic. The ascending node is a northward crossing and the descending node a southward crossing. They are not planets or destinations a soul must reach.

Reading these points as past lives or a direction of personal growth adds symbolic interpretation. Calculating their positions does not establish a previous-life meeting or someone’s hidden intentions.

## An aspect is an angle; an orb is an allowed deviation

[Aspects](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Aspects) and [orbs](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Orb) are astrological terms. An orb allows deviation from a reference angle, with the permitted range depending on the interpretive policy.

**Editorial geometry example:** with both longitudes normalized from 0° inclusive to 360° exclusive, the smaller circular angle is min(|a−b|, 360−|a−b|). If a=350° and b=10°, it is 20°. This is an ecliptic-longitude difference, not a three-dimensional angular separation in the sky, a compatibility score or a cause of psychological resonance. A tuning-fork or electrical-circuit analogy does not demonstrate such a mechanism.

## Keep uncertainties about inputs and interpretation visible

[Astrodienst’s birth-time FAQ](https://www.astro.com/faq/fq_de_time_e.htm) describes using a hypothetical noon and excluding houses when time is unknown. That is the provider’s procedure, not a rule for every tool. Houses calculated from an invented time should not be presented as results from a known birth time.

## Soul contracts and Saturn symbolism do not create obligations

Past lives, soul contracts and twin flames can be read as beliefs or interpretive language. We do not use them to claim that a chart discovers a real contract or decides whom someone must stay with. Nor do we assert that Saturn contacts determine whether a relationship lasts or ends quickly.

Fear or pain is not evidence of a profound destined bond. A chart cannot turn another person’s coldness into proof that they are not rejecting you, and it does not create an obligation to continue the relationship.

## Read research within the scope actually tested

[The bibliographic record of Carlson’s 1985 paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/318419a0) and searchable excerpts of the original concern matching birth charts with CPI personality data. The returned conclusion reports performance that did not exceed chance in that test. It did not directly test synastry’s prediction of romantic success or marriage duration.

Full-text extraction failed during this check. We do not claim a complete reading or use this study alone to judge every astrological claim or life event. The original article’s unsupported Saturn statistics about long marriages are not presented as verified research.

## In a relationship, check words, consent and safety

If you enjoy charts, you may use one as a conversation topic. Asking how each person handles conflict is an **optional editorial conversation example**, not a way to measure another person’s psychology through a chart. Do not impose an interpretation on someone who does not want it.

[NHS guidance on domestic abuse](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/getting-help-for-domestic-violence/) describes control, isolation, threats, stalking and monitoring without consent. These behaviours should not be justified as cosmic growth tasks or the glue of a bond. This is UK guidance; local support arrangements may differ.

When using [the two-person view](/en/circle/pair/), please keep symbolic interpretations separate from facts about the relationship. Precise calculation is not a substitute for predictive validity.
