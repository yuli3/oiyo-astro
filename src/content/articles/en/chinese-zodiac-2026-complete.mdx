## 2026 is a Bing-Wu (丙午) year

The stem-branch name of 2026 is **Bing-Wu (丙午)**.

- **Heavenly stem Bing (丙)**: yang fire in the five-phase system. The tradition compares it to the sun.
- **Earthly branch Wu (午)**: the horse, also assigned to yang fire.

Because both the stem and the branch are fire, the saju tradition calls this a year of "doubled fire". That is a symbolic label. It does not mean the year decides events or anyone's character.

<Callout type="info">
**When does 2026 start?** The everyday zodiac animal usually changes at Lunar New Year, which falls on **February 17, 2026**. In saju (Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring, which falls on **February 4, 2026**. So someone born between January 1 and early February 2026 may still count as a snake (乙巳), depending on which rule is used.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**This is not a fortune forecast.** The zodiac animals and the stem-branch cycle are an old cultural tradition. There is no scientific evidence that a birth year decides personality, health, money or love. The relation names and readings below are interpretations the tradition has attached, and we suggest not using them as grounds for real decisions.
</Callout>

## Wu and the 12 animals: the traditional relation names

The saju tradition gives names to relations between earthly branches. Setting the 2026 branch Wu next to each animal's branch gives the table below. The five-phase column compares Wu's fire with the phase of each animal's branch.

| Animal | Branch | Relation name with Wu | Five-phase relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rat | 子 | Clash (子午沖) | Water controls fire |
| Ox | 丑 | Harm (丑午害); *wonjin* in Korean tradition | Fire produces earth |
| Tiger | 寅 | Triad (寅午戌) | Wood produces fire |
| Rabbit | 卯 | Break (卯午破) | Wood produces fire |
| Dragon | 辰 | No named relation | Fire produces earth |
| Snake | 巳 | Directional group (巳午未, south) | Both fire |
| Horse | 午 | Own-sign year; self-punishment (午午自刑) | Both fire |
| Goat | 未 | Six harmony (午未合) | Fire produces earth |
| Monkey | 申 | No named relation | Fire controls metal |
| Rooster | 酉 | No named relation | Fire controls metal |
| Dog | 戌 | Triad (寅午戌) | Fire produces earth |
| Pig | 亥 | No named relation | Water controls fire |

This is not a scorecard where triads and harmonies are good and clashes and harms are bad. Even in traditional readings, a clash is read as movement and a combination as binding, each with an easy side and a troublesome side. This page does not rank the animals or give star ratings.

## Rat (子: 1924, 1936, 1948, 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020)

Zi and Wu sit opposite each other and form the **Zi-Wu clash (子午沖)**. Traditional readings take a clash as a sign of change and movement. That is not a prediction that you will actually move house or change jobs.

**A question to consider**: If you wrote down one thing you want to change this year and one thing you want to keep, what would they be?

---

## Ox (丑: 1925, 1937, 1949, 1961, 1973, 1985, 1997, 2009, 2021)

Chou and Wu are paired as **harm (害)**, and Korean tradition also calls this pair *wonjin* (怨嗔). In five-phase terms fire produces earth, so traditional readings talk about tension and support at the same time.

**A question to consider**: Among the things you have kept doing steadily, which one would you like to review this year?

---

## Tiger (寅: 1926, 1938, 1950, 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022)

The tradition explains that Yin, Wu and Xu form a **triad (三合)** that makes a fire frame. Traditional readings therefore often describe the tiger and a Wu year as a combination where the energies flow easily. That is an interpretation, not a guaranteed outcome.

**A question to consider**: If there is something you have wanted to start, how small could you make the first step?

---

## Rabbit (卯: 1927, 1939, 1951, 1963, 1975, 1987, 1999, 2011, 2023)

Mao and Wu are classed as a **break (破)** in the tradition. In five-phase terms wood produces fire. Under the Korean folk *samjae* count, the rabbit is in the middle of its three-year samjae period in 2026. See the samjae section below.

**A question to consider**: How does the energy you spend helping others compare with what you keep for yourself right now?

---

## Dragon (辰: 1928, 1940, 1952, 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024)

There is no named relation between Chen and Wu. In five-phase terms fire produces earth, and traditional readings take this as a supportive flow.

**A question to consider**: What work or role would you like to show other people this year?

---

## Snake (巳: 1929, 1941, 1953, 1965, 1977, 1989, 2001, 2013, 2025)

Si, Wu and Wei are all placed in the south and form a **directional group (方合)**. Si and Wu on their own do not make a half triad. Both belong to fire, so traditional readings say similar energies overlap.

**A question to consider**: Among the things you have only watched for a long time, which one would you like to decide on this year?

---

## Horse (午: 1930, 1942, 1954, 1966, 1978, 1990, 2002, 2014, 2026)

For people born in a horse year, 2026 is their **own-sign year (本命年, benmingnian)**. There is also a traditional classification in which Wu meeting Wu is a **self-punishment (自刑)**. Chinese custom passes down the habit of wearing a red string or red clothing in your own-sign year, but there is no evidence that it wards off misfortune. More about the horse itself is in the [horse year guide](/en/chinese-zodiac-horse-complete-guide/).

**A question to consider**: Compared with twelve years ago, what has changed most about you?

---

## Goat (未: 1931, 1943, 1955, 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015)

Wu and Wei are paired in the **six harmony (六合)**. At the same time, under the Korean folk samjae count, the goat is in the middle of its samjae period in 2026. A harmony and a samjae landing on the same year shows that these names are not scores but markers from different traditions.

**A question to consider**: Who would you like to work or spend time with this year, and what would you say to them first?

---

## Monkey (申: 1932, 1944, 1956, 1968, 1980, 1992, 2004, 2016)

There is no named relation between Shen and Wu. In five-phase terms fire controls metal, and traditional readings sometimes take this as a pressure that refines.

**A question to consider**: Between changing things quickly and finishing them properly, which would you rather practise this year?

---

## Rooster (酉: 1933, 1945, 1957, 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017)

There is no named relation between You and Wu either. In five-phase terms it is the same fire-controls-metal relation as the monkey.

**A question to consider**: Among the things you have put off because you wanted them perfect, which could you release at the level they are at now?

---

## Dog (戌: 1934, 1946, 1958, 1970, 1982, 1994, 2006, 2018)

Xu is one of the three seats of the **triad (寅午戌)** together with Yin and Wu. In five-phase terms fire produces earth. Traditional readings take this as a flow that binds things together.

**A question to consider**: Among the relationships you rely on, whom would you like to look after first this year?

---

## Pig (亥: 1935, 1947, 1959, 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019)

There is no named relation between Hai and Wu, and in five-phase terms water controls fire. Under the Korean folk samjae count, the pig is in the middle of its samjae period in 2026.

**A question to consider**: If you set one big picture for this year, what would you like to do less of to make room for it?

## The fire horse superstition and Japan in 1966

In Japan, a superstition dating back to the Edo period held that women born in a fire horse year (*hinoeuma*, 丙午) are headstrong. The superstition is not true, but it did change what people chose to do. According to the Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's vital statistics, about 1.36 million babies were born in 1966, roughly a quarter fewer than in 1965 (about 1.82 million), and births rose again in 1967. Researchers have pointed to shifted birth timing and a rise in induced abortions as part of the background (Kaku, 1975).

This is a historical effect the superstition had on society. It is not evidence that people born in a Bing-Wu year carry a special temperament, and the same goes for children born in 2026.

## Samjae in 2026

Under the Korean folk *samjae* (三災, "three calamities") count, the animals in samjae during the Bing-Wu year 2026 are the **pig, rabbit and goat** (亥, 卯, 未). Their samjae began in the Eul-Sa year 2025, 2026 is the middle year, and it ends in the Jeong-Mi year 2027. The counting method and the related customs are covered in [warding off misfortune (aektaem)](/en/aektaem-warding-off-misfortune/). Samjae is a folk calendar that marks a period for caution. It is not a basis for predicting accidents.
