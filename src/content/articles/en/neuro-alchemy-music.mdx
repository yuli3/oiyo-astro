## 1. Sound, the Sculptor of the Brain

The brain is an organ with plasticity: it changes gradually with experience. Studies link long-term instrument practice to some differences in brain structure and function, and music is a stimulus that strongly touches emotion and memory (Levitin, 2006). The “neuro-alchemy” in this article is a metaphor for that experience. It does not mean that a particular song or frequency rewires your brain’s circuits.

## 2. The Frequency Debate: 432Hz vs. 440Hz

440Hz is the standard tuning pitch of modern music, recommended at an international conference in 1939 and later set as ISO 16. Several claims surround it.

- **440Hz**: Some claim it induces tension, but there is no evidence for this.
- **432Hz**: Some believe it resonates with the geometric order of the universe (sacred geometry) and brings deeper relaxation. This is a symbolic interpretation, not a scientific fact.

The claim that 432Hz is better for the body than 440Hz has little support. One small pilot study reported a slightly lower heart rate (Calamassi & Pomponi, 2019), and that is all.

## 3. Solfeggio Frequencies: Their Meanings and the Evidence

The **Solfeggio Frequencies** are often presented as coming from ancient chants, but the list of frequencies in use today was popularized from the 1990s onward. The name is borrowed from medieval solmization syllables (ut, re, mi…); pitches were not defined in hertz at that time. Within this tradition, each frequency is given a meaning:

1. **396Hz**: Letting go of guilt and fear.
2. **528Hz**: Called “the miracle frequency,” a symbol of change and renewal.
3. **639Hz**: Relationships and connection.
4. **741Hz**: Purification and expression.
5. **852Hz**: Spiritual intuition.

Please take these meanings only as symbols for setting your intention during meditation. There is no scientific evidence that 528Hz repairs DNA or that any frequency heals illness.

### 3.1 Binaural Beats and Brainwave Entrainment

When each ear hears a slightly different frequency, you seem to hear a “beat” at the difference between them. Some claim this can induce a meditative state (Theta waves) or a focused state (Gamma waves), but research on whether brainwaves actually follow that frequency is mixed (Ingendoh et al., 2023). If you find binaural beats relaxing, use them as a way to rest, without expecting guaranteed effects.

## 4. Designing Your Day with Music (Life Coordination)

Music is more than background sound; it is a tool you can choose to match the mood and rhythm you need right now.

- **Creative immersion**: Ambient sound without lyrics and with simple harmony is less distracting.
- **Sorting through emotions**: Pieces that move from minor to major can help you let feelings flow and sense hope.
- **Matching your daily rhythm**: Build playlists with a faster BPM for exercise and slow, quiet tracks before sleep.

> This article is not medical information. Claims of frequency healing have no scientific evidence. If you have sleep problems, ringing in the ears or hearing changes, or ongoing anxiety or low mood, please talk to a doctor or qualified professional.
