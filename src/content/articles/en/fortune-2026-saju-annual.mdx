## The 2026 Byeong-O year (丙午年): stem and branch

### The year's Heavenly Stem: Byeong fire (丙火)

**Byeong fire (丙火)** is fire like the sun. It is a warm, open energy that lights what it meets and gives warmth to living things. Its usual traits:

- **Brightness and openness**: energy that shows itself rather than hiding. The 2026 symbol is that things tend to look more transparent and to come into view.
- **Leadership and a central place**: like the sun, a role that stands in the middle and lights what is around it. The symbol is that leadership and influence matter in public life.
- **Creativity and passion**: energy like strong light and heat, full of creative passion. The image is of new ideas and new ventures feeling active.
- **The risk of excess**: too much light can dazzle. An excess of Byeong fire can be read as pride, haste, and overheating.

### The year's Earthly Branch: O fire (午火)

**O fire (午火)** is the character among the twelve Earthly Branches where fire is strongest. It stands for the sun at noon and the height of summer. The animal gloss is the horse (馬).

- **Fire inside the pillar**: O fire stacks on the stem Byeong fire, so the yearly pillar holds two fire characters. That is not a measurement that the year's temperature, or society as a whole, is hot, and it is not a claim that the world is unprecedentedly hot.
- **The horse**: O (午) carries speed, movement, and change.
- **The clash (沖)**: O fire is in a direct clash (正沖) with Ja water (子水). That clash is 子午冲. The animal gloss of 子 is the rat, and its phase is water. Charts with a lot of Ja water are read as meeting large change through the clash. This is a symbol, not a forecast of events.
- **The combination (合)**: O fire meets Mi earth (未土) in the six-combination 午未合. The animal gloss of 未 is the goat, and its phase is earth. Schools differ on whether the combination transforms into earth or into fire. Do not read it as simply strengthening earth.

### The five phases in 2026

Look only at the yearly pillar Byeong-O (丙午): there are **two fire (火) characters**. That is not a measurement that water and wood are scarce in nature. The other phases are absent from the year pillar. They are not missing from the year as a fact about the world. What follows is the symbolic way myeongri often reads that pillar when it meets a day master.

- **The phase with characters in the yearly pillar**: fire (火).
- **Phases absent from the yearly pillar**: water (水), wood (木), earth (土), and metal (金). An absent character is not proof that the year lacks that energy.
- **Generation and control, read in the usual direction**: earth is read as draining fire (draining), because fire generates earth. Metal is read as the side that receives control from fire, because fire controls metal (火剋金). Fire does not control water. Water controls fire (水剋火).

The day masters are then spoken of like this:

- Day masters that carry **water (水)**, Im (壬) and Gye (癸), or a chart that takes water as yongshin, are read inside a tension with fire. The relation is 水剋火: water meeting a lot of fire. It is not fire controlling water.
- Day masters of **metal (金)**, Gyeong (庚) and Sin (辛), are read as tempered by fire controlling metal (火剋金).
- Day masters of **fire (火)**, Byeong (丙) and Jeong (丁), are full of energy, with a concern about excess. A Byeong day master in a Byeong year is bijeon inside the broad bigeop group (比肩 / 比劫). A Jeong day master meeting Byeong stays in that broad bigeop group, more precisely geopjae (劫財).
- Day masters of **wood (木)**, Gap (甲) and Eul (乙), spend energy in the structure wood generates fire (木生火).
- Day masters of **earth (土)**, Mu (戊) and Gi (己), are in a flow that receives fire generating earth (火生土).

The generating cycle is not reversed: wood generates fire, fire generates earth, earth generates metal, metal generates water, and water generates wood. On the control cycle, water controls fire, fire controls metal, and earth controls water.

## 2026 by day master (日干)

### 甲 (Gap) day master — yang wood (陽木)

**What a Gap day master looks like in 2026**

Gap wood is a large tree that grows straight. Because wood generates fire (木生火), Gap wood feeds the strong fire of the Byeong-O year. The structure supplies one's own energy to that stage, so the year is read as active and forward-moving. If the output runs too far, the wood itself can be spent, so the reading treats managing energy as important.

**Overall**: 2026 Gap wood is read as a year of active work and visible results. Taking a leading role or opening a new field is the favorable image. Moving so far ahead that body and mind are spent is the caution, so the reading also asks for room to spare. This is a symbol, not a forecast that events will turn out this way.

**Work**: the symbol favors starting a project, founding something, or showing leadership. It stands out in roles that show the self. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: the symbol places many openings for income in the first half, and says heavy spending can cancel them. This is a symbolic reading, not financial guidance.

**Caution**: spending too much energy makes exhaustion easy. If the body shows symptoms, check with a clinician, not with myeongri.

**Yongshin for a Gap day master**: when water (水) is the yongshin, the reading treats complementing water in 2026 as important. Watery settings, and ties with people whose water is strong, are the image of help. Which phase counts as yongshin depends on the school.

### 乙 (Eul) day master — yin wood (陰木)

**What an Eul day master looks like in 2026**

Eul wood is flexible grass or a vine. It is finer than Gap, and more adaptive. It still generates the strong fire of the Byeong-O year (木生火), but the reading has it riding that current more softly than Gap does.

**Overall**: 2026 Eul wood is read as a year whose strength is flexible adaptation. Matching the strong current gently, rather than pushing against it, is the image of a better result. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol lights up partnership, collaboration, art, and service. Reading the situation finely and taking a well-placed position is the favorable image, more than stepping out in front. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: the symbol is of income that holds steady, and of an attempt at a new source that may show a result in the second half. This is a symbolic reading, not financial guidance, and not a promise that money will arrive.

**Caution**: watch burnout from giving way and sacrificing too much. Keeping one's own boundary matters.

**Yongshin for an Eul day master**: if water or metal is the yongshin, the reading says that complementing that energy in daily life is taken as supporting the 2026 flow. Schools disagree on the yongshin, and this is not a measured method.

### 丙 (Byeong) day master — yang fire (陽火)

**What a Byeong day master looks like in 2026**

The Heavenly Stem of the 2026 yearly cycle (歲運) is Byeong fire, so a Byeong day master meets the **bigeop cycle (比劫運)**. Byeong meeting Byeong is bijeon (比肩), the same-polarity peer, inside that broad bigeop group (比劫). Meeting the same phase is read as overflowing energy and stronger self-expression, and also as sharper competition and a risk of scattering.

**Overall**: a Byeong day master in 2026 is read as confident and busy. Presence in public life grows, and roles that ask for leadership are where one naturally stands. A competitive frame can appear, so a stance of cooperation and sharing is the image of keeping relationships. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol stands out in creative fields, art, education, media, and public work. It is read as a time for making one's own name known. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: the symbol shows many openings for income. In a bigeop cycle it also shows wealth scattering, or spending tied to siblings and friends, so the image includes a clear boundary. This is a symbolic reading, not financial guidance.

**Caution**: if the self stands too much at the center, clashes with others come easily. Body symptoms belong with a clinician.

### 丁 (Jeong) day master — yin fire (陰火)

**What a Jeong day master looks like in 2026**

Jeong fire is a fine fire, like a candle or a lamp. In the strong fire of the Byeong-O year it is read as gaining energy, but taking in too much can make it grow past what one can steer. Jeong meeting the year's Byeong stays in the broad bigeop group (比劫). More precisely it is geopjae (劫財): yin fire meeting yang fire. It is not a control relation, and fire does not control water.

**Overall**: a Jeong day master in 2026 is read as a year when creativity and feeling shine more. Art, counseling, education, religion, and philosophy are the image of a chance to build a world of one's own. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol shows strong results in fields that need fine feeling. Speaking with the public, teaching, and presenting are where it is read as especially bright. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: the favorable image is income drawn from one's own artistic or creative ability. Spending on emotional impulse is the caution. This is a symbolic reading, not financial guidance.

**Caution**: wide emotional swings. Practice keeping one's own boundary.

### 戊 (Mu) day master — yang earth (陽土)

**What a Mu day master looks like in 2026**

Mu earth is a great mountain or the broad ground. Because fire generates earth (火生土), the fire of the Byeong-O year is read as strengthening Mu earth. For a Mu day master, 2026 is the image of **a year that supplies solid energy**.

**Overall**: a Mu day master in 2026 is read as a good flow in which steadiness and growth sit together. What has been piled up grows firmer, and a base for extending into a new field is the image. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol points to fields such as property and building, education, administration, and management, and to widening influence step by step from a steady role. It is not a forecast that a job will go well, and it is not a promise about property prices.

**Money**: myeongri reads earth as swelling because it receives fire. That is not a promise that real estate or investment returns will do well. It is not financial guidance.

**Caution**: chasing only steadiness can mean missing an opening. The reading asks for a willingness to take a fitting challenge and a fitting change.

### 己 (Gi) day master — yin earth (陰土)

**What a Gi day master looks like in 2026**

Gi earth is a finer earth, like farmland or a damp paddy. Like Mu, it is read as receiving the flow in which fire generates earth (火生土), but it takes the energy in a finer, more detailed way.

**Overall**: a Gi day master in 2026 is read as a year of quiet, substantial results. It is not showy. Steady, sincere effort is the image of a concrete result. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol lights up fields that need detailed management and solid running: healthcare work, welfare, farming, food, and education. It is not evidence that a job will go well, and it says nothing about any organ.

**Money**: the symbol is steady saving and a settled direction, rather than a flashy opening. It is not a promise that long-term investing will do well, and it is not financial guidance.

**Caution**: chasing only what is practical, without rest, leads to exhaustion. If digestion feels uncomfortable, see a clinician.

### 庚 (Gyeong) day master — yang metal (陽金)

**What a Gyeong day master looks like in 2026**

Gyeong metal is hard metal, rock, or a large blade. Because fire controls metal (火剋金), the fire of the Byeong-O year is read as tempering Gyeong metal. In the short term that is burden and challenge. Over a longer span it is the image of making the metal harder and sharper. Fire does not control water here. The control on metal comes from fire.

**Overall**: a Gyeong day master in 2026 is read as **a year where trial and growth sit together**. There can be many challenges and much friction, and that process is what the reading calls a strengthening. Meeting the challenge with a steady mind is the stance the text points to. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol places strength in finance, law, the military and the police, healthcare work, and engineering. Even in a competitive setting, the decisiveness and follow-through associated with Gyeong metal are what stand out. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: read as a year under control, the symbol sits closer to guarding than to expanding greatly. A real financial decision is not based on this piece. This is not financial guidance.

**Caution**: if pressure lasts, set rest first. Body symptoms belong with a clinician.

### 辛 (Sin) day master — yin metal (陰金)

**What a Sin day master looks like in 2026**

Sin metal is a fine, beautiful metal, like a jewel or precise ornament. Like Gyeong, it receives the control by which fire controls metal (火剋金), but the tempering is finer. This Sin is the stem 辛, not the branch 申.

**Overall**: a Sin day master in 2026 is read as **a year when precision and perfectionism shine**. Careful preparation and exact execution are the image of an edge in competition. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol has the fineness of Sin metal recognized in analysis, research, healthcare work, law, fashion, and aesthetic fields. Competing by expertise, rather than by standing in front of a crowd, is the favorable image. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: careful handling of money is the strength in the symbol. Keeping a steady income structure, rather than expanding by force, is what the reading calls wiser for 2026. This is a symbolic reading, not financial guidance, and not a promise that investing will do well.

**Caution**: if perfection cannot be put down, stress piles up. Body symptoms belong with a clinician.

### 壬 (Im) day master — yang water (陽水)

**What an Im day master looks like in 2026**

Im water is a great water, like a river or the sea. By 水剋火, water controls fire, so Im water is on the side that meets and governs the fire of the Byeong-O year. Myeongri reads that as wealth-star luck (財星). The stem Byeong fire is indirect wealth (偏財). The Jeong fire inside O fire is direct wealth (正財). The year is also read as so full of fire that the matters and the wealth one has to face arrive all at once. If the day master is weak, the text calls the burden 財多身弱, abundant wealth with a weak self. This relation is water meeting a lot of fire. It is not fire controlling water.

**Overall**: an Im day master in 2026 is read as **a year of much change and much tension**. More energy is needed to keep one's own current. The depth and insight associated with Im water are what the reading says carry a person through. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: the symbol has Im water's deep insight lighting finance, philosophy, psychology, inquiry, and work tied to water as a resource. Moving after enough analysis, rather than in haste, is the image of a better result in 2026. It is not evidence that a job will go well.

**Money**: the year is read as one with a lot of fire to face. The symbol says guard, rather than decide to expand greatly. It is not investment guidance.

**Caution**: the year is read as full of things to face. If tension lasts, rest. Body symptoms belong with a clinician.

### 癸 (Gye) day master — yin water (陰水)

**What a Gye day master looks like in 2026**

Gye water is a fine water, like dew, rain, or a small stream. It meets the fire more delicately than Im does, and like dew that evaporates quickly it can be read as vulnerable to energy running out. The relation is still 水剋火: water meeting a fire that is even stronger. Fire does not control water.

**Overall**: a Gye day master in 2026 is read as a year that needs **the wisdom of strengthening the inside and saving energy**. Reducing outer activity and leaving time to refill the inside is the important image. The fineness and intuition associated with Gye water can still be read as shining in a hard setting. This is a symbol, not a forecast.

**Work**: quiet work that goes deep, such as writing, counseling, and research, is what myeongri speaks of as close to the Gye symbol. That is not evidence the work will succeed.

**Money**: the reading is about reducing what leaks, rather than expanding greatly outward. Do not use it as the basis of a financial decision. It is not financial guidance.

**Caution**: the reading is to cut outer schedules and leave time to recover. Body symptoms belong with a clinician.

## How the yongshin (用神) is used

The yongshin is the phase said to balance a chart. Schools disagree on which phase that is. Eokbu (strength: suppress or support) and johu (seasonal climate) are two of those schools. The colors and directions below are folk custom matched to that custom. They are not a method whose effect has been measured. Read them on the premise that the 2026 yearly pillar contains two fire characters, not on a claim that the world lacks the other phases.

### When the yongshin is fire (火)

This is the year the yongshin is read as growing stronger. The favorable image is to take openings and act. Folk custom pairs red and orange, the south, and fire-related symbols with this case.

### When the yongshin is water (水)

Against a Byeong-O pillar, a water yongshin is read as the weaker side. That is not a measurement that water is scarce in nature. Folk custom pairs black, navy, and blue, the north, and watery settings with a complement: an aquarium, a fountain, or being near the sea. The pairing is not a proven method.

### When the yongshin is metal (金)

Metal is read as receiving fire's challenge, because fire controls metal. Folk custom pairs white and gold tones, the west, and metal objects with this case. The pairing is not a proven method.

### When the yongshin is wood (木)

Wood generates fire this year, so managing energy is the important image. Folk custom pairs green and blue, the east, and growing plants with this case. The pairing is not a proven method.

### When the yongshin is earth (土)

Because fire generates earth, earth is read as strengthened. Folk custom pairs yellow and brown, and the center and the southwest, with this case. The pairing is not a proven method.

## Reading the 2026 ten-year cycle (大運) and the yearly cycle (歲運)

### The ten-year cycle

The daewoon is a large cycle that changes in blocks of about ten years. When a given daewoon starts depends on the person's birth data. People whose daewoon turns in 2026 are read as especially open to large change. That is a symbol, not a forecast of events.

**How the 2026 Byeong-O year sits with each phase of the ten-year cycle:**

- **Fire (火) daewoon + Byeong-O year**: fire stacked on fire. Passion and openings overflow. Watch excess.
- **Water (水) daewoon + Byeong-O year**: fire and water meet. The relation is 水剋火, water meeting a lot of fire, not fire controlling water. Change and friction can be read, and new openings after the change.
- **Metal (金) daewoon + Byeong-O year**: tempered by fire, because fire controls metal. A time whose image is patience and growing skill.
- **Wood (木) daewoon + Byeong-O year**: wood helps fire, because wood generates fire. Active, but watch energy running out.
- **Earth (土) daewoon + Byeong-O year**: receives the flow in which fire generates earth. A time whose image is steady growth and piling up.

### Keywords for the 2026 Byeong-O year

1. **Speed and change**: things are read as moving fast. Openings arrive quickly for someone already prepared. This is a symbol, not a forecast.
2. **Coming into view**: a time when hidden things show. On the bright side, a hidden talent appears. On the hard side, a covered problem comes to the surface.
3. **Leadership and public activity**: being active in public, and leading, is read as mattering.
4. **A burst of creativity**: new ideas and creative work are read as lively.
5. **The value of care**: when everyone is read as moving fast, thinking slowly and deeply becomes more valuable.

### The monthly cycle (月運) in 2026

The month pillar does not change with calendar January or February. It changes at the solar terms. Keep the month with its solar term: In Ipchun, Myo Gyeongchip, Jin Cheongmyeong, Sa Ipha, O Mangjong, Mi Soseo, Sin Ipchu, Yu Baengno, Sul Hallo, Hae Ipdong, Ja Daeseol, Chuk Sohan. Do not map these onto January through December.

- **In Ipchun**: the In month (寅), animal tiger, phase wood, around Ipchun (立春, Start of Spring).
- **Myo Gyeongchip**: the Myo month (卯), animal rabbit, phase wood, around Gyeongchip (驚蟄, Awakening of Insects).
- **Jin Cheongmyeong**: the Jin month (辰), animal dragon, phase earth, around Cheongmyeong (清明, Clear and Bright).
- **Sa Ipha**: the Sa month (巳), animal snake, phase fire, around Ipha (立夏, Start of Summer).
- **O Mangjong**: the O month (午), animal horse, phase fire, around Mangjong (芒種, Grain in Ear).
- **Mi Soseo**: the Mi month (未), animal goat, phase earth, around Soseo (小暑, Minor Heat).
- **Sin Ipchu**: the Sin branch month (申), animal monkey, phase metal, around Ipchu (立秋, Start of Autumn). This is the branch 申, not the stem 辛.
- **Yu Baengno**: the Yu month (酉), animal rooster, phase metal, around Baengno (白露, White Dew).
- **Sul Hallo**: the Sul month (戌), animal dog, phase earth, around Hallo (寒露, Cold Dew).
- **Hae Ipdong**: the Hae month (亥), animal pig, phase water, around Ipdong (立冬, Start of Winter).
- **Ja Daeseol**: the Ja month (子), animal rat, phase water, around Daeseol (大雪, Major Snow).
- **Chuk Sohan**: the Chuk month (丑), animal ox, phase earth, around Sohan (小寒, Minor Cold).

Read in stretches, not as calendar months:

- **Chuk and In**: the stretch that moves from winter earth toward spring wood.
- **Myo, Jin, and Sa**: the stretch that runs from wood into fire. Read as wood helping fire (木生火).
- **O**: the same branch as the year's O fire. Read as fire characters stacking.
- **Mi and Sin**: the stretch that moves from earth toward metal.
- **Yu and Sul**: metal and earth. Read as gathering in and sorting.
- **Hae and Ja**: water appears and meets fire. The relation is 水剋火, water meeting fire, not fire controlling water. Read as lowering the pace.
