## Fix the conditions before interpreting

This chapter does not label people as leaders or artists. Compare three charts under the same settings and distinguish **input → position → house → angle → symbolic interpretation**. These are editorial calculation dates, not birth records of actual participants.

| Condition | Shared settings |
|---|---|
| Location | Seoul: 37.5665° north, 126.978° east |
| Time | January 1 at 12:00 UTC each year, or 21:00 in Korea |
| Zodiac | Tropical; twelve 30° intervals from longitude 0° |
| Position calculation | OIYO's approximate computeNatalChart algorithms |
| Houses | Whole Sign, calculated separately for this chapter |
| Major orbs | Conjunction, square, trine, opposition: 8°; sextile: 4° |
| Display precision | Three decimal places, not an accuracy guarantee |

These values reproduce engine output. They are not an independent ephemeris comparison or a verified error bound. Distinguish the exact Ascendant from its containing sign. The first Whole Sign house starts at **0° of the rising sign**. Do not assume every tool screen uses this system.

For Whole Sign houses, subtract the rising-sign index from the body's sign index, wrap the result into 0–11, and add one. With Leo rising, Leo is House 1, Virgo House 2, and Capricorn House 6.

The [Swiss Ephemeris house documentation](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swisseph.htm) explains Whole Sign versus Ascendant-based Equal houses. It is not the calculator used for the values below.

The Sun and Mercury are both in Capricorn, but their separation is **8.466°**, exceeding this chapter's 8° conjunction orb. Rounding to 8° before classifying would change the boundary decision.

The Moon at 223.318° and Saturn at 40.376° differ by 182.942°. Their smaller separation is **177.058°**, deviating **2.942°** from opposition at 180°, within the 8° orb. Check degrees rather than just sign names.

As a symbolic exercise, combine self-expression assigned to the Sun with daily work assigned to House 6. This does not establish job performance or a health problem. Form questions about experience rather than letting a chart supply answers.

## A checklist for reading an actual chart

1. **Input:** Record local date, time, time zone, daylight saving, and coordinates. Local noon is not automatically noon UTC.
2. **Settings:** Distinguish tropical from sidereal zodiac, the house system, included bodies, and algorithm.
3. **Positions:** Sign-boundary values are sensitive to input and calculation error. Do not assert the Ascendant or houses when time is unknown.
4. **Angles:** Apply Chapter 5's smaller-angle and orb rules to each pair. Line colors vary by software; color alone is not a standard.
5. **Interpretation:** Record numerical results separately from traditional meanings. Do not force experience to fit the interpretation.

Arbitrary noon for an unknown birth time is a computational assumption, not a recovered time. Do not publish or share someone else's birth date, place, and time without their consent, even when seeking to calculate their chart more precisely.
