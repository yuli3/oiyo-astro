## Standard Saju Analysis Process

We now synthesize everything learned so far to analyze charts. The three cases below are hypothetical charts made for explanation. The standard order of analysis is:

```
Step 1: Identify the Day Master (日干) → understand the "self"
Step 2: Check Five Element distribution → assess strength balance
Step 3: Month Branch (月支) season → environmental energy
Step 4: Analyze the Ten Gods → life patterns
Step 5: Identify the Pattern (格局) → life direction
Step 6: Derive the Lucky Element (用神) → the needed energy
Step 7: Apply Major and Annual Luck → temporal flow
Step 8: Comprehensive interpretation → practical advice
```

## Case 1: Gab-Wood Day Master with an Entrepreneurial Character

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:    甲     丙      甲     壬
Branch:  子     寅      午     申
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gab-Wood (甲木)**
- Upright and powerfully driven energy like a towering tree
- Leadership, pioneer spirit, strong self-respect
- Values principles over compromise

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | Gab (Year), In (Month), Gab (Day) | 3 |
| Fire | Byeong (Month Stem), O (Day Branch) | 2 |
| Water | Im (Hour), Ja (Year Branch) | 2 |
| Metal | Sin (Hour Branch) | 1 |
| Earth | None | 0 |

Wood is the strongest element; Earth is entirely absent.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **In (寅)** → Spring (approximately February–March)
Spring Gab-Wood → even stronger Wood energy

### Step 4: Ten God Analysis

Based on Gab-Wood Day Master:
- Gab (Year Stem): Bigyeon → competitive/independent energy
- Byeong (Month Stem): Siksin → creativity/expressiveness
- Im (Hour Stem): Pyeonin → intuition/originality
- Ja (Year Branch): Jeongin (main energy Gye-Water)
- In (Month Branch): Bigyeon → competitive/independent
- O (Day Branch): Sanggwan → expression/innovation
- Sin (Hour Branch): Pyeongwan (Gyeong-Metal) → challenge/pressure

**Characteristic**: Bigeop (Bigyeon/Geopjae) and Siksang (Siksin/Sanggwan) are strong.

### Step 5: Pattern

Month Branch In-Wood → **Geollok Pattern (建祿格)**: the Day Master 甲 has its prosperity root (祿) in the Month Branch 寅. Strong self-direction; well-suited to independent business.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Gab-Wood is very strong → a strongly dominant chart (身强) needing control

**Lucky Element**: Gyeong-Metal (庚金) — strongly controls Gab-Wood (Metal cuts Wood)
**Favorable Element**: Byeong-Fire (丙火) — drains excess Wood energy
**Unfavorable Element**: Im-Water / Bigeop — further strengthens Wood

### Step 7: Luck Cycle Application (hypothetical)

If currently in a **Mu-Earth (戊土) Major Luck Cycle**:
- Earth is Jaeseong for Wood (Wood controls Earth)
- A period of material achievement for the Gab-Wood Day Master
- Strong chart in a Jaeseong cycle → time to move actively in business/wealth

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This chart has the **powerful will, drive, and creative expressiveness of an entrepreneur or creator**.

- **Careers**: Startup founder, content creator, consultant, independent professional
- **Watch out for**: The absence of Earth means practical sense may be lacking. Better to entrust financial management to a trusted partner
- **Lucky Element use**: Strengthening Gyeong-Metal energy (clear principles, decisiveness) gives direction to that drive
- **Relationships**: People with Gyeong or Sin Day Masters can provide Lucky Element energy

## Case 2: Gi-Earth Day Master with Strong Expression

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:   庚     乙      己     庚
Branch: 申     酉      丑     午
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gi-Earth (己土)** — fine, fertile field soil

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Metal | 庚, 申, 酉, 庚 | 4 |
| Earth | 己, 丑 | 2 |
| Wood | 乙 | 1 |
| Fire | 午 | 1 |
| Water | none | 0 |

Metal dominates. There is no Water at all.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **Yu (酉)** → autumn (8th lunar month)
Gi-Earth in autumn → a cold, dry chart

### Step 4: Ten Gods

Based on the Gi-Earth Day Master:
- 庚 (Year and Hour Stems): Sanggwan → expression, innovation
- 乙 (Month Stem): Pyeongwan → pressure, challenge
- 申 (Year Branch): Sanggwan / 酉 (Month Branch): Siksin
- 丑 (Day Branch): Bigyeon
- 午 (Hour Branch): Pyeonin → intuition, learning

Siksang is very strong, with one Pyeongwan and one Pyeonin.

### Step 5: Pattern

**Siksang Pattern**: the main energy of the Month Branch 酉 is 辛, a Siksin, and the Sanggwan 庚 appears among the stems. Siksang forms the pattern: original and highly expressive.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Metal drains much of the Day Master's strength, so the chart is **weak (身弱)**. Being autumn, it is also cold.
→ **Johu Lucky Element**: Fire — warm it up
→ **Eokbu Lucky Element**: Fire (Inseong) — add strength to the Day Master

Both methods point to the same answer.

**Lucky Element**: Fire (午 in the hour branch)
**Favorable Element**: Earth (Bigeop)
**Unfavorable Element**: Metal, Water (they drain the chart or make it colder)

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This chart is **sensitive and original but needs warmth and support**.

- **Careers**: Designer, writer, technical specialist (creative fields where Siksang shines)
- **Interpersonal**: Conflict in environments that impose rigid rules. Suits a free and creative setting
- **Lucky Element use**: Warm colors (red, orange), south-facing environments, partners with a Byeong-Fire or Jeong-Fire Day Master
- **Watch out for**: Cold, dark environments (basement, north-facing, winter) tend to drain energy

## Case 3: Gye-Water Day Master with an Artistic Character

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:   丁     壬      癸     己
Branch: 亥     寅      卯     未
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gye-Water (癸水)** — delicate, deep water like dew and rain

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Water | 壬, 癸, 亥 | 3 |
| Wood | 寅, 卯 | 2 |
| Earth | 己, 未 | 2 |
| Fire | 丁 | 1 |
| Metal | none | 0 |

By count Water looks plentiful, but the branches **亥, 卯, and 未 form the Three Harmony Wood structure (木局)**, and the Month Branch is 寅 as well. In practice Wood is the strongest element. There is no Metal.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **In (寅)** → spring. Wood energy drains the Gye-Water Day Master.

### Step 4: Ten Gods

For a Gye-Water Day Master, Wood is **Siksang (食傷)**: the energy of creativity and expression.

A chart with very strong Siksang → overflowing drive to express, creativity, and artistry.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Gye-Water Day Master weakened by excessive Siksang → **weak (身弱)**

**Lucky Element**: Metal (Metal generates Water and strengthens Gye-Water) — Gyeong and Sin
**Favorable Element**: Water (Bigeop — Im and Gye)
**Unfavorable Element**: Wood (too much draining)

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This is the chart of **an artist and communicator with marked creativity and artistry**.

- **Careers**: Musician, writer, film director, content creator
- **Strengths**: Original ideas, rich sensitivity, expressiveness
- **Weaknesses**: Energy runs out quickly; practical judgment is lacking
- **Lucky Element use**: Metal energy (partners with a Gyeong or Sin Day Master, white, west-facing settings) brings stability
- **Watch out for**: Energy depletion in spring and summer; recharge in autumn and winter

## Saju Analysis Wrap-up — Three Core Principles

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 1**: Saju is a map of possibilities. A strong chart won't shine without effort; a challenging chart can be overcome with wise choices.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 2**: Once you know your Lucky Element, use it actively. Treat it as a reference for direction when you choose your environment, colors, orientation, and partners.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 3**: Saju is a starting point, not a destination. The choices and efforts of each moment hold more power than any chart.
</Callout>
