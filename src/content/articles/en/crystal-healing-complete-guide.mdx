Attaching a meaning to stones is a custom in more than one culture. In ancient Egypt, lapis lazuli was used as ornament and as a stone of protection. In East Asia, jade has long been held dear. The name crystal healing today is not that whole older custom. It is closer to a modern practice in which the wellness market writes an effect next to each stone and sells it.

## The physics of a stone, and the word healing

A crystal is a mineral that grew for a long time until it became a crystal. Quartz has a **piezoelectric effect**: when it is pressed, a weak voltage appears. Clocks, and the oscillators in electronic circuits, actually use that. The fact is not evidence that holding a stone heals a person's body.

People who practice crystal healing say that each stone resonates with a chakra or with energy. A chakra is a symbol of a state of mind. It is not a map for treating the organs.

<Callout type="info">
**The experiment.** In a controlled experiment, people holding a real crystal and people holding a fake one reported similar sensations. The difference sits closer to expectation than to the stone (French et al., 2001). Using a stone as an anchor for meditation can remain apart from that result. It is not treatment of an illness.
</Callout>

## Customs called cleansing

There is no rule that a stone must be cleansed before you use it. The claim that it absorbs a previous owner's energy is not an experimental fact. What follows are rituals people perform.

- **Moonlight.** On the night of a full moon, leave it by the window and bring it in in the morning. For most stones this is physically gentle.
- **Sunlight.** Some people leave a stone in the morning sun for a short while. A colored stone such as amethyst can fade if it stays out for a long time.
- **Water.** Some people rinse a stone briefly. Selenite is gypsum, so water damages it, and malachite is also easily damaged by water and by acid. Do not put those two in water.
- **Smoke.** People wrap the stone in the smoke of white sage or palo santo. You do not have to recite a spell for them to call it a cleansing.
- **A cluster of clear quartz.** A custom of leaving other stones on it for a day. There is no evidence that energy moves across.

## Writing an intention

There is an order in which, after cleansing, a purpose is set into the stone. You hold it, steady the breath, bring to mind what you want to do, and say that this stone is activated for that purpose. Activation is not a physical switch. It is a ritual of saying aloud what you mean to do. It does not mean that picturing only the result, as if it had already happened, raises achievement.

## Where people put them

Placements people often write down.

- Rose quartz or amethyst in the bedroom. This is not a treatment for sleep. If sleep stays poor for a long time, see a clinician.
- Citrine or clear quartz where you work.
- Black tourmaline or obsidian by the entrance.

The claim that a necklace against the skin passes energy more strongly is sales copy. So is the claim that citrine in a wallet brings luck with money.
