## Low Mood and Major Depression

**Low mood** is a feeling many people have. Sadness and low motivation after a job loss, a breakup, or a bereavement can be a natural response.

**Major depressive disorder** is a diagnosis clinicians consider when that low mood lasts most of the day, nearly every day, or when it seriously disrupts daily functioning. A clear external cause is not always present.

The WHO fact sheet (updated 11 September 2026; estimates for 2023) puts depression at about **322 million** people, roughly **4%** of the world's population. Among adults the estimate is **5.2%** (6.2% of women, 4.1% of men). In South Korea, the 2021 national mental health survey reported a lifetime prevalence of **7.7%** for major depressive disorder.

<Callout type="warning" title="Signs that professional help is needed">
The list below is not a full diagnostic checklist. If some of these last **more than two weeks** and get in the way of daily life, please talk with a mental health professional. Two weeks is the window often used for a major depressive episode. Generalized anxiety is usually described as excessive worry lasting many months.

- Sadness or emptiness most of the time
- Much less interest or pleasure in almost all activities
- Unintended weight change of more than 5% of body weight in a month
- Trouble sleeping, or sleeping far more than usual
- Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
- Repeated thoughts of death

**Crisis**: If you may act on thoughts of suicide, contact a local crisis line now or go to an emergency room.

**United States** (24 hours): call or text **988**; text HOME to **741741**; SAMHSA **1-800-662-4357**.
**Korea** (24 hours): suicide prevention **109**, mental health crisis **1577-0199**. Emergency **119**.
</Callout>

## 3. Read the brain story as a hypothesis

Researchers connect the amygdala, prefrontal cortex, hippocampus, serotonin signaling, and the stress-hormone axis with depression and anxiety. Some studies report a more reactive amygdala or weaker prefrontal regulation. The idea that low serotonin is the cause of depression is a long-used hypothesis, not a settled single cause.

Medicines are not chosen from that hypothesis alone. Benefits and side effects differ from person to person. Please decide starts and stops with the clinician who prescribed them.

## 4. Practices often used in cognitive behavioral therapy

Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) looks at how thoughts, feelings, and actions pull on each other. It is not the first choice for every person.

**Looking again at a thought**
1. Write the sentence that arrived on its own. For example: "I always fail."
2. List what fits that sentence and what does not.
3. Try a less lopsided sentence. For example: "I have failed at things, and I have also finished things."

**Doing a little more**
Stopping activity when you feel low can sink the mood further. Therapists often suggest starting with something small, such as a 15-minute walk, even when motivation is missing.

**Approaching what anxiety makes you avoid**
Avoidance can feel better in the moment and give the worry more room later. Exposure is not a test of endurance you do alone. Keep it safe and gradual, with a professional when you can.

## 6. Everyday care you can try

None of this replaces treatment. If pain is new, or if sleep or appetite keeps falling apart, see a clinician first.

**Moving**
Studies look at activity hard enough to raise your breathing, several times a week. A rise in BDNF is a proposed pathway, not a promise. This page does not say that exercise replaces medicine for mild depression.

**Sleep**
Keep wake time and bedtime roughly steady. Cut screens in the hour before sleep. Worrying or watching videos in bed can teach the bed to be a place of alertness.

**Attention**
Ten minutes a day of noticing the breath is often offered as a starting practice. That duration is not a treatment dose. An app is a support, not care by itself.

**People**
Isolation can make a low mood heavier. It is not established as the single strongest cause. One meaningful conversation in a week is a reasonable place to start.

**Food**
Omega-3 fats and eating patterns rich in vegetables, fish, and olive oil have been studied alongside mood. Food alone is not a treatment for depression. This page does not claim that sugar passes through inflammation and then worsens mood in a straight line.
