## What Is a House?

A house represents **a specific domain of life**. If a planet says 'what' and a sign says 'how,' a house answers **'where'** that energy operates.

Houses depend on birth time, place, and the selected **house system**. Different systems can assign different boundaries and house placements to the same birth data.

| System | First-house boundary | MC and the tenth house |
|---|---|---|
| Whole Sign | 0° of the sign containing the Ascendant | MC need not coincide with the tenth-house boundary |
| Ascendant-based Equal | Exact Ascendant degree, followed by 30° intervals | MC need not coincide with the tenth-house boundary |
| Placidus | Exact Ascendant degree | Uses MC as the tenth-house boundary |

See [Swiss Ephemeris section 6.2 for house-system definitions and section 6.4 for high-latitude calculation limits](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swisseph.htm). Placidus can encounter limits at high latitudes; check the calculator's warnings or fallback system. Correct calculations do not validate the symbolic life themes assigned to them.

## The Ascendant (Rising Sign)

The **Ascendant** is the ecliptic's intersection with the eastern horizon at birth. The rising sign is the 30-degree interval containing this point, not its exact degree. Its relation to the first-house boundary depends on the system above.

| Element | Symbolizes |
|---|---|
| Sun sign | Inner essence, conscious self |
| **Ascendant** | **External impression, first appearance, interface with the world** |
| Moon sign | Emotional response, the unconscious |

The table records symbolic associations, not a measured finding that the Ascendant determines appearance or first impressions.

<Callout type="tip">
**Rising-sign intervals are not constant**. They depend on latitude and time rather than always changing every two hours. Do not assert a specific Ascendant or house placement when the birth time is uncertain.
</Callout>

## Symbolic domains of the twelve houses

These are symbolic themes assigned within astrology, not measurements or predictions of personality, income, health, or relationship outcomes. Houses and signs are distinct coordinates: House 2 is not automatically Taurus. Calculate actual cusp signs and degrees using the chosen house system.

### House 1 — Self and First Impressions
**Keywords**: Appearance, self-awareness, first impressions, physical body
**The house where the Ascendant is located**

Traditional readings associate House 1 with self-presentation, the body and first impressions.

Some interpretations associate planets here with outward expression. This is not evidence of a physical effect on appearance or first impressions.

---

### House 2 — Wealth and Values
**Keywords**: Money, material possessions, personal values, talents

House 2 is associated with possessions, money and personal values. These themes can prompt reflection on what you value; the placement does not measure income or ability.

---

### House 3 — Communication and Learning
**Keywords**: Communication, siblings, short trips, learning, neighbors

House 3 brings together themes of everyday communication, learning, siblings and neighbors. It is not a test of learning style, expression or actual family relationships.

---

### House 4 — Home and Roots
**Keywords**: Family, home, roots, psychological foundation, mother
**IC relationship**: In Placidus, the IC is the fourth-house cusp. This is not universal across house systems.

Readings of House 4 focus on home, roots and private life. Some traditions associate it with the mother; this correspondence does not establish someone's family environment or sense of safety.

---

### House 5 — Creativity and Joy
**Keywords**: Creativity, romance, children, play, self-expression

Creativity, play, romance and children are themes assigned to House 5. They do not determine creative ability, romantic conduct or relationships with children.

---

### House 6 — Work and Health
**Keywords**: Health, daily work, service, routines, pets

House 6 is assigned themes of daily work, routines, and health management. It is not a basis for diagnosis or treatment; actual health decisions require symptoms and medical evidence.

---

### House 7 — Partnership
**Keywords**: Marriage, partnerships, contracts, public relationships
**DC relationship**: The Descendant is 180 degrees opposite the Ascendant in ecliptic longitude. Its relationship to the seventh-house cusp depends on the system.

House 7 is associated with one-to-one relationships, including marriage, contracts and business partnerships.

<Callout type="note">
**Limits of relationship interpretation**: Do not infer another person's intentions, personality, or consent from these symbols. Rather than attributing every conflict to your own “projection,” check actual conversations and behavior.
</Callout>

---

### House 8 — Depth and Transformation
**Keywords**: Death and rebirth, shared assets, psychological depth, other people's money

House 8 is associated with inheritance, shared resources, transformation and psychological depth. Death and rebirth are also traditional themes, not a basis for predicting a death.

---

### House 9 — Philosophy and Exploration
**Keywords**: Higher education, philosophy, travel, religion, foreign lands

House 9 groups themes of higher education, philosophy, religion, travel and foreign experience. A placement does not establish someone's beliefs or educational outcomes.

---

### House 10 — Career and Honor
**Keywords**: Career, fame, social status, father
**MC relationship**: The MC is the upper intersection of the ecliptic and meridian, not the zenith. It is the tenth-house cusp in Placidus, but not necessarily in Whole Sign or Ascendant-based Equal houses.

House 10 is assigned themes of occupation, public roles, and reputation. It is not a test of employability or success in a particular career.

---

### House 11 — Networks and Dreams
**Keywords**: Friends, groups, social ideals, hopes

Friends, communities, social ideals and hopes are associated with House 11. It does not identify actual group membership or measure the quality of a network.

---

### House 12 — Solitude and Reflection
**Keywords**: Retreat, unconscious, solitude, self-sacrifice, spiritual inquiry

House 12 is associated with retreat, unconscious themes, solitude and spiritual inquiry. It does not identify hidden strengths, weaknesses or the extent of spiritual development.

---

## House Strength: Angular, Succedent, and Cadent

| Type | Houses | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Angular** | 1, 4, 7, 10 | First house in each quadrant |
| **Succedent** | 2, 5, 8, 11 | House following an angular house |
| **Cadent** | 3, 6, 9, 12 | House preceding the next angular house |

Traditional interpretations may give angular placements extra weight. This does not measure social influence or establish a likelihood of becoming famous.

---

## House 12 — Hidden Strengths

Some interpretations associate House 12 with solitude, retreat, and reflection. This placement cannot establish hidden talent or a person's level of spiritual growth.

As reflection prompts, you could ask the following. Look for answers in experience, not in the chart.

- When does time alone help you?
- Which interests or projects would you like to express?
- Do you maintain your boundaries while helping others?

## References

These references serve different purposes. Historical works describe interpretive traditions; the [Swiss Ephemeris house documentation](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swisseph.htm) describes calculation methods and their limitations. Neither establishes that a house placement predicts a life outcome. Empirical studies below address particular personality or birth-date hypotheses; they do not test every claim about houses or events.

- Ptolemy. *Tetrabiblos* (2nd century CE). Trans. F. E. Robbins. Loeb Classical Library, Harvard University Press, 1940.

- Dean, G., & Kelly, I. W. (2003). Is astrology relevant to consciousness and psi? *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, 10(6–7), 175–198.
