**Winter Cool Deep** is a deep, cool corner of seasonal color. Navy that sits like deep water, burgundy that leans plum rather than brown, black that can read as a base instead of a costume. On a face this palette suits, the colors do not need to shout. They hold.

This is a draping framework from color consulting. It is not genetically determined, and it is not a medical category.

## What Is Winter Cool Deep?

Winter Cool Deep pairs a **cool undertone** (pink, blue, or cool olive in the skin) with **deep coloring**. Winter Cool Bright is the clearer, higher-chroma cool: vivid, with less weight. Winter Cool Deep is darker in value. Its chroma runs from medium to high, but it is not more saturated than Bright. Jewel depth is not the same thing as Bright's clarity.

**Often discussed features:**

- Skin: deep cool brown, medium-to-deep cool complexion, or cool olive with depth. A lighter cool skin is also discussed here when hair and eyes are very dark and the contrast is strong
- Eyes: very dark brown, black-brown, or another deep cool dark iris
- Hair: very dark brown, black, or deep cool-toned dark hair, without a warm red cast
- Overall impression: cool, rich, and high in contrast

**The pair to keep**: cool and deep. Yellow undertone fights the palette. Pale colors lack the weight. Vivid color with no depth belongs more often to the Bright conversation than to this one.

## Your Signature Colors

### Colors that carry the palette

**Deep navy.** A dark, cool blue. Not summer's powder blue, and not the clearer royal of Winter Cool Bright. A deep navy is the non-black color this palette wears most easily.

**Deep burgundy (cool).** Red-purple with a blue undertone, not a brown one. Autumn's warm burgundy leans the other way. This one leans toward plum.

**Plum.** Deep purple-red. Cool, dark, jewel-like. One of the colors most often named with this palette. It is not reserved for one season type alone.

**Deep emerald.** A rich green with a cool undertone. Darker and less neon than the vivid emerald often paired with Winter Cool Bright.

**Cool forest.** A blue-leaning deep green. Classic hunter green with a yellow cast belongs with warmer palettes, not here.

**Cool charcoal.** Dark grey with a cool undertone. Depth without making pure black do all the work.

**Deep cool jewels.** Deep sapphire, deep amethyst, deep cool teal. Deep versions, not pale ones.

### Neutrals

- **True black.** A base neutral in this palette. Natural on some faces, harsh on others. It is not required
- **Deep cool navy.** A blue stand-in for black
- **Cool charcoal.** For when pure black feels stark
- **Cool white, ice grey, silver.** Contrast against the darks

Neutrals usually set aside here: ivory, camel, warm beige.

**Metal.** Cool silver, white gold, platinum. Warm yellow gold is not forbidden, but it often sits apart from the undertone.

### Colors to approach carefully

- **Warm colors.** The cool undertone is the point of the palette
- **Light, airy pastels.** They lack the weight this coloring is usually matched with
- **Bright, vivid color with no depth.** That conversation belongs more to Winter Cool Bright

## Makeup

### Foundation and base

Cool-toned foundations, in a depth that matches the skin. Do not lift the face lighter than it is, and do not warm it to make it "even." Coverage can run from medium to fuller when the skin asks for it. Fuller coverage is not a rule.

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: deep, cool, rich tones. Dark plum, deep charcoal, deep navy, cool dark brown, black with a cool shimmer. A smoky eye can sit well here because the features already have depth. It is a choice, not a requirement.

**Eyeliner**: black, cool dark navy, or deep purple. Black liner is the usual first choice in this palette, not the only one.

**Mascara**: black.

### Cheeks

Cool berry, deep cool rose, or soft plum. Deeper skin can take more pigment than a very light face. The shape can be clear without being heavy.

**Bronzer**: a cool bronzer, or a neutral one without an orange undertone. Warm bronzers tend to sit apart from this palette.

### Lips

- **Deep burgundy or plum.** The lip colors most often named with this palette. Cool and dark. Not the only option
- **Cool red.** Clear against a cool face, without going orange
- **Deep berry.** Dark, cool, quiet
- **Cranberry.** Still cool, a step less dark

**Usually set aside**: warm nude, coral, warm brown, orange-red, golden nude, pastel pink.

## Fashion and Wardrobe

The wardrobe idea is depth plus cool. The effect people are usually after is steady and definite, not loud.

**Core colors:**

- True black
- Deep navy
- Deep burgundy (cool)
- Plum
- Deep emerald
- Cool forest and deep cool teal
- Cool charcoal
- Deep sapphire
- Cool white, for contrast

Deep cool tones sit next to each other without much argument. A closet that is already dark and cool may overlap this palette. That overlap is not proof of a type.

**All black** is one formula in this palette, not a costume that only one season can wear. On a cool, deep face, black can look chosen rather than harsh. On another face it can still look stark.

**Pattern**: bold geometry and graphic prints in deep cool colors, with strong contrast. Large scale suits the weight of the palette better than a tiny pastel print.

**Fabric**: matte silk, cashmere, leather, velvet in deep cool tones. Texture carries the same richness the color does. It is a suggestion, not a dress code.

### Colors usually set aside in clothes

- Earth tones: camel, terracotta, yellow-cast olive
- Pastels
- Golden yellows
- Orange
- Bright color with no depth

## Hair Color

**Usually easier:**

- Natural very dark brown to black
- Deep cool brown
- Blue-black
- Cool dark brown, if any highlight stays cool and deep
- Deep burgundy on a blue-red base

**Approach carefully:**

- Auburn or warm red. The warm cast fights a cool face
- Warm copper, warm golden highlights, light caramel brown
- Platinum blonde. The contrast can put the hair in front of the face. That is a styling risk, not a ban

---

Winter Cool Deep is the palette of deep winter. The hour when the sky is the darkest blue it can be before it goes black. It is not the palette of warmth or of pale softness. Pale color lacks weight. Warm color fights the undertone. A deep navy or plum can feel, for the first time, like a color that belongs with the face rather than on top of it.

The color does not add a personality. It stops a shade from taking the face away.
