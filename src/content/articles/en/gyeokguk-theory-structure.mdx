## Introduction: Two Ways of Seeing Saju

Beginners count Five Elements when reading Saju: "lots of Wood," "no Fire." But advanced Saju theory looks at **structure (格局, Gyeokguk)**. Like an architect viewing a building's blueprint rather than counting bricks, Gyeokguk theory analyzes what **patterns and order** the 8 characters of Saju form.

Gyeokguk theory was systematized in Ming Dynasty scholar Wan Minying's _Sanming Tonghui_ (三命通會) and Qing Dynasty scholar Shen Xiaozhan's _Ziping Zhenquan_ (子平真詮), and is regarded as one of the core theories of Saju.

## History and Origins

### Xu Ziping's Revolution (Song Dynasty)

Until the Tang Dynasty, Saju focused on the Year Pillar. But Xu Ziping (徐子平), traditionally placed in the early Song Dynasty (10th century; his exact dates are uncertain), shifted the paradigm to **Day Pillar-centric** analysis. He defined: "The Day Stem is myself, and the other 7 characters are relationships I form." This was the birth of **Ziping Saju Theory (子平命理學)**.

### Completion in Ming-Qing Era

- **Wan Minying (Ming):** Compiled hundreds of Gyeokguk patterns in _Sanming Tonghui_
- **Shen Xiaozhan (Qing):** Organized the eight month-based structures as **Jeonggyeok (正格, Regular)** in _Ziping Zhenquan_, treating other special patterns separately as miscellaneous structures (雜格), the category now usually called **Oegyeok (外格, Special)**
- **Ren Tieqiao:** Explored philosophical meaning of Gyeokguk in _Ditian Sui Chanwei_ (滴天髓闡微)

## Core Concept Analysis

### 1. Jeonggyeok (正格): Normal Structure

When the Day Stem (self) receives energy from the Month Branch (birth month) forming a **balanced structure**.

**8 Types of Jeonggyeok:**

- **Jeonggwan-gyeok (正官格):** Month Branch shows Direct Officer. Pursues order, honor, stability.
- **Pyeongwan-gyeok (偏官格, Seven Killings):** Month Branch shows Indirect Officer. Power, competition, struggle.
- **Jeongjae-gyeok (正財格):** Month Branch shows Direct Wealth. Steady accumulation, manager type.
- **Pyeonjae-gyeok (偏財格):** Month Branch shows Indirect Wealth. Entrepreneur, liquidity, expansion.
- **Siksin-gyeok (食神格):** Month Branch shows Eating God. Creation, expression, pure joy.
- **Sanggwan-gyeok (傷官格):** Month Branch shows Hurting Officer. Criticism, innovation, art.
- **Jeongin-gyeok (正印格):** Month Branch shows Direct Resource. Scholarship, motherhood, protection.
- **Pyeongin-gyeok (偏印格):** Month Branch shows Indirect Resource. Unique talent, solitary research.

**Conditions for Jeonggyeok:**

1. Month Branch energy must appear (透出) in Heavenly Stems
2. The entire chart must not **destroy** the structure (e.g., Hurting Officer attacking Direct Officer breaks the pattern)
3. Yongsin (用神, Useful God) must be clear

### 2. Oegyeok (外格): Special Structure

When Day Stem is too strong or weak, abandoning normal balance and **tilting to extremes**.

#### Jonggyeok (從格): Following Structure

Day Stem is too weak to resist, so it **follows** the strong side.

- **Jongsal-gyeok (從殺格):** Officers overwhelm. Follows authority or becomes dictator.
- **Jongjae-gyeok (從財格):** Wealth overwhelms. Follows money. Entrepreneur, materialist.
- **Jonga-gyeok (從兒格):** Food/Hurting overwhelms. Follows children or creations. Artist.

**Conditions for Jonggyeok:**

- Almost no Resources (印星) or Companions (比劫) supporting Day Stem
- If Great Fortune brings supporting energy, the structure is said to **break (破格)**, which is read as unfavorable

#### Hwagyeok (化格): Transformation Structure

When Heavenly Stem **combination (合)** occurs, Day Stem **transforms** into another element.

- **Gapgi-haphwa-to (甲己合化土):** Gap Wood Day Stem meets Gi Earth, transforms to Earth
- **Eulgyeong-haphwa-geum (乙庚合化金):** Eul Wood meets Gyeong Metal, transforms to Metal
- **Byeongsin-haphwa-su (丙辛合化水):** Byeong Fire meets Sin Metal, transforms to Water
- **Jeongim-haphwa-mok (丁壬合化木):** Jeong Fire meets Im Water, transforms to Wood
- **Mugye-haphwa-hwa (戊癸合化火):** Mu Earth meets Gye Water, transforms to Fire

Either stem of a pair can be the Day Stem. For example, a Gi Earth Day Stem combining with Gap Wood is also Gapgi-haphwa-to.

**Conditions for Hwagyeok:**

- The Day Stem must combine with an adjacent stem (Month Stem or Hour Stem)
- Transformed element must match Month Branch energy (e.g., Gapgi-haphwa-to requires birth in a Jin, Sul, Chuk, or Mi month)
- Hwagyeok means **identity change**. Abandoning original self, immersing in new role.

### 3. Success and Failure of Gyeokguk

For Gyeokguk to **succeed (成)**:

1. **Cheong (淸, Clear):** No mixed energies, must be clean
2. **Sun (純, Pure):** One energy must be pure
3. **Junghwa (中和, Harmony):** Neither excessive nor deficient

Gyeokguk **fails (敗)** when:

1. **Honjap (混雜, Mixed):** Direct and Indirect Officers mixed
2. **Pagyeok (破格, Broken):** Character breaking the structure appears
3. **Muryeok (無力, Powerless):** Insufficient power to form structure

## Advanced: Debates and Interpretations

### Classical vs Modern View

**Classical view (structure-centered reading):**

- Gyeokguk is **absolute**. Once determined, you must live accordingly.
- Jonggyeok means "abandoning resistance," thus weak subjectivity.

**Modern view (counseling-centered reading):**

- Gyeokguk is merely **tendency**. Humans can change fate with free will.
- Jonggyeok can be reinterpreted as "wisdom of flowing with current."

### Psychological Reinterpretation

- **Jeonggyeok:** "Adaptive personality" finding balance within social norms
- **Jonggyeok:** "Survival personality" adapted to extreme environment (e.g., Jongsal = authoritarian tendency)
- **Hwagyeok:** Identity confusion or "role-immersed personality"

## Practical Application

### Career Images Linked to Gyeokguk

The list below only translates each structure's traditional tendencies into career images. It is not a statistical distribution or career advice.

- **Jeonggwan-gyeok:** Civil servant, lawyer, corporate executive
- **Pyeonjae-gyeok:** Entrepreneur, investor, sales
- **Siksin-gyeok:** Chef, writer, YouTuber
- **Sanggwan-gyeok:** Critic, innovator, artist
- **Jongsal-gyeok:** Military, police, CEO (strong leadership)
- **Jongjae-gyeok:** Real estate investor, finance

### Reading Gyeokguk Changes in Great Fortune

Gyeokguk is not **fixed**. When new energy enters in Great Fortune (10-year cycle), Gyeokguk **succeeds** or **fails**.

**Example:** Jeonggwan-gyeok chart (a hypothetical illustration; the age at which each Great Fortune begins differs from person to person)

- First Fortune: Resource Fortune → Officer and Resource support each other (官印相生), read as favorable for study and certifications
- Second Fortune: Wealth Fortune → Wealth generates Officer (財生官) and is often read as supporting the structure. But if Resource is the Yongsin, Wealth attacking Resource (財剋印) can become a burden
- Third Fortune: Companion Fortune → Companions attack Wealth, sometimes read as financial loss and intensified competition

## Wrapping Up: Insights from Gyeokguk Theory

Gyeokguk theory is not merely a tool to judge "good chart, bad chart." It answers the existential question: **"What structure was I born into?"**

Jeonggyeok is life in harmony with society, Jonggyeok is life surrendering to flow, Hwagyeok is life finding new self through transformation. Which is superior? It depends on what life you find **meaningful**.

### Questions for Deeper Exploration

1. When Gyeokguk is destroyed, can it be restored?
2. What does Jonggyeok mean in modern society? (Is conforming to organizations a virtue?)
3. Is Gyeokguk theory determinism or a map of possibilities?

> Gyeokguk theory is an interpretive framework of traditional Saju; it is not a scientifically validated theory. Please read it as a reference for self-reflection, not as grounds for life decisions.
