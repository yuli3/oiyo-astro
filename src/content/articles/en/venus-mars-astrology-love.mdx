Many people look only at the Sun sign when they talk about love. Traditional Western astrology speaks of **Venus** as a symbol of how people show affection, and of **Mars** as a symbol of desire and energy. In controlled studies, birth charts and birth dates did not predict personality or events (Carlson, 1985; Hartmann et al., 2006).

## Venus — The Planet of Love

Traditional Western astrology talks about Venus as a symbol of **how people show affection**. Talk of beauty, and of the relationship someone wants, is told inside that symbol.

You'll need your birth chart to find your Venus sign (free tools like Astro.com can calculate it for you).

## How traditional texts talk about Venus–Mars element pairs

### The basic story

**Reading Venus and Mars together**: older texts tell a story about one person's Mars and another person's Venus when they share an element or sit on an opposite axis. That story is not a match score, and it is not a measurement of strong attraction.

**Placements the texts often mention**:
- Person A's Venus in Sagittarius + Person B's Mars in Aries → both fire. Traditional texts tell this pair as a fire story.
- Person A's Venus in Taurus + Person B's Mars in Scorpio → an opposite axis. Traditional texts write this axis together with tension. That does not mean strong attraction was measured.

### What traditional texts say about element pairs

| Venus Element | Mars Element | What traditional texts say |
|---------|---------|------|
| Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) | Fire | Passionate, but egos can clash |
| Fire | Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) | Air feeds fire — a natural harmony |
| Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) | Earth | Stable, but can lack change |
| Earth | Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) | Water enriches earth — emotionally grounding |
| Air | Air | Intellectually connected, but can lack feeling |
| Air | Fire | A lively combination of ideas and energy |
| Water | Water | Deep emotional connection, but can lack practicality |
| Water | Earth | Emotion grounded by practicality — a stabilizing mix |

## An example read through traditional texts

**Person A (Venus in Scorpio, Mars in Aries) + Person B (Venus in Aries, Mars in Scorpio)**

→ Traditional texts note that A's Mars (Aries) and B's Venus (Aries) share a sign, and they write that as the same symbol.
→ Traditional texts note that B's Mars (Scorpio) and A's Venus (Scorpio) share a sign, and they write that as a symbol of deep connection.
→ The same texts also tell this combination as a story of tension or of who leads. They do not state how strong the attraction is, or treat the pair as a fixed picture of a couple who fight.
