## What this page does not do

Hippocrates is often named at the start of Western medicine. Astrology books put two different sentences in his mouth: that a physician who does not know astrology is no physician, and that an astrologer who does not know medicine cannot be a physician. Those lines contradict each other, and neither is a verified sentence from the Hippocratic corpus. This page does not call him an astrologer.

Medical astrology is a tradition that treated the body as a small copy of a sky chart. Signs were given regions. Planets were given themes of function. It does not diagnose, name the day you will fall ill, or replace medicine. If the body hurts, see a clinician.

## The zodiac man: old region assignments

The sequence from the head (Aries) to the feet (Pisces) is a common figure in medical-astrology images from the Middle Ages onward. The list below is regions only. Disease names are left out. A sign does not mean you have the illness older manuals attached to it.

1. **Aries:** head, face
2. **Taurus:** neck, voice
3. **Gemini:** shoulders, arms, hands, the place of the lungs
4. **Cancer:** chest, stomach
5. **Leo:** heart, back
6. **Virgo:** belly, the place of the intestines
7. **Libra:** lower back, the place of the kidneys
8. **Scorpio:** the places of excretion and generation
9. **Sagittarius:** hips, thighs
10. **Capricorn:** knees, bones, teeth
11. **Aquarius:** calves, ankles
12. **Pisces:** feet

## Themes the planets carried

If signs were regions, planets were themes of function. Do not read them as causes. This page does not say Venus makes diabetes, Jupiter makes tumors, or Pluto makes cancer.

- **Sun:** vitality, the theme of the heartbeat
- **Moon:** fluids, rhythm, a monthly cycle
- **Mercury:** nerve signals, breath, the hands
- **Venus:** veins, a taste for sweetness
- **Mars:** muscle, heat, inflammation as a theme
- **Jupiter:** the liver, growth, excess as a theme
- **Saturn:** bone, teeth, skin, hardening as a theme
- **Uranus:** sudden change as a theme
- **Neptune:** states with a blurred edge
- **Pluto:** ending and beginning again

## Houses and transits

Traditional texts give the 6th house to illness and labor, and the 8th to crisis and transformation. This page does not say the 6th is the house of curable disease or the 8th is the house of death.

It also does not say that a Saturn square or opposition to the Sun or Moon drops vitality and guarantees a breakdown, or that Jupiter makes medicine work. A transit is how the tradition talked about timing. It is not a schedule of illness.

## Elements are not prescriptions

Hot, cold, dry, and moist are Galenic words that later sat beside the four elements. This page does not prescribe cold food for a fiery chart, or a tea, or a bath, as treatment.

- **Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):** drawn as a hot, dry temperament
- **Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn):** drawn as a cold, dry temperament
- **Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius):** drawn as a hot, moist temperament
- **Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces):** drawn as a cold, moist temperament

Those four lines are old temperament language. They are not a diet.

## Closing

This page does not explain a sore back as Libra screaming, or poor digestion as Cancer's feelings stuck in the stomach. Medical astrology is a symbol of how bodies were drawn onto a sky chart. A symptom is a reason to see a clinician.
