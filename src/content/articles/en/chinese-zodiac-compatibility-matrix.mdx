Put the twelve animals on both axes and you get 12×12, or 144 cells. Leave out the same-animal cells and the mirrored ones, and 66 distinct pairs of two different animals remain. This page writes into each cell the name of the earthly-branch relation used in traditional Chinese metaphysics.

<Callout type="note">
**Before you read**: triad, six-harmony, clash and harm are names from an old traditional system. There is no scientific evidence that two people's birth years decide whether a relationship goes well. For that reason this page gives no compatibility scores or star ratings. It shows only the names of the relations.
</Callout>

## The four relation names

| Name | Meaning in the tradition | Pairs |
|------|------|------|
| Triad (三合) | Three branches gather into one element frame | 申子辰 (water) · 亥卯未 (wood) · 寅午戌 (fire) · 巳酉丑 (metal) |
| Six-harmony (六合) | Two branches pair up and combine | 子丑 · 寅亥 · 卯戌 · 辰酉 · 巳申 · 午未 |
| Clash (冲) | Opposite positions on the circle, six steps apart, collide | 子午 · 丑未 · 寅申 · 卯酉 · 辰戌 · 巳亥 |
| Harm (害) | A pair said to get in each other's way | 子未 · 丑午 · 寅巳 · 卯辰 · 申亥 · 酉戌 |

The tradition also has other names, such as break (破) and punishment (刑). This chart uses only the four above.

## How to read the chart

- **It is not a ranking.** Triad and six-harmony are not a high score, and clash and harm are not a low one. They are simply the names the tradition has used.
- **Most cells have no name.** Each animal has a named relation with only 5 of the other 11 animals (2 triad, 1 six-harmony, 1 clash, 1 harm). With the other 6 it has none of the relations in this chart.
- **Same-animal cells are left unnamed.** The tradition does have ideas such as self-punishment (自刑), but this chart does not cover them.
- **It looks at the year branch only.** Traditional saju compatibility is described as reading several pillars, the day pillar above all, rather than the year animal alone. We suggest you do not judge a relationship by the animal sign.

A real relationship is built by two people's conversations, respect and time. Please use this chart as a reading on how the tradition has named relationships.
