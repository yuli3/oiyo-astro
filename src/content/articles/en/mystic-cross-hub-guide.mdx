Seeing ‘independent’ in several descriptions does not confirm that it describes you accurately. Mystic Cross is reading material for comparing expressions and forming questions, not a validated combined assessment or prediction engine. Its comparison tables and examples are editorial material, not measurements.

## 1. Agreement is not independent evidence

Two accounts agreeing does not by itself establish greater reliability. They may share language or assumptions; agreement between descriptions differs from evidence checking actual behaviour. This is an editorial and methodological judgement, not a study calculating increased reliability.

Disagreement does not automatically identify which account is true either. Check meanings, inputs and interpretation rules first. Record behaviour that does not fit, and do not combine several outputs into one personality score.

## 2. Distinguish the kinds of evidence

| Framework | Layer discussed here | Boundary to respect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MBTI | A model describing preferences | Not ability, hiring suitability or relationship success |
| Saju day stem | Traditional interpretation of yin-yang, five elements and four birth pillars | Not a measured innate personality |
| Tarot | Symbolic reading using images as prompts | Not a test measuring your current psychological state |
| Astrology | Traditional meanings assigned to bodies and signs | Not validated personal traits or event causation |
| Attachment | Psychological concepts studying relationship anxiety and avoidance | Not a celestial classification or clinical diagnosis |

The MBTI Foundation's ethics code rejects using type to judge ability, intelligence or success, or to restrict people. Fraley's attachment explanation discusses anxiety and avoidance dimensions and self-report measurement. It does not establish attachment typing from the Moon or Venus.

The Academy of Korean Studies explains the four birth pillars and the traditional focus on the day stem. Cultural terminology is different from measuring an individual's temperament. Modern Jungian approaches also read tarot as a metaphor for individuation; this does not establish that Jung himself created a definitive 22-card sequence of stages.

## 4. A fictional example of reading

**Editorial example:** two accounts call you independent, but you regularly consult others before making important work decisions. Do not discard observations merely because a word appears twice.

1. Define independent: working alone, having decision authority or not asking for help?
2. Separate each account's inputs and interpretation rules.
3. Record whom you consulted and about what in one recent decision.
4. Keep both matching and non-matching behaviours.
5. Choose one concrete question for the next conversation: “Do I want decision authority and feedback too?”

This is a reflection example, not a procedure with measured effects on self-understanding or personality classification.

## 5. Record interpretations separately from observations

| Example observation | Interpretation to keep separate | What to check next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asked two colleagues before a meeting | Do I make every decision alone? | Which decisions involve whose input |
| Objected to a schedule change | Do I always dislike change? | Change itself versus an increased burden |
| Offered a solution during conflict | Did the other person want a solution? | Whether listening or suggestions would help |

This table is not measured data. Do not call an interpretation of fixed birth inputs a snapshot measuring your present state. Important career, relationship or health decisions require actual experience and circumstances, with appropriate professional help when needed.

## 6. Common questions

**Are three matching accounts more trustworthy?** The count alone proves neither accuracy nor independence. We provide no combined score or diagnosis.

**Can cards or signs identify attachment style?** This article offers no such method. Research concepts, self-report measurements and symbolic prompts differ.

**Are all chapters available in every language?** Not yet. The hub links existing local chapters and marks missing translations. [Traditional Western zodiac descriptions](/en/zodiac/personality/) and the Chinese zodiac are different topics.

## 7. Sources and verification boundaries

- [MBTI Foundation ethics](https://www.myersbriggs.org/using-type-as-a-professional/mbti-code-of-ethics/): official instrument use limits, not validation of an Oiyo combined model.
- [Fraley's adult attachment explanation](https://labs.psychology.illinois.edu/~rcfraley/attachment.htm): researcher-authored dimensions and measurement, not celestial correspondence.
- [Ken James course at the Jung Institute Chicago](https://jungchicago.org/product/james-tarot-individuation/): evidence of a modern Jungian tarot interpretation, not Jung's original text or an efficacy trial.
- [Academy of Korean Studies entry on saju](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0025957): traditional concepts, not validated innate traits or MBTI mappings.

Making questions more concrete is the series' editorial purpose. We do not claim that cross-reading has been shown to produce more accurate assessments.
