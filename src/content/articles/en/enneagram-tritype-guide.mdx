Beyond the basic nine types, **Tritype** identifies your dominant type in each of the three centers — Gut, Heart, and Head — to produce a richer, more precise personality profile. Proposed by Katherine Chernick Fauvre, the idea is widely used in advanced Enneagram study. Keep in mind that it is an extension with even less validation research behind it than the basic nine types.

## What Is Tritype?

The nine Enneagram types are organized into three Centers:

| Center | Types | Core Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| Gut (Body) Center | 8, 9, 1 | Anger |
| Heart Center | 2, 3, 4 | Shame |
| Head Center | 5, 6, 7 | Fear |

Your Tritype is a combination of three numbers — one from each center.

**Example**: Tritype 479
- From the Heart Center (2, 3, 4): **4** — listed first, so it is the primary type
- From the Head Center (5, 6, 7): **7**
- From the Gut Center (8, 9, 1): **9**

<Callout type="info">
**Tritype Order**: The first number listed is your primary type. 147, 471, and 714 share the same numbers but indicate different primary drives.
</Callout>

## How to Find Your Tritype

1. **Identify your primary type** through an Enneagram test
2. **Find your dominant type in each center**:
   - Gut Center (8, 9, 1): Which feels most like your core orientation?
   - Heart Center (2, 3, 4): Which resonates most deeply?
   - Head Center (5, 6, 7): Which describes your primary mental pattern?
3. **Read the combination**: Your three numbers together form your Tritype
