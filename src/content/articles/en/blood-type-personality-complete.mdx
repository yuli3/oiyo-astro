## A blood type image is not a person’s characteristics

Hearing “type A is meticulous” or “type O is a leader” may remind you of yourself or someone you know. This article treats those expressions as examples of cultural stereotypes, not validated personality findings, career aptitude lists or relationship scores.

Research findings are not all identical. Do not dismiss a small significant difference, but do not use it to sort individuals into four personalities either. Separate biological classification, cultural descriptions and actual behaviour.

## ABO is a biological classification, not a personality scale

[NCBI’s ABO textbook](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK2267/?report=printable) explains the carbohydrate structures of A and B antigen determinants and the glycoproteins and glycolipids carrying them. Calling ABO simply “types of antigen proteins” is inaccurate. Type O lacks A and B determinants but has the H precursor. It does not lack every antigen.

The text explains ABO biology, not scores of diligence, loyalty or creativity. Biological importance does not validate career or romantic personality charts. Conversely, limitations of personality charts do not invalidate all medical research involving ABO.

## Small research differences are not personal predictions

[Nawata’s official 2014 article page](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjpsy/85/2/85_85.13016/_article) and [official PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjpsy/85/2/85_85.13016/_pdf/-char/en) report secondary analyses of three Japanese and US survey datasets. The original respondent total was 11,729, but analysed numbers differ by item. Of 68 everyday attitude items, 65 showed no significant differences; the table’s largest η² rounds to .0030, about 0.3% or less. This does not mean every standardized personality trait was measured or every difference was exactly zero.

[Tsuchimine and colleagues’ 2015 original](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0126983) recruited 1,572 and analysed 1,427 with complete data. Medical students and hospital staff completed the TCI self-report and underwent genetic testing. The study reported small significant differences in Persistence: partial η² was .010 for genotype and .006 for phenotype. “There was no association at all” is an inaccurate summary. The specific sample, uncontrolled environmental factors and self-report limits also prevent concluding an individual’s persistence or job ability.

[Rogers and Glendon’s official 2003 abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0191886902001010) distinguishes 360 recruited from 240 analysed and reports that predicted blood type personality differences were not confirmed on Big Five and LOT-R measures. We checked official abstract search returns only; full text and effect size were not verified.

Read η² and partial η² with their measures and models. Comparing numbers alone cannot rank study accuracy or turn a group difference into a probability of correctly guessing a person’s character. Continue with [the methods, results and limits of blood type personality research](/en/fortune-blood-type-psychology/).

## Do not choose careers, partners or diets by blood type

Hiring and roles require actual task performance, experience, preferences and working conditions. Relationships require discussion of contact, private time, agreements and consent. People with the same type may not want a relationship, while different types can keep shared agreements. Star ratings or “best combinations” cannot replace this information.

[Wang and colleagues’ 2014 diet study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0084749) used cross-sectional data from 1,455 people and did not support extra benefits from matching diets to blood type. Associations between dietary patterns and health markers are not blood type matching effects. Cross-sectional findings establish neither causation nor personal dietary prescriptions, and do not deny every ABO–disease association. This article provides no blood type disease chart, diet or exercise prescription.

## Leave people a choice in conversation

If someone does not want to disclose their blood type, stop asking. “It is only fun” does not justify requiring disclosure or agreement with a type description. You cannot infer an unknown blood type from personality either.

For reflection, compare experiences such as when you prepared carefully and when you decided spontaneously, rather than saying “I am a typical A.” These questions are not validated scales or counselling procedures and do not establish the validity of blood type personality theory.
