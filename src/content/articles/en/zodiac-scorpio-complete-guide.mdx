In the tropical zodiac, Scorpio's usual stretch runs from about 23 October to 21 November. The boundary shifts a little from year to year. A birthday inside that window does not make someone Scorpio in every year. What follows is the portrait sun-sign pages usually give. It is not a measurement of personality from a birth date.

## Scorpio in brief

| Item | What the pages say |
|------|------|
| Usual range | About 23 October to 21 November. It shifts a little by year |
| Element | Water |
| Modality | Fixed |
| Ruler | Traditional ruler: Mars. Modern astrology added Pluto. Not an ancient co-rulership |
| Symbol | The scorpion ♏. The phoenix is a modern image, not a classical symbol |
| Colors | Deep red, black, and dark purple are a modern popular list |
| Numbers | 8, 11, 18, and 22 are a modern popular list |
| Body | Reproductive system, bladder, and nose. Traditional medical-astrology correspondences, not health advice |
| House | Modern astrology links Scorpio with the 8th house: transformation, shared resources, and sex |

Fixed water, in this vocabulary, means feeling held in one place for a long time. That is why Scorpio pages talk about depth, focus, and a temper that does not cool quickly.

## Traits the portrait repeats

Sun-sign writing paints Scorpio as deep and intense. The lines below are that portrait. They are not a claim that everyone born near these dates lives this way.

### Often listed on the bright side

**Depth.** The pages prefer motive and feeling under the sentence, and they call small talk thin.

**Commitment.** Once trust is given, the portrait says it stays. That is not true of every person, or of every relationship.

**A reading eye.** The tradition says this sign notices feeling under the words. A hunch can be a clue. It is not proof, and it is not a gift for catching lies.

**Coming back changed.** Hard seasons are said to alter the stance afterward. The phoenix belongs to that modern story. It is not a classical symbol.

**A long decision.** The portrait says a chosen aim is not dropped lightly. That is a description, not a measured law of this sign.

### Often listed on the shadow side

**Control.** Many pages say uncertainty in a bond turns into holding the other person too tightly.

**Anger that stays.** Hurt is said to be put down slowly. Revenge is not the nature of the sign.

**Jealousy.** The same closeness is said to bring fear of loss.

**Secrecy.** Other people's secrets are kept, while one's own interior stays shut. Pages call that a wall in close relationships.

## Love

Scorpio love, in this portrait, wants deep trust more than a light meeting. The usual story is watchfulness first, and more openness after trust. Betrayal is said to be remembered for a long time. That is not a rule for every relationship.

What the pages say this sign wants: frankness, steadiness, and a link of inner life rather than talk alone. What they call difficult: performance, lies, and attempts to steer the other person.

## What compatibility pages often say

There is no ranking and no score. Popular astrology often mentions Cancer, Pisces, and Virgo beside Scorpio, and it calls Leo and Aquarius harder. The list does not decide a relationship between two people.

## Mars and Pluto

The traditional ruler is Mars. Modern astrology added Pluto. The two were not an ancient co-rulership.

Mars, in this telling, is force held and applied. Pages often contrast Aries, where Mars shows quickly, with Scorpio, where Mars is said to wait. That contrast is a modern gloss.

Transformation, endings and beginnings, and hidden power are modern meanings attached to Pluto. The phoenix is a symbol of that modern story, not a classical one.
