The moon is the closest celestial body to Earth. The rise and fall of the ocean come mainly from the moon's gravity. The next claim does not follow: that the body contains a lot of water, so the moon pulls people the way it pulls the tides. Tides appear in large seas, and it is also inaccurate to put an adult's body water at 70 percent.

Some farming calendars and old medical calendars matched sowing or bloodletting to the moon's phase. That is the history of a custom. It is not a reason to choose today's surgery or clinic dates by the moon.

## One round is about 29.5 days

The synodic month, the return of the moon to the same phase, is about **29.5 days**. Across that span the shape is called by eight names. The waxing side runs from new moon to full moon. The waning side runs from full moon to the next new moon.

The line that one starts while the moon grows and finishes while it shrinks is a **modern intention calendar**. It is not a measurement that energy actually rose and then fell. It sits on a different layer from the moon sign in astrology.

## The eight phases

### 1. New moon

The moon is almost invisible. In the modern ritual this is the slot for a seed and a start. Some lists also attach the colors black and silver.

What people do:

- They write the **actions to take** this month. Visualization that only pictures the outcome as if it had already happened is not recommended here as a method.
- They put the first step of a new task on the calendar.
- They sit quietly and set the scope of the month.

Lighting a candle and writing five wishes is a custom. Declaring the sentences does not bring the thing closer.

### 2. Waxing crescent

The right side brightens in a thin curve. This slot is for splitting the actions written at the new moon into small plans.

### 3. First quarter

Half of the moon is bright. This slot is for checking what is stuck and choosing the next action. An energy sentence about defeating obstacles is a slogan.

### 4. Between the first quarter and the full moon

Just before the full moon, the disk is a swelling convex shape. This slot is for reading the plan again and changing only the details.

### 5. Full moon

The moon is round. In the modern ritual this is the slot for looking back on the work so far and writing down what one is thankful for. It is not a measurement that emotions grow in a scientific sense, or that dreams and intuition grow stronger. Setting crystals in the moonlight is a custom, and it is not the best day for a cleansing.

Some people write on paper a matter they want to set down. If you burn the paper, use a fireproof container, clear the area, and watch until the end. Tearing the paper without burning it is the same custom. This page does not follow the claim that a person becomes free after the burning.

### 6. Between the full moon and the last quarter

The disk is a shrinking convex shape. This slot is for sorting what one is thankful for, and objects one will no longer keep.

### 7. Last quarter

Half of the moon is bright again. This slot is for giving a name to a feeling that was postponed, or for cleaning the house. Forgiveness is not completed in this phase.

### 8. Waning crescent

Just before the next new moon, a thin moon remains. This slot is for rest. You may write the next synodic month's actions ahead of time, or leave the space empty.

## Full moons and the names of the signs

This is a modern list that attaches a theme according to which sign the full moon stands near. It is not that the energy of that sign is strengthened.

| Name | Theme often attached |
|------|----------------|
| Aries | beginnings, a personal schedule |
| Taurus | money, the senses, possessions |
| Gemini | speech, learning |
| Cancer | home, family |
| Leo | expression, play |
| Virgo | daily life, tidying |
| Libra | a partner, an agreement |
| Scorpio | secrets, what is shared |
| Sagittarius | distant plans, belief |
| Capricorn | work, structure |

This page does not read a Virgo full moon as a day of health cleansing. The body's affairs belong to medical care.

## A modern table that ties in the chakras

The table below pairs moon phases with chakras at a later date. It is not Ptolemy's division. Chakras are metaphors of the mind.

| Phase | Metaphor for the mind | What people do |
|------|------------------|
| New moon | the root, noticing the ground under the feet | sit and steady the breath |
| Full moon | a wide view, words of intuition | look back over the day |
| Last quarter | the chest, words about a relationship | write down what was left |

If you mark the new-moon and full-moon dates on a calendar and write only the actions for those days, the calendar is already usable. There is no promise that three months of it will change the rhythm of a life.
