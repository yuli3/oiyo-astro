## The twelve signs

The **zodiac** is the name for dividing the path the Sun travels in a year into twelve sectors. The system took shape in Mesopotamia and in the Hellenistic age, and it is still the best-known language of astrology.

In Western astrology, a sign joins a sector of the sky to a story of character and of life. Controlled studies did not predict personality or events from a birth date. It reads better as a cultural tradition and as a tool of self-description. The portraits below are not an inborn quality of a person born in that sector.

The twelve signs are grouped by **four elements** and **three modalities**. Cardinal signs begin, fixed signs stay, and mutable signs finish and move on.

| Element | Signs | Words that often appear |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Beginnings, expression, far places |
| Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | The senses, tending, structure |
| Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Speech, relationship, thought |
| Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Feeling, depth, boundaries |

Dates are the **spans often printed** for the tropical zodiac. The moment the Sun enters a sign shifts a little each year. If you were born near a boundary, it is better to look at that year's chart.

Colors, weekdays, numbers, and gems are a **modern popular list**, not measurements from a classical textbook. From one article on the same sign to another, the numbers sometimes differ.

## Aries

About 21 March to about 19 April. The traditional ruler is Mars. The element is fire. The modality is cardinal.

As the first of the twelve sectors, it draws beginnings and a forward rush. Words written as strengths are courage, frankness, and independence. Words written as weaknesses are haste, short patience, and a picture of growing bored easily.

Signs often written beside it are Leo and Sagittarius, of the same fire, and Gemini and Aquarius, of air.

A modern popular list puts up red, Tuesday, 9, and diamond.

## Taurus

About 20 April to about 20 May. Venus, earth, fixed. Venus is the traditional ruler.

It draws the senses, possession, and a slow strength that goes through to the end. Persistence and practicality are written as strengths. Stubbornness, refusal of change, and a mind that wants to hold on are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Virgo, Capricorn, Cancer, and Pisces.

A modern popular list puts up green, Friday, 6, and emerald.

## Gemini

About 21 May to about 20 June. Mercury, air, mutable. Mercury is the traditional ruler.

It draws speech, curiosity, and many interests. Flexibility and wit are written as strengths. Scattered attention and shallow ties are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Libra, Aquarius, Aries, and Leo.

A modern popular list puts up yellow, Wednesday, 5, and agate.

## Cancer

About 21 June to about 22 July. The Moon, water, cardinal. The Moon is the traditional ruler.

It draws home, care, and memory. Empathy and devotion are written as strengths. Swings of feeling, and holding on, are written as weaknesses. A large change of mood is not an illness.

Signs often written beside it are Scorpio, Pisces, Taurus, and Virgo.

A modern popular list puts up white and silver, Monday, 2, and pearl.

## Leo

About 23 July to about 22 August. The Sun, fire, fixed. The Sun is the traditional ruler.

It draws the stage, generosity, and a wish to be recognized. Expression and loyalty are written as strengths. Display, and a picture of being sensitive to criticism, are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Aries, Sagittarius, Gemini, and Libra.

A modern popular list puts up gold, Sunday, 1 and 5, and ruby.

## Virgo

About 23 August to about 22 September. Mercury, earth, mutable. Mercury is the traditional ruler.

It draws a picture of sorting, mending, and looking at use. Analysis and care with detail are written as strengths. Too much pointing-out, and worry, are written as weaknesses. Traditional medical astrology connects Virgo and the 6th house with daily life and with routines of the body. It is not a method of health care, and it is not a sign that recommends a checkup.

Signs often written beside it are Taurus, Capricorn, Cancer, and Scorpio.

A modern popular list puts up brown and beige, Wednesday, 6, and sapphire.

## Libra

About 23 September to about 22 October. Venus, air, cardinal. Venus is the traditional ruler.

It draws balance, a pair, and shape. Mediation and a sense of form are written as strengths. A picture of putting decisions off, and a mind that tries to avoid conflict, are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Gemini, Aquarius, Aries, and Leo.

A modern popular list puts up pale blue and pink, Friday, 6 and 9, and opal.

## Scorpio

About 23 October to about 21 November. The traditional ruler is Mars. Modern lists add Pluto. Water, fixed.

It draws depth, secrets, and a focus gathered in one place. Insight and loyalty are written as strengths. Suspicion, control, and secrecy are written as weaknesses. Vengefulness is a popular portrait, not the quality of that sector.

Signs often written beside it are Cancer, Pisces, Taurus, and Virgo.

A modern popular list puts up black and red, Tuesday, 8, and topaz.

## Sagittarius

About 22 November to about 21 December. Jupiter, fire, mutable. Jupiter is the traditional ruler.

It draws far places, belief, and frank speech. Optimism and adventure are written as strengths. Exaggeration, and a picture of being unable to stay, are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Aries, Leo, Gemini, and Libra.

A modern popular list puts up purple and blue-green, Thursday, 3, and turquoise.

## Capricorn

About 22 December to about 19 January. Saturn, earth, cardinal. Saturn is the traditional ruler.

It draws long aims, self-discipline, and responsibility. Patience and trustworthiness are written as strengths. A picture of speaking about feeling late, and a mind that looks only at work, are written as weaknesses. The sentence that one grows stronger as time passes is a popular sentence.

Signs often written beside it are Taurus, Virgo, Cancer, and Scorpio.

A modern popular list puts up deep green and brown, Saturday, 8, and garnet.

## Aquarius

About 20 January to about 18 February. The traditional ruler is Saturn. Modern lists add Uranus. Air, fixed.

It draws thought kept at a distance, the group, and a picture of doubting the existing grain. Independence and the spark of an idea are written as strengths. Emotional distance, and an individualism that is stubborn, are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Gemini, Libra, Aries, and Leo.

A modern popular list puts up a vivid blue, Saturday, 4 and 11, and amethyst.

## Pisces

About 19 February to about 20 March. The traditional ruler is Jupiter. Modern lists add Neptune. Water, mutable.

As the last of the twelve sectors, it draws empathy with a blurred boundary, and imagination. The claim that it holds the energy of every sign is modern rhetoric. Creativity and tolerance are written as strengths. A picture of wanting to slip out of practical life, and a mind that draws a boundary late, are written as weaknesses.

Signs often written beside it are Cancer, Scorpio, Taurus, and Virgo.

A modern popular list puts up the color of the sea and lavender, Thursday, 7 and 12, and aquamarine.

The pairs named above are the same element, and pairs such as fire with air and earth with water, which tradition writes as helping one another. That is not a ranking of best matches.
