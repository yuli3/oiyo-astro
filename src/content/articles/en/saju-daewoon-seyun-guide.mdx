## What Is "Luck" (運) in Saju?

If the Four Pillars (四柱八字) are **the basic blueprint set at birth**, then **luck cycles (運) are the energy of time flowing over that blueprint**.

In Saju (Korean Four Pillars astrology), the same chart is read differently depending on which luck cycle arrives at which time. The tradition holds that even a favorable chart can meet difficulties in an unsuitable period, and a challenging chart can flourish in a well-matched one.

Luck is read on three levels:

| Cycle | Length | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Daewoon (大運)** | 10 years | The big current of life, a decade-long energy |
| **Seyun (歲運)** | 1 year | The fortune of a given year |
| **Wolun (月運)** | 1 month | Finer, month-by-month fortune |

---

## Daewoon (大運): The Great Ten-Year Waves

### What is Daewoon?

Daewoon is **the broad current of life that changes every ten years**. It is calculated from the month pillar (月柱), moving either forward or backward through the sixty-pillar cycle.

- **Men born in a yang year, women born in a yin year**: move forward to the pillars after the month pillar
- **Men born in a yin year, women born in a yang year**: move backward to the pillars before the month pillar

Whether a year is yang or yin depends on the heavenly stem of the birth year: yang stems are 甲·丙·戊·庚·壬, yin stems are 乙·丁·己·辛·癸.

<Callout type="note">
**Calculating the starting age of Daewoon**: If the cycle moves forward, count the days from the birth date to the next solar term (節入); if it moves backward, count the days from the previous solar term to the birth date. Days ÷ 3 = starting age (the traditional rule treats 3 days as 1 year).
Example: 15 days → the first Daewoon begins around age 5
</Callout>

### Principles for interpreting Daewoon

One traditional view reads the stem and branch of a Daewoon separately:
- **Daewoon heavenly stem**: thought to govern mainly the first 5 years (psychological and interpersonal change)
- **Daewoon earthly branch**: thought to govern mainly the last 5 years (environmental and material change)

Other schools, however, read the stem and branch together across all ten years, or give more weight to the branch because it carries the season.

### How a Daewoon relates to you

| Daewoon element | Relation to your Day Master | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Resource (印星) | Element that produces the Day Master | Recharging, a time of support |
| Companion (比劫) | Same element | Peers and rivalry, energy spread out |
| Output (食傷) | Element the Day Master produces | A time of expression, creation and activity |
| Wealth (財星) | Element the Day Master controls | A time of money and tangible results |
| Officer (官星) | Element that controls the Day Master | A time of tests, pressure and growth |

### Applying Daewoon in practice

**Signs of a favorable Daewoon:**
- When the Daewoon carries your Useful God (用神) element
- When an element arrives that balances your chart
- A Resource Daewoon that strengthens the Day Master

**Signs of a difficult Daewoon:**
- When the Daewoon carries your Unfavorable God (忌神) element
- When many punishments (刑), clashes (沖), breaks (破) and harms (害) occur
- When an already excessive element grows even stronger

## Seyun (歲運): The Flow of a Given Year

### What is Seyun?

Seyun is determined by **that year's pillar in the sixty-pillar cycle**. 2026 is the year of **Byeong-o (丙午)**, so the Byeong-o energy interacts with everyone's chart.

Seyun rides on top of the Daewoon. If **Daewoon is the current of a great river**, **Seyun is the wind blowing across it**.

### How to read Seyun

1. **Relation between the Seyun stem and your Day Master**: combination, clash, production or control?
2. **Relation between the Seyun branch and your day branch**: is there a clash, punishment or combination?
3. **How Seyun works with Daewoon**: does it reinforce the Daewoon or collide with it?

| Seyun relation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Seyun stem produces (生) the Day Master | A year when energy, support and connections come in |
| Seyun stem controls (剋) the Day Master | A year full of tests, pressure and change |
| Seyun branch clashes (沖) with the day branch | Moving house, changing jobs, shifts in relationships |
| Seyun combines (合) with Daewoon | Luck concentrates, bringing major change |
| Seyun clashes (沖) with Daewoon | The Daewoon's flow is disrupted |

### Six Combinations and Three Harmonies in Seyun

When a branch in your natal chart forms a **combination (合)** with the Seyun branch, the related connections or opportunities are said to become active that year.

**Three Harmonies (三合):**
- Shin-Ja-Jin (申子辰) Water frame: Water (水) energy at its peak
- In-O-Sul (寅午戌) Fire frame: Fire (火) energy at its peak
- Hae-Myo-Mi (亥卯未) Wood frame: Wood (木) energy at its peak
- Sa-Yu-Chuk (巳酉丑) Metal frame: Metal (金) energy at its peak

**Six Combinations (六合):** 子丑 · 寅亥 · 卯戌 · 辰酉 · 巳申 · 午未

## Wolun (月運): Fine-Grained Monthly Fortune

### What is Wolun?

Wolun looks at how **that month's pillar in the sixty-pillar cycle** interacts with your chart. It is a more detailed reading than Seyun.

**How to use Wolun**: to see when an important decision (a job, an investment, a move, a marriage) might be best timed.

### Judging the strength of a month

| Month's energy | Relation to your chart | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Useful God month (用神月) | The energy you need | Best time to act boldly |
| Favorable God month (喜神月) | Energy that supports the Useful God | Push forward strategically |
| Idle God month (閑神月) | Neutral energy | Carry on as usual |
| Unfavorable God month (忌神月) | Energy that works against you | Be careful and defensive |
| Enemy God month (仇神月) | The least favorable energy | Postpone big decisions, keep things stable |

---

## How Daewoon, Seyun and Wolun Work Together

In Saju, a time when all three cycles are favorable is read as a **turning point**, and a time when none of them suit you is read as **a period calling for the most caution**.

### The best combination

```
✅ Good Daewoon + good Seyun + good Wolun
→ A major opportunity. Time to decide boldly and move.
```

### Periods that call for caution

```
⚠️ Difficult Daewoon + difficult Seyun
→ Put off big decisions and stay defensive. Look after your health and finances.
```

### Mixed periods

```
🔄 Good Daewoon + difficult Seyun
→ The overall direction is good, but watch out for specific events that year.
   Keep a long-term view and ride out temporary difficulties.
```

## Worked Example: A Gap Wood (甲木) Day Master

**Assumption**: Gap Wood Day Master, currently in a Gyeong Metal (庚金) Daewoon

**Daewoon analysis (Gyeong Metal Daewoon)**:
- Gyeong Metal controls (剋) Gap Wood (Metal controls Wood)
- A period of major tests and pressure
- Old ways are challenged and change is pushed on you
- Yet this is also where the greatest growth happens

**2026 Byeong-o (丙午) Seyun analysis**:
- Byeong Fire (丙火) is the Eating God (食神) of Gap Wood, and through Fire controlling Metal (火剋金) it restrains Gyeong Metal → it can be read as a year that eases the Daewoon's pressure
- Also check whether the Seyun branch O (午) clashes or combines with the natal day branch (e.g. the 子午 clash or the 午未 combination)

**Practical advice**:
- During the Gyeong Metal Daewoon, focus on strengthening your foundations rather than new ventures
- Assuming this chart's Useful God is Water (水) and Wood (木), carry out important decisions in months of those elements
- If you have concerns about your health, please consult a medical professional rather than relying on fortune readings

<Callout type="tip">
**The key to using fortune readings**: The real value of studying Saju is not avoiding bad luck, but knowing what kind of cycle you are in and preparing accordingly.
</Callout>
