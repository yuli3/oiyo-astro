## Ninth in a folktale

In the widely told zodiac race folktale, the monkey is the **ninth animal**. The best-known monkey in East Asia is probably Sun Wukong from *Journey to the West*: full of tricks and talent, and unwilling to be tamed. These are stories about literature and symbols. They are not a measured personality of people born in a monkey year.

## What the tradition assigns

The rows below are traditional assignments. They are not a personality score.

| Item | Traditional assignment |
|---|---|
| Earthly branch | Shen (申) |
| Element and yin-yang | Yang metal |
| Direction | West-southwest on the twelve-branch compass; west in the seasonal grouping (申酉戌) |
| Time | The Shen double-hour, 15:00–17:00 |

White, blue, and gold, the numbers 4 and 9, and the chrysanthemum show up often on modern popular lists. They are not values fixed by the old texts.

## When a monkey year begins

A zodiac year does not change on 1 January. In everyday use the animal usually changes at **Lunar New Year**, while saju (Four Pillars) practice uses **Lichun, the start of spring (usually around 4 February)** as the boundary. So someone born in January 1980 is a goat (己未) under both conventions, not a monkey. In 1980 Lichun fell on 5 February and Lunar New Year on 16 February, so someone born between those dates has the year pillar 庚申 in saju but is a goat by the Lunar New Year count. The answer can depend on which convention you follow.

## Year pillars

The heavenly stem is the element name the sexagenary cycle gives that year. The branch stays Shen; only the stem changes. This page does not read personality from that name. The same stem and branch are also the notation saju uses for the year pillar. That notation is not evidence of character.

| 1944 | 甲申 | wood |

There is no data showing that monkeys born in Geng-Shen (庚申), where metal appears twice, are sharper or more decisive. The tradition itself does not rank any of these years as 「the strongest monkey」.

## The portrait the tradition tells

The tradition often draws a monkey year as someone quick-minded, who lightens a room with wit and humour, who is curious about new things and adapts fast to unfamiliar situations. On the other side come the complaints: attention scattered in many directions, cleverness that can look like scheming, and a habit of promising more than they can keep. All of that is a traditional portrait. It is not a law for people born in those years.

## Love and relationships

The tradition describes monkey-year love as playful and never dull. They make their partner laugh and care a great deal about the spark of good conversation. The matching warning is that they want freedom and may feel stifled if they feel tied down. That is neither a diagnosis nor a destiny. As a wished-for partner, the tradition names someone who gets the joke and respects each person's own time. That, too, is the tradition's story.

## Names of traditional relations

This page does not use star ratings or the language of destiny. It keeps the names.

| Name | Pair | Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Triad (三合) | Monkey, rat, dragon | 申子辰 |
| Six-harmony (六合) | Monkey and snake | 巳申 |
| Clash (冲) | Monkey and tiger | 寅申 |
| Harm (害) | Monkey and pig | 申亥 |

Triad and six-harmony are not a higher score, and clash and harm are not a lower one. They are the names this scheme has used.

## Work

The tradition often links the monkey with work that needs quick reflexes and a way with words, work that solves problems by finding a new method, and work that shows a talent in front of people. That is not proof that those jobs fit best, and this page has no data showing that any sign is more common in any profession.

## Names kept only as birth years

Only the birth year is written. It is not used as evidence of personality. Leonardo da Vinci was born in 1452, Tom Hanks in 1956, and Hugh Jackman in 1968.

## 2026

2026 is Bing-Wu (丙午). Shen (申) and Wu (午) do not appear together in any of the triad, six-harmony, clash, or harm pairs above. This page does not forecast the year.
