Enneagram Type 6 is known as **The Loyalist** or **The Guardian**. They are vigilant about uncertainty and potential danger, seeking safety in trustworthy people and reliable systems. Their governing conviction: *"The world is dangerous. I must rely on what I can trust."*

## Type 6 Core Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|------|------|
| Type Name | The Loyalist / The Guardian |
| Basic Desire | Security, support, and certainty |
| Basic Fear | Being abandoned without support |
| Core Motivation | Securing safety and building trusted systems |
| Center | Thinking (Head) Center |
| Worldview | "I don't know where danger will come from — I must always stay alert" |

<Callout type="info">
**Two Patterns of Type 6**:
- **Phobic 6**: When danger is sensed, they flee or avoid. Appears quieter and more compliant.
- **Counter-Phobic 6**: Confronts fear head-on. Manifests as extreme sports or provocative behavior. Can look like a Type 8 on the surface.
</Callout>

## Personality Traits

### Strengths

**Loyalty and Trustworthiness**: Once they trust someone or an organization, their loyalty is absolute. The very definition of reliability.

**Responsibility**: Sees every task through to the end. The most dependable member of any team.

**Risk Anticipation**: Spots potential problems before they arise. An exceptional risk manager.

**Humor**: Uses humor to relieve stress — a trademark self-deprecating wit that draws people in.

**Community Building**: Exceptional at connecting and uniting people within a group.

### Weaknesses

**Excessive Worry**: Burns energy by worrying about things that haven't happened yet.

**Ambivalence toward Authority**: Simultaneously needs authority and distrusts it.

**Suspicion**: Frequently questions others' motives; trust takes a long time to build.

**Decision Paralysis**: Fear of making the wrong choice leads to procrastination.

## Wing Types

### 6w5 (Five Wing)

- More introverted and analytical
- Manages anxiety through knowledge
- Pursues academic expertise
- Quiet and observational

### 6w7 (Seven Wing)

- More extroverted and sociable
- Manages anxiety through humor and optimism
- Balances fun and responsibility
- Energetic and personable

| State | Direction | Characteristics |

| Under Stress | → Type 3 (Achiever) | Becomes competitive and preoccupied with image |
| Feeling Secure | → Type 9 (Peacemaker) | Relaxed, trusting, and at ease |

## Relationship Patterns

### Relationship Strengths

- Once trust is given, becomes the best possible partner
- Strong protective instinct for their partner
- Will face any hardship together

### Relationship Challenges

- Frequently tests a partner's loyalty
- When anxious, may over-verify or become suspicious
- Very slow to build trust in the early stages

### Ideal Partner

Someone who gives Type 6 consistent, reliable reassurance. Partners who are unpredictable or unstable push Type 6 into extreme anxiety.

## Growth Guide for Type 6

1. **Trust your own inner wisdom**: Practice believing your own judgment more than external authority.
2. **Distinguish worry from real danger**: in one study, 91% of the worries people with generalized anxiety disorder wrote down never came true (LaFreniere & Newman, 2020).
3. **Make friends with uncertainty**: Accepting that not everything can be controlled is growth.
4. **The present moment is safe**: Focus on the peace of right now rather than future dangers.
