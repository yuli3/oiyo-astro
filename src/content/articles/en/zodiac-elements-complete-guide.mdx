Western astrology has long divided the twelve signs into four groups: fire, earth, air, and water. This is a different tradition from MBTI, which divides into sixteen types. This page does not match the two, and it does not say the elements are a more accurate classification than the sun sign. What follows is the temperament that sun-sign writing has long drawn. It is not a result of measuring personality or a job from the day of birth.

## The four elements

| Element | Signs | Words that often appear |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | beginning, expression, far places |
| Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | sensation, tending, structure |
| Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | speech, relation, ideas |
| Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | feeling, depth, seeping in |

Even inside the same element, the modality differs. Cardinal opens the door. Fixed stays in one place. Mutable moves toward the end. The metaphors of flame, ground, wind, and water below are pictures used in modern commentary.

## Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius

Writing on fire records heat, brightness, and movement that goes outward.

- Aries, cardinal. It writes beginning and challenge, like a new flame.
- Leo, fixed. It writes the center, expression, and the stage.
- Sagittarius, mutable. It writes fire that goes far, and travel and belief.

Love writing often records quick expression, a picture of not bearing boredom, and heat that draws the other person. The line that freedom is needed is also repeated. This does not mean that a person born in that span is always like this.

## Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn

Writing on earth records what remains in the hand, time, and the body.

- Taurus, fixed. It writes sensation, possession, and slow trust.
- Virgo, mutable. It writes a picture of mending, sorting, and seeing use.
- Capricorn, cardinal. It writes structure, responsibility, and a long aim.

Love writing often records trust built slowly, sensation such as food and touch, and a rhythm you can predict.

## Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius

Writing on air records speech, comparison, and the space between people.

- Gemini, mutable. It writes several subjects and quick speech.
- Libra, cardinal. It writes the pair, balance, and form.
- Aquarius, fixed. It writes thought kept at a distance, and the group.

Love writing often records conversation, a distance like that of a friend, and time to be alone.

## Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Writing on water records atmosphere, memory, and a feeling that does not show easily.

- Cancer, cardinal. It writes home, care, and the tide coming in and going out.
- Scorpio, fixed. It writes depth, secrecy, and transformation.
- Pisces, mutable. It writes empathy with blurred boundaries.

Love writing often records atmosphere more than words, a close feeling, and a picture of seeping in easily.

## Pairs often written together

Popular writing often sets fire with air, and earth with water, as pairs that help each other. It also often groups the same element together. Fire with water, and earth with air, are more often written as a different grain. This is the tradition of grouping elements since Ptolemy, and the popular commentary that followed. It is not a compatibility ranking by stars or by scores, and it does not measure who fits whom.

If a chart looks as if it has many planets of one element, traditional interpretation reads that element's words as sounding loud. A lot of fire does not mean burnout arrives. A lot of water does not mean an emotional illness appears. If the body has symptoms, it is better to get medical care. The saying that a partner fills a lacking element is also a metaphor of the same tradition.
