Numerology is a tradition that treats each number as having its own character and links that character to patterns in life. Its best-known value is the **Life Path Number**, worked out from your birth date and often treated as your lucky number. This guide shows how to calculate it and what each number traditionally means.

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

**Method**: Add every digit of your birth date, then keep adding the digits of the result until you reach a single digit (1 to 9).

**Example**: Born July 15, 1990

1+9+9+0 + 7 + 1+5 = 32 → 3+2 = **5**

→ Life Path Number: **5**

<Callout type="info">
**Master numbers**: If the total is 11, 22, or 33, you stop there instead of reducing further. These three are called master numbers, and tradition reads them as carrying both greater potential and greater responsibility.
</Callout>

If you would rather not add it up by hand, enter your birth date in the [numerology calculator](/en/numerology/calculator/).

## What Each Life Path Number Means

The **days that reduce to the same number** below are the days of the month whose digits add up to that number. Numerology treats these dates and numbers as lucky for that person.

### 1 — The Leader, the Pioneer

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 1, 10, 19, 28
- **Core traits**: Independence, creativity, leadership
- **Life task**: Building confidence and opening your own path

### 2 — The Cooperator, the Diplomat

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 2, 11, 20, 29
- **Core traits**: Empathy, harmony, cooperation
- **Life task**: Working in harmony with others while honoring your own needs

### 3 — The Communicator, the Creator

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 3, 12, 21, 30
- **Core traits**: Creativity, expressiveness, sociability
- **Life task**: Bringing joy to the people around you through creative self-expression

### 4 — The Builder, the Doer

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 4, 13, 22, 31
- **Core traits**: Stability, practicality, diligence
- **Life task**: Laying a solid foundation through patience and effort

### 5 — The Free Spirit, the Explorer

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 5, 14, 23
- **Core traits**: Freedom, adventure, change
- **Life task**: Growing through varied experience without fearing change

### 6 — The Nurturer, the Protector

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 6, 15, 24
- **Core traits**: Responsibility, love, service
- **Life task**: Finding meaning in caring for family and community

### 7 — The Thinker, the Seeker

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 7, 16, 25
- **Core traits**: Intuition, wisdom, inner exploration
- **Life task**: Looking deeply inward and finding your own answers

### 8 — The Achiever, the Organizer

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 8, 17, 26
- **Core traits**: Drive, material success, leadership
- **Life task**: Balancing material success with inner growth

### 9 — The Completer, the Humanitarian

- **Days that reduce to the same number**: 9, 18, 27
- **Core traits**: Wisdom, compassion, completion
- **Life task**: Working for more people, beyond your personal wants

## Master Numbers

### 11 — The Number of Intuition

Read as a number of strong intuition and inspiration, along with greater sensitivity and pressure.

### 22 — The Number of the Builder

Read as the number that turns large visions into reality. It stands for both the strength to take on big work and its weight.

### 33 — The Number of Care

Read as the number of teaching and caring for others: the responsibility of 6 on a wider scale.

## Using a Lucky Number Lightly

- Use it as a small nudge when you pick the day to start a new habit or goal.
- Use it for choices where any option is fine, such as a seat or a locker number.
- Use the life task of your number as a question for reviewing your goals for the year.

<Callout type="warning">
**Keep in mind**: For decisions with real consequences, such as contracts, investments, and health, go by the terms and the facts, not by a number. A lucky number is a tool for fun and self-reflection.
</Callout>
