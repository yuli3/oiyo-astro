## What is the egogram?

The **egogram** is a counseling sketch. In 1972 John Dusay drew the ego states of Eric Berne's transactional analysis as five bars. It is not a diagnosis, and it does not name an illness.

Berne's structure has three states: Parent, Adult, and Child. Transactional analysis then talks about the Parent as critical or nurturing, and the Child as free or adapted. Dusay's egogram is a picture of those five.

- **CP (Critical Parent)**: rules, standards, responsibility
- **NP (Nurturing Parent)**: care, warmth, support
- **A (Adult)**: facts, choices, a reading of what is in front of you
- **FC (Free Child)**: play, spontaneity, feeling
- **AC (Adapted Child)**: fitting in, watching the room, holding back

The bars are a sketch of where energy sits right now. They can shift with the situation and the season of a life. There is no single normal shape.

## 2. Names for the shapes

These four sketches are teaching names for the picture. They are not official diagnostic types from Dusay, and they are not measured population shares.

### Sketch 1. N-shape, both ends loud

```
CP ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  Loud
NP ▓▓▓▓▓     Middle
A  ▓▓▓▓      Middle
FC ▓▓▓▓▓     Middle
AC ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  Loud
```

The voice that sets rules and the voice that watches for approval are both loud. It can look strict from the outside and uneasy about judgment on the inside.

### Sketch 2. U-shape, the middle quiet

```
CP ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  Loud

A  ▓▓▓       Quiet
FC ▓▓▓▓      Quiet

```

Criticism and compliance are loud. Care, thinking, and play are quiet. This picture does not judge how someone was raised. This guide has no source for that kind of rate.

### Sketch 3. M-shape, care and play loud

```
CP ▓▓▓▓      Quiet
NP ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  Loud
A  ▓▓▓▓      Quiet
FC ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  Loud
AC ▓▓▓▓      Quiet
```

Warmth and spontaneity are loud. Rules and fact-sorting are drawn smaller. That is an observation about the picture, not the name of a disorder.

### Sketch 4. Reverse N, the middle loud

```
CP ▓▓▓▓▓     Middle
NP ▓▓▓▓▓▓    Loud
A  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓   Loud
FC ▓▓▓▓▓▓    Loud
AC ▓▓▓        Quiet
```

Care, judgment, and spontaneity sit above compliance. Counselors often like this picture as a talking example. It is not a passing grade for health, and nothing here shows that this shape is the normal one.

## 4. Wake the quiet bar

Dusay proposed a **constancy hypothesis**: treat the amount of energy as roughly fixed, so raising one state lowers the others in the picture (Dusay, 1972). That is a working idea for counseling, not a law of physics. The practical suggestion is to energize a quiet state, not only to press down a loud one.

<Callout type="tip" title="When CP is loud">
**When CP is loud.** Do not only try to shrink the criticism. Turn the nurturing voice on first.

- Before you correct someone, ask what might have led them there
- Say, to yourself as well, that unfinished is allowed
- Trade "you should" for "it would help if"
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="When AC is loud">
**When AC is loud.** Do not only scold the people-pleasing. Make a small choice, or a small refusal. That is practice for Free Child and Adult.

- Say the feeling or the opinion out loud
- Decide something small yourself, such as the meal or the time
- Practice a refusal: "That is hard for me right now"
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="When A is quiet">
**When A is quiet.** This is not an order to delete feeling. It is a way to put feeling, fact, and interpretation on separate lines.

- Pause before you answer
- Write the situation as facts and choices
- Keep three lines: feeling, fact, interpretation
</Callout>

## 5. The egogram and jobs

The egogram is not a career test. This guide does not say that a shape is common among managers, and it does not give a percentage for any occupation. There is no source here for those distributions.

What you can carry into work is the same observation as in any other conversation. A loud CP makes the standard easy to see, and the tone can still sound like blame. A loud AC agrees quickly, and the opinion can stay inside. The model does not sort people into jobs. It asks which voice is running the talk.
