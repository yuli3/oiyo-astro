## Introduction: The Adventure Called Life

Mythologist Joseph Campbell described a common pattern after studying myths around the world. The **Monomyth**, or 'Hero's Journey'.
Departure → Initiation → Return.
That three-part shape is his claim about myths, not a law of nature. The major arcana from 0 to 21 do not match it card for card, and the likeness does not prove that tarot is a map of the soul. Hajo Banzhaf wrote _Tarot and the Journey of the Hero_, and Rachel Pollack is a tarot commentator. This essay is a modern interpretive overlay in that vein, not a one-to-one proof. It leaves out the Empress (3), the Lovers (6), Strength (8), Justice (11), and Temperance (14).

## Act 1: The Departure - Out into the World

### 0. The Fool

Birth of the Hero. Pure state of knowing nothing and thus fearing nothing. Standing on the edge of a cliff but looking at the sky.

### 1. Magician & 2. High Priestess

Checking resources. Magician provides conscious ability (Tool), High Priestess provides unconscious ability (Intuition). Archetypes of Father (Yang) and Mother (Yin).

### 4. Emperor & 5. Hierophant

Socialization process. Learning worldly rules (Emperor) and spiritual traditions (Hierophant). Hero feels safe here but simultaneously stifled.

### 7. Chariot

First victory. Establishing Ego and rushing out into the world. Full of spirit saying "I can do it." But still immature.

## Act 2: The Initiation - Series of Trials

### 9. Hermit

Diving inward. Hero enters the cave not to find the world but to find 'Self'.

### 10. Wheel of Fortune

Experiencing force majeure of destiny. Facing a colossal force not under my will.

### 12. Hanged Man

Voluntary sacrifice and shift of perspective. Hero hangs upside down seeing the world differently. Shift from Doing to Being.

### 13. Death

The greatest trial. The old self must die completely. Pain of breaking the shell.

### 15. Devil & 16. Tower

Encounter with Shadow. Facing hidden desires (Devil), and accumulated arrogance (Tower) is struck by lightning and collapses. Thorough destruction.

## Act 3: The Return - Integration and Wholeness

### 17. Star

Hope after destruction. Pouring water while naked. Pure healing.

### 18. Moon & 19. Sun

Final gateway. Passing through unconscious terror (Moon) and recovering clear light of consciousness (Sun).

### 20. Judgement

Resurrection. The dead rise at the sound of the trumpet. Realizing Calling and being reborn.

### 21. World

Wholeness. Hero returns to where he started, but he has changed. The journey of Fool 0 has ended, and a higher dimension journey of new 0 begins.

## Practical Application: Where Am I Now?

Some people read the pain they are in as something other than meaningless hardship. That is one way of reading a card, not a fact about anyone's suffering.

- **If the Tower appears:** A question people sometimes ask themselves is what would have to fall for them to feel a little freer. The card does not place you at stage 16, and it does not promise that the Star comes next in your life.
- **If Hermit phase:** Don't be lonely. Now is the time to turn your back on the world and light the inner lamp.

## Wrapping Up: You Are That Hero

Tarot reading is not a game where a fortune teller guesses your fate. Some people set the cards beside the hero's-journey story and look for a question to ask next. It is not confirmation of which stage they are in.
Draw a card. And write your own myth.
