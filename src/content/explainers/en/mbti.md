The **Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)** is a preference model and assessment approach developed from Jung’s psychological types by Katharine Cook Briggs and Isabel Briggs Myers. Four letters can provide vocabulary for a conversation, but do not establish ability, mental health, occupational suitability or a relationship’s future.

Distinguish the official model, research on a particular instrument version, and OIYO’s own activity. Popularity or a description that feels familiar is not evidence of accuracy or superiority over other tools.

## 1. What are the four preference pairs?

The [Foundation’s official preference explanation](https://www.myersbriggs.org/my-mbti-personality-type/the-mbti-preferences/) says people can use both sides. This table summarizes model terms, not rules confining people to one behavior.

| Preference pair | Focus distinguished within the model | Do not conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extraversion E / Introversion I | Attention toward outer people and activity or inner ideas and reflection | E has better social skills and I is shy |
| Sensing S / Intuition N | Concrete information and experience or patterns and possibilities considered first | S lacks imagination and N knows the future |
| Thinking T / Feeling F | Emphasis on logical consistency or values and consequences for people in decisions | T has no emotions and F cannot reason |
| Judging J / Perceiving P | Reaching closure or keeping options open in outer life | J is diligent and P irresponsible |

Feeling here refers to values-based judgment, not simply emotional intensity. Introversion is not a diagnosis of loneliness or social anxiety, and Intuition is not a supernatural power.

## 2. Sixteen combinations and cognitive functions

Taking one letter from each pair gives 2×2×2×2=16 combinations: ISTJ, ISFJ, INFJ, INTJ, ISTP, ISFP, INFP, INTP, ESTP, ESFP, ENFP, ENTP, ESTJ, ESFJ, ENFJ and ENTJ. INFP means reading I, N, F and P together within this model, not that every characteristic of a person is settled.

The [official account of type dynamics](https://www.myersbriggs.org/unique-features-of-myers-briggs/type-dynamics-overview/) combines Sensing, Intuition, Thinking and Feeling with inward and outward orientations, and explains an order including dominant and auxiliary processes. This is an interpretive system within the model. Four letters or OIYO answers do not measure brain circuitry, actual ability in eight functions or an individual’s developmental stage. We do not present “hero,” “parent” or “child” nicknames as if they were all official measurement categories.

Letters such as NT or NF can be grouped, but “Analysts,” “Diplomats,” “Sentinels” and “Explorers” are not treated here as universal official categories. This guide does not prescribe occupations or relationships through such nicknames.

## 3. Separate model assumptions from research findings

Official explanations propose preferences as natural, inborn dispositions. Do not turn that theory into measured evidence that one individual is fixed for life or that opposite behavior will always require more energy. Habits, skills and situational behavior are more complex than four letters. Nor do we use “persona” as another name for an opposite function.

The [institutional abstract of McCrae and Costa’s 1989 original study](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1458465) reports that data from 468 people did not support genuinely dichotomous preferences or qualitatively distinct types, and proposes interpreting the four indices dimensionally. Only the institutional abstract was checked for this revision. One older study of a particular instrument cannot settle the validity of every current version, language and use. Conversely, an official model description does not establish that cognitive functions are rigorously validated or that this is the “most useful” tool.

## 4. What does the current OIYO activity calculate?

The activity currently uses 16 OIYO-authored two-option items, four per preference pair. For each axis it divides the number choosing the first pole—E, S, T or J—by four and multiplies by 100. Counts of 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4 yield 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100%. Values at least 50 select the first letter; lower values select the opposite. A 2–2 tie therefore also selects the first letter. This is a code rule, not a psychologically resolved tie.

50% is not a population percentile, diagnostic probability or half your personality. Changing one answer can change a letter near the boundary. Function orders and deeper descriptions shown with the result are interpretations, not separate functions directly measured by those answers.

OIYO’s activity is not official Form M or Form Q. Item equivalence, independent validity and norms, translation equivalence and any required rights have not been verified or certified by this explanation. A code label saying the items are original does not replace official certification or legal permission.

## 5. Turn differences into a conversation

Imagine one person wants a meeting to reach a decision while another wants more information. Rather than saying J/P makes conflict inevitable, check the deadline, missing information and conditions for revising the decision. This is an editorial example, not a validated mediation technique.

| Common assertion | A question to ask instead |
| --- | --- |
| T only wants solutions; F only wants empathy | Would you like me to listen now, or work on solutions together? |
| Different function users inevitably clash | Which actual needs and constraints conflicted? |
| Your type determines your career | Which tasks do you enjoy, and what skills, qualifications and conditions matter? |
| A changed result means your whole personality changed | Were the situation, items, language and boundary scores the same? |

The [official code of ethics](https://www.myersbriggs.org/using-type-as-a-professional/mbti-code-of-ethics/) also warns against absolute labeling, hiring screening, or prescribing careers and relationships solely by type. Do not use OIYO results for diagnosis, hiring or judgments of trustworthiness. Sharing is optional; nobody needs to be pressured to disclose a type.
