Human Design is usually introduced as **"an integration of four ancient wisdoms."** The ingredients are indeed old. But **the system itself was built in 1987.**

## 1. When, and by whom

Alan Krakower, a Canadian, devised it after an experience he said he had on Ibiza in 1987, and set the system out under the name **Ra Uru Hu**.

Four ingredients: the 64 hexagrams of the **I Ching**, **Western astrology**, the Tree of Life from **Kabbalah**, and the **chakra** system. A modern element is added on top: the neutrino.

Being clear about the date matters. "Ancient wisdom" fits the ingredients but **does not fit the system.** The rules of combination, the five types and the concept of authority were all made after 1987.

## 2. What the system divides

| Type | Share of population (per the system) | Operating principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generator | Largest | Respond, then move |
| Manifesting Generator | Large | Respond, inform, move |
| Projector | Middling | Wait for the invitation |
| Manifestor | Small | Move first, then inform |
| Reflector | Very small | Observe over a lunar cycle |

To this are added **Authority** — what you leave a decision to — and **Centers** — which of nine are defined in your chart.

## 3. The neutrino claim does not hold

When the system reaches for what distinguishes it from other divinatory methods, it offers this: **neutrinos from the stars pass through the body at the moment of birth and imprint information.**

Neutrinos are real particles, and they do stream through us constantly. The trouble is what comes next.

- Neutrinos **barely interact with matter.** Most pass straight through the entire Earth. That is why detecting them takes instruments on the scale of thousands of tonnes.
- Barely interacting means **there is no route by which they could leave information.** No mechanism for "imprinting" anything on a body has ever been offered.
- Nor is there any observation supporting the idea that neutrino properties vary by birth time and place in a way that produces individual differences.

This part **borrows the vocabulary of physics without making a claim in physics.** Whatever you do with the system, this explanation should not be accepted as scientific grounding.

## 4. Why people use it anyway

Lacking evidence is not the same as lacking use. What Human Design mostly leaves people with takes the form of **behavioural guidance**.

- "Wait for the invitation" (Projector) reads as **check the situation before pushing forward.**
- "Respond, then move" (Generator) reads as **do not force a start on something you feel no pull toward.**
- The idea of Authority makes you ask **what you have been leaving decisions to.**

Guidance like that can work on a person regardless of whether the system is true. But that is **not evidence the system is right — it also means the advice is general.**

## 5. What to keep in mind

- The system **has not been validated.** There is no independent research showing that its types predict actual behaviour.
- **The population figures are claims internal to the system**, not statistics confirmed from outside.
- Do not leave decisions about career, health or relationships to a type. Readings like "I am a Projector so I should not work" are actively harmful.
- Use it for enjoyment and self-description, knowing it is **a symbolic system made in 1987**.
